1. Open [Competitions](/wettbewerbe) and click **New competition**.
2. Enter name and date and click **Create**.
3. Open **Set up** and select a target with its preview image and a shot count under **Shooting conditions**. Click **Apply shooting conditions**. Known discipline details are derived from the target.
4. Configure the individual and unit rankings under **Rankings**. Multiple rankings can use the same recorded hits; separately fired cards belong to separate rounds. Save changed rules with **Apply scoring rules**.
5. Choose certificates and result visibility under **Output & sharing**.
6. Record results, including on a second device via QR code. Stand mode lets you select the ranking or shooting round.
7. Open **Evaluation**, choose **Mark as provisional** and decide ties in a shoot-off.
8. Under **Competition and finalisation**, click **Close as final** and print the certificates.

Available shooting facilities can be configured under [Competitions › Setup](/wettbewerbe/einrichtung), including a high stand for the bird shoot. Target selection then shows matching targets first; the full catalogue remains available for other venues. Bird shooting uses bird parts instead of shooting rounds; external competitions record your own places.
