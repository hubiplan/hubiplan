1. Open [Music › Musicians](/musik/musiker).
2. Click **New musician**.
3. Search for the member with at least two letters of the name, or record a guest with contact details.
4. Under **Instrumental skills**, choose the instrument from the list and enter the section or part.
5. Mark the main instrument and save.

The member record shows a short profile with the instruments on the **Music** tab.
