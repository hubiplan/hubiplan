1. Open [Settings › Outbox](/einstellungen/postausgang) and look for the email. Its status shows whether it was sent or whether the address could not be reached.
2. If the address could not be reached, correct the email address in the person's file and send again.
3. If the email was sent, ask the person to look in the spam folder and save the sender as a contact.
4. If many recipients are affected, contact platform support. With **Platform support may see text and attachments** you also release the contents to them for troubleshooting.

Text and attachments of delivered emails are cleared after 30 days; undelivered ones stay complete for up to 90 days.
