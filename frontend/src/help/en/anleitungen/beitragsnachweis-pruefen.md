1. Under [Finances › Fee rates](/finanzen/einrichtung/beitraege), in **Reduction with proof**, click **Add reduction**: name, reduced annual fee or percentage off, deadline (e.g. 28.02.) and optionally a grace period.
2. In the member record, under **Reduction with proof**, assign the reduction with its start date.
3. Look at incoming proofs under [Members › Fee proofs](/mitglieder/beitragsnachweise) and **Accept**, ask a **Query** or **Decline**.
4. When the conditions no longer apply, use **Enter end** in the member record (e.g. 31/12).

A proof on paper is entered in the member record with **Enter proof** without a file, as seen.
