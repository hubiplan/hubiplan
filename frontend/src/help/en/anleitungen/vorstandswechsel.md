1. Fill every newly elected office under [Members › Offices](/mitglieder/aemter) with **Assign** and let the predecessor's term end, see [Fill an office after an election](/hilfe/amt-besetzen).
2. Check under [Settings › Accounts](/einstellungen/konten) whether the new people have accepted their invitation and set up two-factor sign-in.
3. Look there under **Terms of office** whether former office holders still have roles they no longer need, and end them.
4. Check the legal notice, letterhead and signatures. After assigning, HUBIplan points out when the role appears there.
5. Outside HUBIplan: register the change with the register of associations, change the bank authorities and hand over keys and documents.

Anyone who no longer holds an office loses the rights of the role when the term ends. The account remains for the member area.
