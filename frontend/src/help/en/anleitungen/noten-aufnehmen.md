1. Open [Sheet music](/noten); if the music module is on, you find the library under Music at **Sheet music library**.
2. Create the piece with title and composer.
3. Below it, add the edition or arrangement with arranger, publisher, duration and instrumentation.
4. Under **Part inventory**, enter copies, storage place and format for each instrument and part.
5. Upload digital scores under [Documents](/dokumente) and link them to the arrangement under **Sheet music documents**.
6. Tick **In the repertoire on the website** if the piece should appear on the website.

You take over an existing catalogue from a CSV file with **Import sheet music catalogue**. A file in the archive grants no copying or performance rights.
