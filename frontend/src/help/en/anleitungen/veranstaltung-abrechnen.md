1. Open the [events](/finanzen/veranstaltungen) in Finance.
2. Click **New occasion** and enter the name and start.
3. If you like, enter **Planned income (optional)** and **Planned expenses (optional)**.
4. When booking in the cash book, select the event.
5. Assign older bookings with **Assign existing entries**.
6. Open the event with **View** and check income, expenses and result.
7. Fetch the evaluation as a PDF or with **Excel**.

With a **Series**, for example "Summer party", you compare the years with each other.
