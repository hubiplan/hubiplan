1. Open [Tasks](/aufgaben) and choose **New task**.
2. Enter a title and, if you like, a description and a deadline.
3. Decide which people or roles may read or edit the task; without sharing it stays private.
4. Click **Create**.
5. Add attachments, reminders or a repetition if needed.
6. Close the dialogue with **Done**.
7. Tick the task in the list as soon as it is done.

You set email and push notifications under [Notifications](/mein-konto/benachrichtigungen) in your account.
