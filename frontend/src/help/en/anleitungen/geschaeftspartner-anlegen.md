1. Open the [business partners](/geschaeftspartner).
2. Click **Create business partner**.
3. Choose the type and enter the name; address, contact and tax details are optional.
4. Under **Roles**, tick what the partner is for you, for example supplier or sponsor.
5. Save the partner.
6. Use **Add contact** to add the people you deal with.

Names that appear several times in the cash book are suggested as business partners at the bottom of the page.
