1. Under **Types and notice periods**, switch on **Online voting on the phone allowed** for the type, and for hybrid meetings also **Remote participation allowed (hybrid meeting)**.
2. Open the meeting under [Meetings](/versammlungen) and choose **Open check-in via app**.
3. Show the code on the projector with **Show code full screen**; members scan it and tap **I'm here**.
4. For members taking part remotely, tick **Allow remote participation for this meeting**.
5. Under **New vote**, choose the agenda item, the question and whether the ballot is secret, and click **Create vote**.
6. When the item comes up, click **Start vote**.
7. When everyone has voted, click **End vote**; only then does the result appear.
8. **Transfer to the minutes** records the result as a resolution or election under the agenda item.

The code changes every 30 seconds. Members present and entitled to vote can vote; anyone holding a proxy casts a separate vote for it. In a secret ballot no person is recorded on the ballot; separately, HUBIplan only notes who has already voted. Voting and remote participation need a basis in the constitution (section 32 (2) German Civil Code).
