1. Open [Equipment](/material).
2. Click **Add equipment**.
3. Enter a name and a short description.
4. Choose the location: **With a member** or a full address.
5. Click **Save**.
6. On the detail page, upload a picture under **Equipment photo**.

Free-text addresses are visible to everyone who may read the equipment of this unit.
