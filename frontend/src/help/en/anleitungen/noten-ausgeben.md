1. Open [Music › Rehearsals](/musik/proben) and then the **Sheet music checkout**.
2. Choose the musician, arrangement and part.
3. Enter the number and the return date and save.
4. Record the return as soon as the music is back; a partial return is possible too.

Each hand-out reduces the free stock. Overdue returns are marked; members see their outstanding music in the member area.
