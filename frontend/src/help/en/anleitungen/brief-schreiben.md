1. Open [Circulars › Letter](/rundschreiben/brief).
2. Under **Your role**, choose the office in which you are writing.
3. If you like, upload your signature as an image.
4. Check the sheet with **Show preview**.
5. Click **Create letterhead** and then **Download**.
6. Open the file in Word or LibreOffice and write the letter.

For letters to many recipients, use a circular sent as a letter.
