1. Open the member's file in the [member list](/mitglieder).
2. Change the address on the **Personal data** tab.
3. If the bank has changed too, record a new mandate, see [Record a SEPA mandate](/hilfe/mandat-erfassen).
4. If the person belongs to a household, check whether they stay in it, see [Create a household](/hilfe/haushalt-anlegen).

Members can also change their address themselves in the member area or the app. You then only check the report, see [Check a change request](/hilfe/aenderungsmeldung-pruefen).
