1. Open [Events](/termine) and click **New event** (or a day in the calendar).
2. Enter title, date, time and place; for regular meetings choose a repetition. The dialog asks in this order: title, when, where, category and who sees the event. The header and the bar with **Create event** stay in place while you scroll.
3. Choose who is invited and whether acceptances and declines are collected.
4. Click **Create event**.

If the end time is not yet known, enable **Hide end time on the website** under **More settings**.
The website then shows only the start time; the entered end time is kept in the internal calendar.
For a recurring event, the setting applies to all occurrences.

Invitees receive an email and accept or decline there or in the member area.
