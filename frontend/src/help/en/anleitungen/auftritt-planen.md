1. First create the performance as a single event under [Events](/termine).
2. Under [Music](/musik), choose this event at **Performances** and add meeting point, uniform, format and notes.
3. Open **Plan instrumentation**, choose an active template and click **Apply template**.
4. Select suitable musicians under **Batch assignment**.
5. Click **Request selected musicians**; the musicians accept or decline in the member area.
6. Put the programme together under **Musical programme**.
7. Print **Programme list as PDF** and **Parts list as PDF** for the performance.

If someone declines, **Find replacement** under line-up planning suggests musicians of the same part without a known conflict.
