1. Clarify the rights first: you need permission from the person who took the photo and the consent of recognisable people.
2. Open the [website pictures](/webseite/bilder).
3. Upload a file or paste a copied picture with Ctrl+V.
4. Mind the format: JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 15 MB; save HEIC pictures as JPEG first.
5. Crop the picture.
6. Write a short alt text that says what the picture shows.
