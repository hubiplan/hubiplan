1. Create a new event or meeting in your email program or calendar, with title, time and place.
2. Invite only the mailing list's address as attendee, for example `vorstand@…`, and send the invitation.
3. After about a minute, open the link in HUBIplan's mail “Please confirm”.
4. Check time, place and video link and, if needed, choose **Offer yes/no replies** or **Create a new video room (Nextcloud Talk)**.
5. Click **Take over and inform**.

The list's recipients get the invitation from HUBIplan; do not invite them yourself as well, or they get it twice. The Nextcloud calendar creates the Talk link with “Add Talk conversation” when you create the event; otherwise HUBIplan creates a new room if the list is set up that way. If you later move or cancel the event in your email program, a confirmation link comes again. The available lists are under [Circulars](/rundschreiben) on the “Mailing list” tab.

In the app: You can also share an invitation as a calendar file (.ics) to the HUBIplan app or open it with the app. After you unlock management, **New event** opens, filled in with title, time, location and video link. Check the details and tap **Create**.
