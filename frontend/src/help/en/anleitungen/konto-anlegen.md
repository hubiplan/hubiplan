You do not create an account on its own. It is created with the invitation as soon as the person gets a role or may use the member area.

1. If the person is to manage something, fill their role under [Members › Offices](/mitglieder/aemter) with **Assign**, see [Create a role and invite a person](/hilfe/rolle-anlegen).
2. If a member is only to use the member area, set up the access in their file on the **Roles and account** tab.
3. The person receives an email with a link that is valid for 7 days and sets their password themselves.
4. If nothing arrives, open [Settings › Accounts](/einstellungen/konten), choose the filter **invitation expired** under **Show**, tick the account and send the link again.

Anyone with a role must set up two-factor sign-in, see [Two-factor sign-in](/hilfe/zwei-faktor).
