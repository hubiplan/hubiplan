1. Open the [offices](/mitglieder/aemter).
2. At the top, choose the club or a sub-unit under **Unit**.
3. Click **Assign** next to the office.
4. Choose the person from the member list and the date of the election.
5. Read the note on whether the role carries rights and to which address an invitation goes.
6. Leave the tick that ends the predecessor's term on the day before, and confirm.

If the role appears in the legal notice or on the letterhead, HUBIplan points this out afterwards. For registered clubs, a change of the board must be entered in the register of associations.
