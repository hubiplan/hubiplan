1. Open [Volunteer duties](/helferdienste) and choose **New duty roster**.
2. Enter a title and link the plan to an event if needed.
3. Create each duty with **Add duty**, such as bar or set-up, with place and minimum age if needed.
4. Below it, enter the shifts with day, times and people per shift; several work at once, such as “14-18, 18-22”.
5. Under **Roster settings**, decide whether members sign up themselves and until when they can cancel.
6. Assign people to a shift with **Assign** if needed.
7. Choose **Publish**; only then do members see the plan.
8. If places are still free, **Call-out as circular** creates a call to all members.

**Notice as PDF** prints one page per duty with empty lines to fill in. For next year, **Copy roster** creates a new plan without assignments.
