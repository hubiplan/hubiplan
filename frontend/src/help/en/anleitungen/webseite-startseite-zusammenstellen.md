1. Open the [home page of the website](/webseite/startseite).
2. Click **Add section** and choose a type, for example **Banner**, **Tiles** or **Latest posts**.
3. Open the section with **Edit** and fill in the content, **Background** and **Section width**.
4. Click **Apply**.
5. Drag the sections by the handle into the order you want.
6. Check the **Preview** and click **Publish**.

With **How long to show**, a section disappears by itself, for example the notice about a festival.
