1. Open the [year-end closing](/finanzen/abschluss) and there the tab **Cash audit**.
2. Choose **Create audit assignment** with financial year, names of the auditors, deadline and number of samples.
3. Give the auditors a role under [Roles](/einstellungen/rollen) that can read but not keep the books, ideally with an end date.
4. The auditors compare each sample with its receipt and enter the balance per account from the bank statement.
5. **Draw 5 more** enlarges the sample.
6. The auditors finish with **Complete audit**.
7. Download the **Audit report (PDF)** for the meeting.

Only the auditors may tick items off, not the treasurer.
