1. Open [Finances › Expenses](/finanzen/auslagen) and choose a claim under **To review**.
2. Look at the receipt and notes and choose the category for each item.
3. Click **Approve**, or **Send a query** if something is missing.
4. Under **Approved** choose account or cash box and date and click **Paid out: book**.

Several transfers at once: create a SEPA file for online banking under **Batch transfer**.
