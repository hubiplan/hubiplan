1. Open the [certificate templates](/wettbewerbe/urkunden).
2. Click **New template** and enter the heading and the text with the placeholders.
3. Choose the background image and **Signatures** and switch on **Show coat of arms**.
4. Check the template with **Sample certificate (PDF)**.
5. Open the [competition](/wettbewerbe) and then its evaluation.
6. Choose the template and ranking and click **Create certificates (PDF)**.

The PDF contains one certificate for each placed participant down to the chosen place.
