1. Under Taxes, open the [VAT](/finanzen/steuern/umsatzsteuer) tab.
2. Under **Settings**, tick **The club is liable for VAT (not a small business)** once.
3. Choose the **Advance return period** there.
4. Choose the month or quarter you are reporting.
5. Copy the box numbers 81, 86, 48, 66 and 83 into ELSTER.

HUBIplan transmits nothing to ELSTER. Only bookings with a tax rate are counted; the form does not replace tax advice.
