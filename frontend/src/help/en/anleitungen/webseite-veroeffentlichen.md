1. Change the design, fine-tuning, areas or home page; a note about unpublished changes then appears at the top.
2. Check the draft in the **Preview**.
3. Click **Publish** to make the draft public, or **Discard** to take back all changes.
4. Open the [versions](/webseite/fassungen) if you need an earlier state.
5. Under **Earlier versions of the design**, click **Restore** next to the version you want.
6. Check the draft and click **Publish** again.

HUBIplan keeps the last five published designs. Texts have their own versions under **Content**.
