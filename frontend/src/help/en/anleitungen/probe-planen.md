1. Create the rehearsal as an event or recurring event in the [calendar](/termine).
2. Open [Music › Rehearsals](/musik/proben) and link the event as a rehearsal.
3. Under **Upcoming rehearsals**, click **Edit rehearsal programme** next to the event.
4. Enter the focus and add the pieces with **Add rehearsal piece**.
5. Choose **This occurrence only** or **Entire series** and save.
6. Assign the musicians under **Rehearsal assignments**.

Assigned members see the rehearsal, programme and released sheet music in their member area and accept or decline there.
