1. Finish booking the old year: import all bank statements and count the cash boxes, see [Count the cash](/hilfe/kasse-zaehlen).
2. Issue the donation receipts for all donations of the year, see [Issue donation receipts](/hilfe/zuwendungsbestaetigungen).
3. Close the year and prepare the cash audit, see [Close the financial year](/hilfe/jahresabschluss) and [Prepare the cash audit](/hilfe/kassenpruefung-vorbereiten).
4. Check the fee rates for the new year. Then the [fee run](/hilfe/beitragslauf) and the [direct debits](/hilfe/lastschrift) follow.
5. Report the member numbers as of the cut-off date to the umbrella association.
6. Invite to the annual general meeting, see [Invite to the meeting](/hilfe/versammlung-einladen).

With a financial year that differs from the calendar year, the same order applies at the end of the financial year. Donation receipts still follow the calendar year.
