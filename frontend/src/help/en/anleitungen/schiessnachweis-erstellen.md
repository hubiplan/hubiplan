1. Open [Training](/training) and choose **Shooting log**.
2. Under **Reported shooting dates**, confirm what the member reported themselves.
3. Under **A member's shooting log**, choose the member.
4. Check the status under section 14 WaffG: acquisition (last twelve months) and possession (last
   24 months, for handguns and long guns).
5. If a date is missing, add it under **Add shooting date**.
6. If needed, choose the weapon and a reference date and click **Create PDF**.
7. Sign the confirmation as the board member authorised to represent the club, stamp it and attach
   it to the association's application.

For shooting dates from training to reach the shooting log, tick in the training session that it
is a shooting date and enter discipline and weapon.
