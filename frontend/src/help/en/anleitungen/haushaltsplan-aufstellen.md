1. Open the [budget](/finanzen/haushaltsplan) and choose the financial year at the top.
2. Take over last year's plan or actual figures, or click **Start empty**.
3. Click **Edit** and adjust the amounts per category.
4. Add missing categories with **Add line**.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Fetch the paper for the meeting with **PDF** or **Excel**.
7. Record the meeting's resolution with **Record as adopted**.

An adopted budget is locked; **Edit as draft again** opens it for a change.
