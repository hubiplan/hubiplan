1. Open [My invoices](/mein-bereich/rechnungen) in your member area.
2. There you find your invoices from the club, such as the annual fee, each with its status.
3. Check whether an invoice is still open and pay it as stated on the invoice.

The treasurer of your club answers questions about an invoice.
