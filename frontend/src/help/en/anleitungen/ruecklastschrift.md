1. Import the bank statement under [Finance › Bank statements](/finanzen/kontoauszuege). HUBIplan recognises the returned direct debit and names the invoice it belongs to.
2. Book the transaction as an expense with **New entry**. Book a bank charge as a separate expense.
3. Open the invoice under [Finance › Invoices](/finanzen/rechnungen) and remove the payment there under **Payments**. The invoice is then open again.
4. Clarify the reason with the member: insufficient funds, new bank details or an objection. With new bank details you need a new mandate, see [Record a SEPA mandate](/hilfe/mandat-erfassen).
5. Collect the amount again with the next direct debit run or ask for a transfer. If no payment arrives, the [reminder](/hilfe/mahnen) follows.

A member can object to a direct debit for eight weeks without giving a reason. Whether the club passes on the bank charge is governed by the fee regulations.
