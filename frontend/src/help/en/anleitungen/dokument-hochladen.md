1. Open [Documents](/dokumente).
2. If needed, open **New folder**, enter its name and visibility and confirm with **Create folder**.
3. Click the tile or list entry of the folder you want.
4. Choose the file, optionally add a title, date and note, then click **Upload**.
5. Click **Documents** in the folder path to return to the overview.

Anyone allowed to see the folder finds the document straight away. Deleted items stay in the bin for 30 days.
