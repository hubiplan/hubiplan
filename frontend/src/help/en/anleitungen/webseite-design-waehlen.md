1. Open [Design and colours](/webseite/gestaltung).
2. Choose a design; the preview on the right shows the website with it straight away.
3. If needed, assign a colour from your palette to each part of the design.
4. Under **Text, surfaces and background**, set the colours for text and background.
5. Check the preview with **Phone** and **Tablet** as well.
6. Click **Publish**.

Until you publish, visitors see the previous state. You maintain the palette under Settings › Appearance.
