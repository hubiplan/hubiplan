1. Open the [meetings](/mein-bereich/versammlungen) in your member area.
2. **Invitations** shows time, place and agenda.
3. If your club allows motions, choose **Submit a motion**.
4. Enter a title and reasons and click **Submit motion**.
5. As long as no decision has been made, you can take the motion back with **Withdraw**.

You find approved minutes under **Minutes**.
