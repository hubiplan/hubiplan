1. Open the [custom fields](/mitglieder/einrichtung/zusatzfelder).
2. Click **New field**.
3. Enter the **Label** and choose the **Type**, for example text, date or selection.
4. For a selection, enter the possible values, one per line.
5. Click **Create**.
6. Enter the values in the members' records, or show the field as a column in the list.

Only add what you really need (data minimisation).
