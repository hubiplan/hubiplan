1. Open [Settings › Roles & users](/einstellungen/rollen) and click **New role**.
2. Give the role a name, such as “treasurer”, and choose **no access**, **read** or **edit** for each area. Grant only as much as necessary. The buttons “read everywhere” and “edit everywhere” help with board roles.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Assign the role to a person with **Assign**, optionally for a limited time. If the person has no account yet, they receive an invitation by email. The link in it is valid for 7 days.
5. If the deadline passes before the person has set a password, click **Resend invitation** in their row. Alternatively, they choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.

Anyone who manages roles, runs exports, books in the cash book, may see all members or reads the change log and outbox must set up two-factor authentication. There are 14 days to do so; permissions for a single department do not need it.
