1. Open [Finance › Set up › Bank accounts and cash](/finanzen/einrichtung/konten).
2. Tick **Use DATEV export for the tax adviser**.
3. For accounts and categories, enter the **DATEV account** your tax adviser tells you.
4. Open the [annual closing](/finanzen/abschluss).
5. Click "DATEV" next to the financial year and pass the file to the tax adviser.

The file is a posting batch in DATEV format. Bookings with a tax rate get the matching tax key.
