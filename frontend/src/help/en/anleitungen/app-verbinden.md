1. Install the HUBIplan app on your phone.
2. In the browser, open [My account › App & other instances](/mein-konto/app) and click **Connect app**.
3. Scan the QR code shown with the app.

The device then appears in the list. If you lose your phone, sign it out there with **Sign out**.

If you have forgotten your password, first enter your club’s valid address in the Android app and tap **Forgot password? Reset in browser**. The app opens that instance’s password recovery page; afterwards, return to the app and sign in with the new password. Your email and password are not included in the link. If no browser is available, the app displays a selectable destination address.

![Starting point: Connect the HUBIplan app to your account](bild:app-verbinden)

At the bottom (on a tablet, on the left) there are at most five destinations: **Events**, **Tasks**, **Messages**, **Card** and – with administration rights – **Administration**. **Card** holds your membership card (also offline), your photo and your details. Open **My account** with the icon at the top right. The Back button first takes you to **Events** from any area; only there does it close the app.

**Administration** starts with the list “What would you like to do?”: **Check card** at the top, then **Open items** with a count and the areas you have rights for (tasks, members, events, treasury, fees, occasions). **‹ Overview** or the Back button takes you back to this list.

In the Android app, changed administration forms and short messages ask before discarding input. System Back, visible Back, tab changes and unit changes use the same decision. Booking text, amount, account, category and an already selected receipt photo are restored during Android state restoration; an unfinished receipt signature adapts to its new field size. Server errors are shown at the affected fields. Failed booking and sub-unit member details can be reloaded directly. In line with the privacy policy, private task drafts remain in memory only.

Android dropdowns expose their expanded or collapsed state in the selected language. The current circular is also marked as selected for accessibility services. Success messages for profile changes and circulars are exposed as polite status messages.
