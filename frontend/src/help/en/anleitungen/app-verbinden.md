On first start the app asks: **How would you like to connect?** Both ways are equally good.

**With a QR code from another screen** (e.g. on a computer):

1. Install the HUBIplan app on your phone.
2. In the browser, open [My account › App & other instances](/mein-konto/app) and click **Connect app**.
3. In the app, tap **Scan the QR code from another screen** and scan the code shown.

**With your phone only:**

1. Install the HUBIplan app and open HUBIplan in your phone’s browser at [My account › App & other instances](/mein-konto/app).
2. Tap **Connect app** and then **Open in the HUBIplan app**. If nothing happens, the app is not installed yet.
3. The app shows your club’s address. If it is correct, tap **Yes, connect**.

**With email and password:** In the app, tap **Sign in with email and password** and enter your club’s address, your email and your password. Password managers (e.g. from Google or Bitwarden) offer to fill in the fields and can save the sign-in.

If you only see a note under “My account › App & other instances” instead of the **Connect app** button, your account has no access to the member area yet: it must be linked to your membership, and your club must have switched on the member area.

The device then appears in the list. If you lose your phone, sign it out there with **Sign out**.

If you have forgotten your password, first enter your club’s valid address in the Android app and tap **Forgot password? Reset in browser**. The app opens that instance’s password recovery page; afterwards, return to the app and sign in with the new password. Your email and password are not included in the link. If no browser is available, the app displays a selectable destination address.

At the bottom (on a tablet, on the left) there are at most five destinations: “Events”, “Tasks”, “Messages”, “Card” and – with administration rights – “Administration”. “Card” holds your membership card (also offline), your photo and your details. Open “My account” with the icon at the top right. The Back button first takes you to “Events” from any area; only there does it close the app.

“Administration” starts with the list “What would you like to do?”: “Check card” at the top, then “To do” with a count (data changes, photos and events waiting for you) and the areas you have rights for (assign tasks, members, events, cash book, invoices, anniversaries and birthdays). The arrow at the top left or the Back button takes you back to this list. If the phone has no screen lock yet, the button “Set up screen lock” opens the system setting directly.

Pull lists such as events, messages, tasks and the administration areas down to reload them; a line above shows when they were loaded. Without a connection the app shows a grey notice “Offline – you are seeing the data from …” and keeps the last loaded list.

In the Android app, changed administration forms and short messages ask before discarding input. System Back, visible Back, tab changes and unit changes use the same decision. Booking text, amount, account, category and an already selected receipt photo are restored during Android state restoration; an unfinished receipt signature adapts to its new field size. Server errors are shown at the affected fields. Failed booking and sub-unit member details can be reloaded directly. In line with the privacy policy, private task drafts remain in memory only.

In the event list your reply is shown with an icon and plain text: **Attending**, **Not attending** or **Not answered yet**. In dark mode the app tones down light club colours in the top bar so they do not dazzle in the evening.

Android dropdowns expose their expanded or collapsed state in the selected language. The current circular is also marked as selected for accessibility services. Success messages for profile changes and circulars are exposed as polite status messages.
