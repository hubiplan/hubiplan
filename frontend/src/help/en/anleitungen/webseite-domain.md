1. Open [Domain](/webseite/domain).
2. Enter the address, for example "www.musterverein.example", and click **Add domain**.
3. At your domain provider, point the domain to the server of your HUBIplan instance; whoever operates the instance gives you the details.
4. Check in the browser whether the website appears at the new address.
5. Click **As main address** next to the address you want.

The operator must also set up the domain on the server, including the certificate; agree the switch with them beforehand.
