1. Open the [volunteer duties](/mein-bereich/helferdienste) in your member area.
2. Pick a shift with free places.
3. Click **Sign me up**.
4. **My duties** lists all your shifts; **Add to calendar** downloads them as a calendar file.
5. If you cannot make it after all, choose **Cancel duty** while the cancellation period is running.

After the cancellation period, you can only cancel through the administration.
