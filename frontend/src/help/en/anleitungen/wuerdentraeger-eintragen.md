1. Open the [title holders](/mitglieder/wuerdentraeger).
2. Use **New title** to set up which titles you have, for example king or prince.
3. Click **New reign** and choose the title and year.
4. Choose the member, or enter a name for earlier years.
5. Use **Add companion** to add, for example, the queen.
6. Upload a photo and tick **Show in the chronicle on the website**.

You take over an old list of kings from Excel with **Import list of kings**.
