1. Open the [invoices](/finanzen/rechnungen) and then the wrong invoice.
2. Click **Cancel and create again** if you want to correct the invoice straight away, otherwise **Cancel invoice**.
3. Enter the reason; it appears on the cancellation invoice.
4. Send the document to the recipient with **Send cancellation invoice by email**.
5. Correct the new draft and issue the invoice.

The issued invoice stays unchanged; the cancellation invoice has its own number. Invoices from a fee run are recalculated in the fee run.
