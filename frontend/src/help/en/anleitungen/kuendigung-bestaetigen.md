1. Open [Members › Cancellations](/mitglieder/anfragen/kuendigungen). Open cancellations also appear on the home page under “To do”.
2. Check the **Leaving date**. Only change it if the statutes require a different date.
3. Write a reply to the member if needed.
4. Click **Confirm leaving** or **Decline** and confirm the prompt.

When you confirm, HUBIplan records the leaving date with the reason “leaving”; the person remains a member until that date. The member receives a confirmation of leaving by email. If you decline, the membership continues and the member receives your reply.
