1. Open [Members › Membership applications](/mitglieder/antraege) and click the application number.
2. Check the details and the possible matches. If the person is already known to you or another club, choose **Admit this person**, or create a transfer request for a transfer.
3. If your rule needs several people to approve, click **Approve** or **Raise objection**. The page shows which approval is still missing.
4. If something is missing, send a question by email with **Ask a question …**. The reply comes to you.
5. Click **Admit …**, check the start date and click **Admit now**. If you like, a welcome email with an invitation to the member area goes out.

**Decline …** ends the application; a rejection email is only sent if you choose it. In the list, several ready applications can be admitted at once.
