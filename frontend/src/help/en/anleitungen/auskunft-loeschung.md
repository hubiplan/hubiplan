1. Check that the request really comes from the person, for example by calling back.
2. Open the file in the [member list](/mitglieder).
3. For access: download the information as a ZIP under **Privacy** and give it to the person in person or encrypted.
4. For erasure: for former members you delete the data under **Privacy**. Name, year of birth and membership periods remain for the club's history. If the person objects to that too, you then remove their name from chronicle and honours.

Answer within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR). As long as someone is a member, the club needs the data for the membership; only voluntary items such as photo and publications are then deleted. Invoices and entries remain until the end of the retention period.
