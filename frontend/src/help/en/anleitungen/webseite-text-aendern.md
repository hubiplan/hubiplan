1. Open the [website content](/webseite/inhalte).
2. Choose the text you want to change.
3. Write your change; HUBIplan saves as you type.
4. Use **Show image captions on the website** to decide whether captions appear under pictures.
5. Check the result on the website.

The last versions of each text are kept.
