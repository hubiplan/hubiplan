1. Open the [reserves](/finanzen/ruecklagen).
2. Click **New reserve**, choose the type and enter the name and purpose.
3. Click **Create**.
4. Click **Allocate** next to the reserve and enter the amount, date and **Reason**, for example the board resolution.
5. At the bottom, check under **Maximum for the free reserve** whether the allocation is permitted.
6. Click **Release** as soon as the money is spent.

Charitable clubs may build reserves only for the reasons in section 62 AO (German Fiscal Code).
