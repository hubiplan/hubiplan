1. Open [Surveys](/umfragen) and choose **New survey**, then the template **Find a date**.
2. Enter a title and the date options, each with day and time.
3. Choose who is asked and when the survey closes.
4. **Start** notifies everyone asked.
5. Once the best date is clear, choose the button “Create event on …”. Everyone who said “yes”
   accepts it with one tap.
