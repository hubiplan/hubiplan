1. Open the [business partners](/geschaeftspartner) and then the partner.
2. Click **Add agreement**.
3. Choose the type, for example **Contract**, and enter the period, the amount or benefit in kind and what you give in return.
4. For a contract, enter the **Notice period in days** and whether it renews automatically.
5. Click **Save agreement**.
6. File the signed contract under **Documents** in the partner record.

With an end date, HUBIplan creates a task that reminds you of the renewal or notice date in good time (Tasks module).
