1. Open the [appearance](/einstellungen/erscheinungsbild).
2. Upload the crest or logo and choose the crop.
3. If you like, upload a light version under **Logo / coat of arms for dark mode**.
4. Enter the club colours; under **Further colours** you add the rest of the palette.
5. For each colour, tick under **Used for** what it is used for, for example **Body text** or **Background**.
6. Check everything with **View colour sheet**.

Colours and logo take effect immediately in HUBIplan, on letters, membership cards and the website.
