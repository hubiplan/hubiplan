At the bottom (on a tablet, on the left) there are five destinations: “Home”, “Events”, “News”, “Me” and a fifth one depending on your role: “Administration” with administration rights, otherwise “Sheet music” or “Tasks”. You choose which under “My account” › “Settings” › “Fifth tab”; everything else is on “Home” under “Quick access”. After the redesign, “Home” shows the card “How to find your way around” once; “Got it” closes it.

“Home” shows your next event with buttons to accept or decline, below it what is waiting for you (for example events without a reply, new messages, open invoices) and the tiles your club shows on the start page. “Events” groups the events by month; the switch “without a reply” shows only what still needs an answer. “News” brings together messages, the notice board and surveys; switch between them at the top.

“Me” shows your membership card at the top (also offline; tap it to show it large), and below it, as in [My area](/mein-bereich), four groups: “Money” (invoices, direct debit mandate, fee proofs, orders, expenses), “Taking part” (such as volunteer duties, training, my results, absence, bookings), “Club” (meetings with agenda, motions and minutes, documents) and “My data” (address, phone, photo, cancellation).

Only items your club has switched on appear. The app opens invoices and minutes only online and does not store them. Long-press the app icon and “Show ID card” opens your card directly. Open “My account” with the icon at the top right; under “Appearance” you choose whether the app is light, dark or follows the device. The Back button first takes you to “Home” from any area; only there does it close the app.

“Administration” starts as a workspace: “Check card” and “New” at the top (receipt, booking, event or task, depending on your rights), then “To do” with a count (data changes, photos and events waiting for you) and the areas you have rights for, in the groups of the main menu in the browser: “Planning”, “Club”, “Club life” and “Administration”. With many areas, “Find a section” narrows the list. The arrow at the top left or the Back button takes you back to this overview.

Forms you have started are protected: Back or switching tabs asks before an entry is lost. You pick date and time with the device's own pickers.

With the matching rights you also find “Membership applications” (review, approve, accept or reject), “Approve bookings” (confirm or reject open requests for the clubhouse, range or equipment) and “Run meeting”: record attendance, scan membership cards at the entrance, open check-in via the app and show the changing QR code on the phone or tablet, create, start and close votes and apply the result. Preparing, inviting and the minutes stay in the browser.

If the phone has no screen lock yet, the button “Set up screen lock” opens the system setting directly.

Pull lists such as events, messages, tasks and the administration areas down to reload them; a line above shows when they were loaded. Without a connection the app shows a grey notice “Offline – you are seeing the data from …” and keeps the last loaded list.

In the Android app, changed administration forms and short messages ask before discarding input. System Back, visible Back, tab changes and unit changes use the same decision. Booking text, amount, account, category and an already selected receipt photo are restored during Android state restoration; an unfinished receipt signature adapts to its new field size. Server errors are shown at the affected fields. Failed booking and sub-unit member details can be reloaded directly. In line with the privacy policy, private task drafts remain in memory only.

In the event list your reply is shown with an icon and plain text: “Attending”, “Not attending” or “Not answered yet”. In dark mode the app tones down light club colours in the top bar so they do not dazzle in the evening.

Android dropdowns expose their expanded or collapsed state in the selected language. The current circular is also marked as selected for accessibility services. Success messages for profile changes and circulars are exposed as polite status messages.
