1. Open the [documents](/dokumente).
2. Enter a name under **New folder**.
3. Under **Visible to**, choose who sees the folder, for example only the board or all members.
4. Click **Create folder**.
5. Open the folder and upload the files with **Upload**.

Members find shared folders in their member area. A lock marks folders for the board only.
