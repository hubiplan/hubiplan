1. Open [Members › Trends › Cut-off date](/mitglieder/entwicklung/stichtag).
2. Enter the day the report asks for under **Cut-off date**, often 1 January.
3. Choose the grouping under **Age groups**, such as that of the sports federation or youth funding.
4. Under **Age**, choose whether completed years on the cut-off date or the year of birth count.
5. Under **Membership types**, untick the types that are not reported.
6. Check the tables; if a date of birth, gender or address is missing, add it in the member records.
7. Download the figures with **PDF**, **Excel** or **CSV** and use them for the report.
