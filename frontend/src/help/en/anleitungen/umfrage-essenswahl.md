1. Open the [surveys](/umfragen).
2. Click **New survey** and choose the template **Meal choice**.
3. Enter the dishes as answers.
4. Link the survey to the event so that only those invited or attending are asked.
5. Check the number of people asked with **How many are asked?**.
6. Click **Start**.
7. After the end, fetch the result with **PDF** or **Excel** as an order list for the kitchen.

Anyone bringing a companion to the event chooses up to two meals. If someone cancels the event, their answer disappears.
