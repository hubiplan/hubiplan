With a passkey you sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. You need neither a
password nor a code. A passkey counts as two-factor authentication. It is stored on your phone
or computer or in your password manager and only works on the right site, so a fake site cannot
capture it.

## Set up a passkey

1. Open [Sign-in & security](/mein-konto/anmeldung) under **My account**.
2. In the **Passkeys** section, enter a name if you like, e.g. “Phone”.
3. Click **Add passkey** and confirm with your password.
4. Your browser or phone asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN. The passkey is then saved.
5. If this is your first second factor, HUBIplan shows **Recovery codes**. Keep them safe in
   case your device is ever missing.

A passkey only works for the address where you set it up. If you use HUBIplan under several
addresses, set up a separate passkey under each address. The list shows which address a passkey
works for.

## Sign in

On the sign-in page, click **Sign in with passkey**. Many browsers also suggest the passkey
directly in the email address field. If you sign in with your password, you can choose
**Confirm with passkey** instead of entering the code.

HUBIplan also asks for the passkey for sensitive actions. With **Enter code** you use a code
from your authenticator app or a recovery code instead.

## Lost your device?

Sign in with your password and a recovery code and remove the old passkey with **Remove**.
Without codes, the board can reset your two-factor authentication. If your passkey is stored
with Google, Apple, Microsoft or in a password manager, it is usually already available on a
new device with the same account.

To connect the HUBIplan app, sign in with your passkey in the browser and scan the QR code under
**My account** › App & other instances.
