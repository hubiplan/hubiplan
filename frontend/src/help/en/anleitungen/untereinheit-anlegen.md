1. Open the [sub-units](/untereinheiten).
2. In the form for a new unit, enter the name under **Name**, and a short name if you like.
3. Under **Belongs to**, choose whether the new unit sits below another sub-unit.
4. Click **Create**.
5. Open the new unit with **Edit** and upload an image under **Coat of arms / logo**.
6. Switch on **Own workspace** if the unit keeps its own cash book, events or website.

If your club has no sub-units, hide the menu item under Settings › Terms.
