1. Open [Expenses](/mein-bereich/auslagen) in your member area or in the app.
2. Choose **Claim an expense** and write what you bought and for whom.
3. Enter date and amount; for several things use **Add item**.
4. Photograph the receipt with **Take photo** or choose a file.
5. Choose bank transfer (with IBAN) or cash and click **Submit**.

The treasurer reviews it and gets in touch if there are questions. You are notified after the payout.
