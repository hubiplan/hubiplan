1. Open the [content of the website](/webseite/inhalte).
2. Click **New text page** and give the page a title.
3. Write the text; HUBIplan saves as you type.
4. Under **Page sections**, use **Add section** to add, for example, **Frequently asked questions** or a **Document to download**.
5. Under [Menu and pages](/webseite/aufbau), decide where the page appears in the menu.
6. Click **Publish**.

If a photo is still missing, write a note for the image slot under **Text with image**; the published page does not show the empty slot.
