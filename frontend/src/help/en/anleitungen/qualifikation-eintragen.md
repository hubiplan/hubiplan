1. Open the member's file, for example via [Members › Qualifications](/mitglieder/qualifikationen).
2. In the **Qualifications** section, choose the type under **Record qualification …**.
3. Enter when the certificate was obtained (for the criminal record certificate: inspected); HUBIplan suggests the expiry date from the usual validity.
4. Click **Enter**.
5. To renew, click **renew** at the certificate, check the details and click **Save renewal**; the old certificate stays under “Earlier certificates”.

Before expiry the member receives an email, and the certificate appears under “To do”.
You define which types exist under Members › Setup › Qualifications.
