1. Open [Fine-tuning](/webseite/feinabstimmung).
2. Start with one of the **Presets**, for example **Clear** or **Festive**.
3. Under **Font**, choose the **Font** and the **Font size**.
4. Under **Header**, set the **Header height** and the **Header image**.
5. Under **Readability**, decide whether the website offers a **Dark mode**.
6. Check the preview and click **Publish**.

**Reset all** restores the defaults of the design.
