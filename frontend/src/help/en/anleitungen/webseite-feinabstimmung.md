1. Open [Fine-tuning](/webseite/feinabstimmung).
2. Under **Quick start**, begin with one of the **Presets**, for example **Calm** or **Festive**.
3. Choose **Font** on the left and then the **Font**.
4. Under **Readability**, set the **Font size** and decide whether the website offers a **Dark mode**.
5. Under **Header and menu**, set the **Height** and the **Background**, for example a **Header image**.
6. If you cannot find a control, click **Show** above the preview and then the spot on the website, or use the search field.
7. Check the preview and click **Publish**.

**Undo** takes back the last step, **Reset all** under Quick start restores the defaults of the design.
