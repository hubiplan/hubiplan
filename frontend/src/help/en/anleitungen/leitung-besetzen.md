1. Open the [sub-units](/untereinheiten) and then the unit you want.
2. Click **Assign head** if the person should get an account with all rights for their area.
3. Choose the member and confirm.
4. Assign further roles under **Who has which role?** with **Assign**; this creates no account and sends no e-mail.
5. If you like, end the term of the predecessor in the same step.

Earlier terms are listed under Members › Offices with the unit filter.
