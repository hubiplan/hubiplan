---

1. Open [My data](/mein-bereich/daten) in your member area.
2. In the “Cancel membership” section, click **Cancel membership**.
3. Choose the date under **Leaving on**. The earliest date according to the statutes is suggested; a later date is possible.
4. Click **Send cancellation** and confirm the prompt.

You receive a confirmation of receipt by email. Until the club confirms the cancellation, you can take it back with **Withdraw**. Afterwards you receive the confirmation of leaving; you remain a member until the leaving date. The section only appears if your club has switched on cancelling in the member area.
