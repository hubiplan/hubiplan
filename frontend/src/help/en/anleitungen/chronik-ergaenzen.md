1. Open [Chronicle](/chronik).
2. Add an event to the **Timeline** with a year or period, a heading and a short text.
3. Add up to six pictures if you like and choose where they should go.
4. Structure long periods with **Chapter** as subheadings.
5. Bring in many entries at once with **Add from Excel**.
6. Check the draft and choose **Publish**.

Until then, changes remain a draft. You can bring earlier versions back into the draft at the bottom of the page.
