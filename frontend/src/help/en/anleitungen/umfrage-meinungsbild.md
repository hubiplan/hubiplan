1. Open the [surveys](/umfragen).
2. Click **New survey** and choose **Opinion poll (yes/no/abstain)**.
3. Word the question so that it can be answered with yes or no.
4. Choose who is asked: all members, individual sub-units or only the board.
5. Decide who sees names: **open**, **only the administrators see names** or **anonymous**.
6. Click **Start**.
7. Close the survey at the set end or immediately with **Close now**.

A straw poll is not a resolution; formal votes belong in the meetings. After the start, questions and audience can no longer be changed.
