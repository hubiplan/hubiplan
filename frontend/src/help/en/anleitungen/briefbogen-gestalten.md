1. Open the [letterhead](/einstellungen/briefbogen).
2. Under **Logo for the letterhead**, upload an image of your own or keep the crest from the appearance settings.
3. Under **Name in the letterhead**, enter how the name breaks across lines in the header.
4. Choose **Body text font** and **Line colour**.
5. Check the bank details and the information in the footer.
6. Look at the result under **Preview of the first page**.

The letterhead applies to all letters and PDFs of the unit, including invoices, receipts and circulars.
