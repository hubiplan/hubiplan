1. Open the [gallery](/webseite/galerie).
2. Enter a title and click **New album**.
3. Enter a date so that the website shows the year and sorts by it.
4. Use **Upload photos** to upload many photos at once, or drag them onto the page.
5. Choose the cover picture.
6. Tick **Show on the website**.

Show photos of recognisable people only with their consent. The gallery area must be switched on under Menu and pages.
