1. Open the meeting under [Meetings](/versammlungen) and choose **Attendance**.
2. Choose **Present** or **Excused** for each person.
3. If the type allows proxies, choose **Represented** and then the present member who holds the proxy.
4. Enter guests under **Guest without a vote**.
5. Read at the top how many voting members are present and whether the meeting has a quorum.

With **Open check-in via app**, members check themselves in with a QR code; the list then updates by itself and shows “via app” or “remote”.

For the list you also need read access to members. Organisations as members attend **By representative**.
