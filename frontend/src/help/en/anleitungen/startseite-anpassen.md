1. Open the home page.
2. Click **Customise home page** at the top right.
3. Drag a block to its new place.
4. Hide or show a block with **Show** and choose under **Latitude** how much room it gets.
5. Click **Done**.
6. For the quick actions, choose up to six actions you need often under **Customise**.

The arrangement applies only to you and separately per unit. **Restore default** returns to the usual order.
