1. Open the [deadline calendar](/fristenkalender).
2. Check under **Suggestions** whether the duty is already prepared, then click **Apply**.
3. Otherwise click **New obligation** and enter the name and the next deadline.
4. Choose the recurrence and how many days in advance HUBIplan reminds you.
5. Choose the responsible role, for example the treasurer.
6. Click **Save obligation**.
7. Later mark the duty with **Done**; a recurring duty then moves on to its next deadline.

The duty belongs to the role: when the office changes hands, HUBIplan reminds the new holders. The suggestions do not replace legal or tax advice.
