1. Open [Members › Setup › Online application](/mitglieder/einrichtung/antrag).
2. Tick **Switch on online application** and choose under **Membership types in the form** the types people can apply for.
3. Decide which **Fields** are required or optional, and whether a SEPA mandate is offered.
4. Choose under **Who decides?** who has to approve admission according to your statutes.
5. Under **Share the form**, copy the address or download the QR code, e.g. for the website, flyers or a notice board.

For a tablet at the club booth, create a link with label and validity under **Booth mode** and open it on the device. People sign on the screen there; no email confirmation is needed.
