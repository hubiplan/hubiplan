1. Find the year's jubilarians under [Members › Anniversaries](/mitglieder/jubilaeen).
2. Open the person's record.
3. In the **Honours** section, choose the honour and the date and click **Award**.

If the person holds a special title with it, enter it under **Title**, e.g. "honorary
captain" for the honour "honorary member". The website then shows this title instead of the
name of the honour. You can change it later with the pencil next to the honour.

You define which honours exist under Members › Setup › Honours.
