1. Open the [entries](/meldungen).
2. Choose the announcement; if it arrived on paper or by e-mail, record it yourself with **Create call for entries**.
3. Add a team with **Add team** and choose its members.
4. Add guests with **Add guest** if the announcement allows non-members.
5. Check the notes below the entry.
6. Click **Submit entry**.

If the organiser uses HUBIplan, the entry goes straight to them; otherwise you send it as a letter, e-mail or spreadsheet. You can report changes later.
