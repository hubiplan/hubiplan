As a musician you find your performances, rehearsals, sheet music and absences here.

## Performances and rehearsals

**My performances** lists each date separately, with performance type, meeting point, uniform and programme. The uniform comes with a description, a checklist of what belongs to it and, where available, an image. Respond with **Accept** or **Decline**; your current response is marked. If HUBIplan knows of a conflict with another date, it is shown as text.

## Sheet music

**Sheet music for this rehearsal** shows the files released for your part. There is no sheet music for cancelled rehearsals or after you have declined.

## Absences

Under **My absences** you enter when you are not available. Start and end are enough; you do not need to give a reason.

## Remuneration

Under **My remuneration** you only see your own approved remuneration, with **Partially paid** and **Outstanding remuneration**.
