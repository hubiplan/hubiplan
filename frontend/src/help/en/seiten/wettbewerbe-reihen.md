A series groups a competition that recurs regularly, for example the annual cup shooting. This keeps its history visible over the years.

On the website the archive of a series appears under a readable address made from its name, such as `/ergebnisse/reihe/pokalschiessen/`. You can change it on the series under **Address on the website**; earlier addresses redirect.
