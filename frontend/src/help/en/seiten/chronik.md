Under **Chronicle** you maintain the history of the club as a timeline: short entries with a
year or period, optionally with pictures, and chapters as subheadings. A short introduction sits
above it.

The chronicle is a module of its own and useful even without a website, for example for an
anniversary book. If the **Website** module is switched on, the published version appears there
under “Chronicle”.

HUBIplan saves changes as a draft. **Publish** makes the draft the valid version; earlier
versions can be brought back into the draft below. You can take over many entries at once from
an Excel file.

Anyone with the “Maintain chronicle” right may edit it; anyone with “Read chronicle” may read
it. Roles that edit website content already have both.
