If the club has set your department to confirm expense claims first, you see the claims for your department here. The leaders check that the expense was for you and choose **Confirm** or **Send a query**. Then the treasurer reviews, approves and pays. Your own claim is confirmed by someone else. To confirm, the role needs the right "Review expense claims" in the department.
