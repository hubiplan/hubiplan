Here you decide which sections the website has, such as events, sub-units, chronicle or contact, what they are called in the menu and in which order they appear.

Unpublished changes: When there is a draft, the bar “Unpublished changes” with **Publish** and **Discard** is shown at the very top. It stays visible below the top bar while you scroll.

Members: The heading “Members” has one list per member type, for example “Active” and “Passive”. You switch each list on separately and give it a name with the pencil. The lists show today’s members by surname, with the year they joined and, if you like, their main instrument (settings under “People”). Only members who consented appear: in the member record on the “Photo & website” tab under “Publication on the website”, item “Member list”. Anyone who objected to publishing their name is still left out. “In memoriam” shows members whose reason for leaving is “death”, with the year of death, but only if the board has approved each of them in their member record under “In memoriam”; a text page with the address `in-gedenken` appears above it as an introduction.

Custom headings: “Add heading” creates a menu item without its own page, for example “Band”. Drag sections onto it and they become its sub-items. Without sub-items the heading does not appear. If you leave the name empty, it is removed when saved.

External links: “Add external link” creates a menu item that leads to another website, for example your federation. Use the pencil to enter the name and the address (with https://). The link opens in a new window and can be moved or made a sub-item like any other item. If you leave the name empty, it is removed when saved.

Button: Under “Highlight a menu item as a button” you choose one main item that appears in the menu as a coloured button, such as “Contact” or a link “Become a member”. There is at most one button; headings and items with sub-items are not possible.

Menu items in the footer: if the menu bar gets too full, “Menu items in the footer” lets you set for each main item whether it appears “in the menu”, “footer only” or “menu and footer”. In the footer it appears next to the legal notice and privacy policy, for example “Contact”. The page stays reachable and its block stays switched on. Headings and items with sub-items are not possible. “Home” and the search are set under Website › Fine-tuning, in the header and menu settings.

Draft: order, names, links, button and footer place of the menu go into the draft, like design and home page. The preview under Website › Design and colours shows the new menu; it becomes public with **Publish**. The check boxes that switch a section on or off, however, apply immediately.

Home page: You put together the home page sections under Website › Home, with the preview next to them.

Repertoire and press: “Repertoire” shows the pieces from the sheet music library that are ticked “In the repertoire on the website”. “Press” shows the press clippings from Website › Documents.

Bookings: “Bookings” shows facilities and material whose booking calendar is not set to “do not show” under “On the website”, as a week view with a calendar subscription. Depending on the setting it shows only “Booked” or, for public bookings, also the purpose; names never appear.

Search: With the “Search” section a magnifying glass appears in the menu. It searches everything the website shows publicly: the greeting and sections of the home page (including tiles, buttons and questions), text pages with their sections, the chronicle, units, area introductions, albums, documents and events. Drafts and people are not searched. Part of a word is enough, and case and accents do not matter: “flag” finds “Flag bearers”.

People: “Show titles before names” puts the title from the member record before the name (e.g. “Dr. Max Muster”) for the board, lists and results; when off, names appear without a title. “Show main instrument below the name” takes the main instrument from Music › Musicians. “Show postal address for the board” only applies to people with the consent “Postal address” in their member record.

Events: If there are sub-units, a check box adds their public events to the website calendar; if there are parent units with events (e.g. the umbrella association), a second one adds their events that are shared downwards. If there are neither, the “Events” block is not shown.
