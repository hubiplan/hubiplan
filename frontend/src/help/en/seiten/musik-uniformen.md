Here you define the fixed choice for your performances. Editing requires music editing permission.

## Uniforms

Use **Add uniform** to record, for example, a summer uniform, a winter uniform or traditional costume. Under **What belongs to it** write one line per item, such as “cap” or “white gloves”. You can add an image. On a performance you choose the uniform from the list; use **Uniform note** for deviations such as “without jacket”. Assigned musicians see the uniform, description, checklist and image in the member area.

Archive a uniform you no longer wear. It stays on past performances but cannot be newly chosen. It can only be removed while no performance uses it.

## Performance types

HUBIplan provides common types such as procession, parade, tattoo, morning pint and concert. Use **Add performance type** for your own. Each type can suggest a formation and a uniform; both are filled in as soon as you choose the type on a performance and can be changed there. With **Show the type with the event on the website** the type appears with the public event on the club website.

Under Music › Performances you can filter by type. **Performances by year and type** counts the performances that were not cancelled.
