Under **Fine-tuning** you adjust the chosen design without any programming. Each control has fixed steps, for example corners “square”, “slight” or “round”; fonts are chosen from a list; yes/no questions are switches. Where it helps, a small icon above the word shows what the step looks like. Without your own choice the website shows what the design provides; that step is marked with a dot, and switches say “as the design”. If a design lies between the steps (for example the corners), there is no dot. All changes go into the draft first, and the preview on the right shows the effect.

The controls are in nine collapsible groups: **Font**, **Readability**, **Page**, **Cards, images, buttons**, **Header**, **Menu**, **Home**, **Sub-pages and events** and **Footer**. Only controls that currently apply are shown: the position of the coat of arms only when the coat of arms is shown, the title image controls only when the home page has one, the colour on top only when darkening, the search only with the “Search” block and the date only with events switched on. Whatever a design sets itself (such as the position of the coat of arms in the sidebar design) is listed in one line at the bottom of the group.

## Presets and preview

**Presets** set several controls at once: **Calm** (plain, lots of white space), **Festive** (serifs, script font, title image in club colour), **Modern** (round corners, shadows, menu as buttons) and **Easy to read** (Atkinson Hyperlegible font, large text, high contrast, striped tables). Afterwards every control can still be changed one by one. The search field filters the controls, for example by “crest” or “phone”. Changed controls are listed at the top as small chips: a click jumps to the control, “×” resets it. “↺” next to a control resets it, “Reset group …” the whole group, **Reset all** all controls. A second click on the chosen field also resets the control.

After a change the preview jumps to the matching spot and briefly highlights it: header and menu at the top, the footer at the bottom, the heading of sub-pages on a sub-page (the legal notice), the date on the events page. For **Menu on phones** it switches to the phone view.

## Font

**Font** (“System”, “Modern” with Open Sans and Roboto, “Festive” with Open Sans and serif headings, “**Clear**” with Source Sans 3 or “**Elegant**” with Source Sans 3 and headings in Crimson Pro or “**Easy to read**” with Atkinson Hyperlegible, whose letters are especially easy to tell apart), a separate **Heading font** (“same as text”, “Modern”, “Festive”, “Clear”, “Elegant”, “**Blackletter**” or “**Bold**” with the narrow, heavy Oswald; blackletter only suits headings and is never used for body text), headings plain, with a line, **short line** (a short stroke in the decorative colour below the heading) or in colour, their alignment, the **Heading size** (“normal” or “larger”), the **Page title size** (“small”, “medium”, “normal” or “larger”; only the heading at the top of the page, it takes precedence over the heading size, and “small” or “medium” fits if the title would otherwise look larger than the club name in the header), the line spacing and a script font for the greeting. The fonts are stored on the website itself (free licences); nothing is loaded from other servers.

## Readability

**Font size** up to “very large”, **Increased contrast** (darker text, stronger lines), **Dark mode** › “follow device” (the website turns dark when the visitor's phone or computer is set to dark; header, menu and footer keep their club colours, design packages with their own CSS stay light), **Striped tables** (every second row shaded, in the surface colour or lightly tinted), the **“Back to top” button on long pages** and **Fade in while scrolling** (“subtle”: sections and cards appear gently while scrolling; visitors who chose “reduce motion” on their device never see it).

## Page, cards, images, buttons

**Page** and Cards, images, buttons: width, background, spacing, corners (“square” also applies to photos and initials of people, the social media icons, the chronicle with its photos, cards and jump links, and the fields of the contact form), **Light sections** (home page sections with a light background “tinted” in the club colour, “warm” or “grey”), cards (border, shadow, flat or **white with stripe**: white cards with a fine edge, events with a club-colour stripe on the left, subunit tiles on top), images, **Image format in tiles** (3:2, 4:3 or square for home page tiles, gallery albums and posts), frames around images and buttons (“filled”, “outlined” or **Label**: capitals, slightly spaced). On coloured sections, buttons and decorative lines always use the text colour of the section so they stand out.

## Header

With **Header background** › **Header image** you choose an image from the image library that appears at the top of the website instead of the coat of arms and club name, for example a banner with lettering. It should contain the club name and be wide, about 1200 × 300 pixels. Without a header image, or with the background “white” or “coloured”, the coat of arms and name stay. **Header height** sets how tall the header image can get: “low”, “medium”, “normal” or “high”. On a phone a wide header image quickly becomes too small next to the menu button; below the header image you can therefore choose an optional **Header image for phones**, which the website shows below 600 pixels wide. You can also choose several header images (and several header images for phones), up to ten: the website then shows one of them on each visit, on every page. Also here: coat of arms, name, decorative line and **Pin header and menu to the top**. The coat of arms and the club name cannot both be hidden.

## Menu

Type of menu (bar, buttons, text), the active item, **Lettering** (“normal” or in capitals), **Menu font size**, **Menu on phones** (“collapsible” behind the “Menu” button or as a **swipeable bar**), **“Home” in the menu** (off: on larger screens the coat of arms, name or header image lead to the home page, the phone menu keeps “Home”) and **Search** (“in the menu”, “top right” in the header or “in the footer”). To hide the search completely, switch off the “Search” block under Website › Menu and building blocks.

## Home

**Cover image** (height), **Darken title image** (“slight” or “strong”) with **Colour on top** (“black”, “club colour” or “second colour”): a semi-transparent colour over the image makes text on it easier to read and tints the image in the club colours. **Image crop** sets whether the top, middle or bottom stays visible when the image is cropped. **Name on the title image** with **Position of the name** (“top”, “centred” or “band at the bottom”), **Image carousel advances** (“only by hand”, “slowly”, “normal” or “random”: the home page then shows a different image from the carousel on each visit, which stays fixed like a single title image; the same applies to the image in the heading band of the other pages) and **Separate sections** (“no”, “with a line” or “alternately tinted”; in the “Stage” design this switches the coloured bands off). If the image carousel is set to full width under Website › Home, the title image reaches from edge to edge. **Cover photo as stage** › “edge to edge” puts the cover photo right below the header without border or gap, at a limited height, with a gradient in the dark club colour at the bottom; the title lines and overline from the “Image carousel” block sit on it on the left. Several title lines change calmly with the images; pausing the carousel also pauses the line. **Image carousel buttons** (pause, back, next and counter) are “always visible” or only appear “on mouse-over” on the image or with the keyboard; on phones and tablets they always stay visible. Without your own choice they are always shown on the stage, otherwise on mouse-over. “In content width” shows the same stage only as wide as the content column, so more of the photo stays visible on wide screens; the **Stage width** is “like the content”, “a little wider” or “wide”, and the **Area beside the stage** then shows the page background or the dark club colour. On phones it looks like “edge to edge”.

## Sub-pages and events

**Heading of sub-pages** “plain”, as a **coloured band** or **with title image** (the home page image behind the heading, darkened as set there), the **Date field** as a tile, strip or **Cards** (the upcoming events on the home page and sub-unit pages as cards side by side with a large date, the next one in the club colour) and **Date next to it**: only the weekday, short (“Sat 14/11/2026”) or long (“Saturday 14 November 2026”).

There are also controls for individual pages; without your own choice every page stays as it was:

- **Title holders** “Band and cards”: the current title holders appear large in a band in the dark club colour directly below the page title, with the photo on the left and, on the right, the dignity as a top line with a short line in the second colour, the name in large type and the term of office. Below, the last eight predecessors appear as cards (two side by side on a phone) and a button “All … entries” expands the full list. Without a photo the band stays, with text only.
- **People without a photo** “no placeholder”: the board, honours and contacts of sub-units no longer show an initial letter when there is no photo; the name moves to the left. Photos stay.
- **Person cards** “Office as a label”: the office in small capitals above the name, the name in large type in the heading font, “Write a message” as an underlined text link and a top edge in the club colour; several people in one office are listed one below the other, honorary members in a three-column grid.
- **Honours** “one card per person”: all honoured people appear in one shared grid with three columns (one on a phone) like the board, without subheadings. At the top of each card the honour appears in small type, below it the name in large type. If someone was given their own title with the award (field **Title**, e.g. “Ehrenhauptmann” for the honour “Ehrenmitglied”), the card shows this title. You enter the title in the member record next to the honour.
- **Form of address in the contact form** “formal (Sie)” or “informal (du)”: applies to the labels, the introduction, error messages and the confirmation on German pages. You write the box to the right of the form (e.g. “Want to join in?” with a button) under Website › Texts as the introduction of the **Contact** area.
- **Chronicle eras** “as a band”: each era edge to edge in the dark club colour, with the period in large type in the second colour, the title and the text of the era; the timeline continues below. The jump links at the top become underlined links, and the years use the heading font.
- **Result rankings** “Places 1 to 3 as cards”: above each ranking the first three places appear as cards with a large place number (place 1 in the club colour), name, sub-unit and result with its unit, such as “48 Ringe”. In a tie everyone sharing a place gets a card; the table below starts at place 4 and is left out when there are no further places.
- **Long lists** “in two columns”: long, narrow tables such as the list of all kings, the winners of a challenge trophy or the winners of a competition over the years appear side by side in two columns on a computer screen, so the page is only half as long. You read the left column from top to bottom first, then the right one; with an odd number of rows the left column has one more, and both show the header row. This only applies to tables with no more than two columns and at least 20 rows, and only from about 800 pixels wide; on a phone and in narrow windows it stays a single column. Screen readers still read one table in the right order.

Labels, the numbers of places 2 and 3 and the box next to the contact form use the colour you chose for the date field among the design colours.

## Footer

Colour, alignment, **Layout** (“simple” or “with crest and name”: the crest on the left, next to it the club name with founding year and town and the note on the umbrella association, on the right the links stacked and social media, below a fine line “© year · data from HUBIplan”; stacked on phones), with the crest layout the switches **Club name next to the crest** and **Founding year and town** (switch off if the crest or the header already shows the name; without the name the note on the umbrella association sits centred next to the crest, and the crest gives the name to screen readers) and **Line above the footer** (none, ornament line, second colour or club colour).
