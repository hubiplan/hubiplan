Under **Fine-tuning** you adjust the chosen design without any programming. Each control has two to four fixed steps, for example corners “square”, “slight” or “round”; yes/no questions are switches. Where it helps, a small icon above the word shows what the step looks like. Without your own choice the website shows what the design provides; that step is marked with a dot, and switches say “as the design”. If a design lies between the steps (for example the corners), there is no dot. All changes go into the draft first, and the preview on the right shows the effect.

The controls are in nine collapsible groups: **Font**, **Readability**, **Page**, **Cards, images, buttons**, **Header**, **Menu**, **Home**, **Sub-pages and events** and **Footer**. Only controls that currently apply are shown: the position of the coat of arms only when the coat of arms is shown, the title image controls only when the home page has one, the colour on top only when darkening, the search only with the “Search” block and the date only with events switched on. Whatever a design sets itself (such as the position of the coat of arms in the sidebar design) is listed in one line at the bottom of the group.

**Presets** set several controls at once: **Calm** (plain, lots of white space), **Festive** (serifs, script font, title image in club colour), **Modern** (round corners, shadows, menu as buttons) and **Easy to read** (large text, high contrast, striped tables). Afterwards every control can still be changed one by one. The search field filters the controls, for example by “crest” or “phone”. Changed controls are listed at the top as small chips: a click jumps to the control, “×” resets it. “↺” next to a control resets it, “Reset group …” the whole group, **Reset all** all controls. A second click on the chosen field also resets the control.

After a change the preview jumps to the matching spot and briefly highlights it: header and menu at the top, the footer at the bottom, the heading of sub-pages on a sub-page (the legal notice), the date on the events page. For **Menu on phones** it switches to the phone view.

Font: typeface, a separate **Heading font** (“same as text”, “Modern” or “Festive”), headings plain, with a line or in colour, their alignment, the line spacing and a script font for the greeting. The fonts are stored on the website itself (free licences); nothing is loaded from other servers.

Readability: **Font size** up to “very large”, **Increased contrast** (darker text, stronger lines), **Dark mode** › “follow device” (the website turns dark when the visitor's phone or computer is set to dark; header, menu and footer keep their club colours, design packages with their own CSS stay light), **Striped tables** (every second row shaded, in the surface colour or lightly tinted), the **“Back to top” button on long pages** and **Fade in while scrolling** (“subtle”: sections and cards appear gently while scrolling; visitors who chose “reduce motion” on their device never see it).

**Page** and Cards, images, buttons: width, background, spacing, corners, cards, images, **Image format in tiles** (3:2, 4:3 or square for home page tiles, gallery albums and posts), frames around images and buttons.

Header: With **Header background** › **Header image** you choose an image from the image library that appears at the top of the website instead of the coat of arms and club name, for example a banner with lettering. It should contain the club name and be wide, about 1200 × 300 pixels. Without a header image, or with the background “white” or “coloured”, the coat of arms and name stay. **Header height** sets how tall the header image can get: “low”, “medium”, “normal” or “high”. On a phone a wide header image quickly becomes too small next to the menu button; below the header image you can therefore choose an optional **Header image for phones**, which the website shows below 600 pixels wide. Also here: coat of arms, name, decorative line and **Pin header and menu to the top**. The coat of arms and the club name cannot both be hidden.

Menu: type of menu (bar, buttons, text), the active item, **Lettering** (“normal” or in capitals), **Menu font size**, **Menu on phones** (“collapsible” behind the “Menu” button or as a **swipeable bar**), **“Home” in the menu** (off: on larger screens the coat of arms, name or header image lead to the home page, the phone menu keeps “Home”) and **Search** (“in the menu”, “top right” in the header or “in the footer”). To hide the search completely, switch off the “Search” block under Website › Menu and building blocks.

Home: **Cover image** (height), **Darken title image** (“slight” or “strong”) with **Colour on top** (“black”, “club colour” or “second colour”): a semi-transparent colour over the image makes text on it easier to read and tints the image in the club colours. **Image crop** sets whether the top, middle or bottom stays visible when the image is cropped. **Name on the title image** with **Position of the name** (“top”, “centred” or “band at the bottom”), **Image carousel advances** (“only by hand”, “slowly” or “normal”) and **Separate sections** (“no”, “with a line” or “alternately tinted”; in the “Stage” design this switches the coloured bands off).

Sub-pages and events: **Heading of sub-pages** “plain”, as a **coloured band** or **with title image** (the home page image behind the heading, darkened as set there), the **Date field** as a tile or strip and **Date next to it**: only the weekday, short (“Sat 14/11/2026”) or long (“Saturday 14 November 2026”).

Footer: colour and alignment.
