Under **Fine-tuning** you adjust the chosen design without any programming. Each control has fixed steps, for example corners “square”, “slight” or “round”; fonts are chosen from a list; yes/no questions are switches. Where it helps, a small icon above the word shows what the step looks like. Without your own choice the website shows what the design provides; that step is marked with a dot, and switches say “as the design”. If a design lies between the steps (for example the corners), there is no dot. All changes go into the draft first, and the preview on the right shows the effect.

On the left is the bar with the areas of the website, from top to bottom: **Basic style**, **Font**, **Readability**, **Header and menu**, **Title image and home page**, **Subpages**, **Website areas** and **Footer**. A click shows the controls of that area on the right, grouped into small sections (for the header and menu, for example, “Header”, “Menu” and “On phones”). The number next to an area tells you how many controls are changed there. On a phone you first see the list of areas; “‹ All areas” takes you back.

Each section shows the most important controls first. Less common ones are under “More settings” and are named there; changed controls always stay visible. The switch **Show all controls** expands everything and remembers this on the device. A small “i” next to the name opens an explanation.

Whatever does not apply right now stays greyed out and says why, for example “Choose “Darken” first.” for the tint, or “Search is switched off.” with a link to Menu and pages; “Go there” jumps to the control it depends on. Dependent controls are indented below that control. Whatever a design sets itself (such as the position of the coat of arms in the sidebar design) is listed in one line at the bottom of the section with the link **Change design**. Changed controls have a coloured edge on the left.

## Quick start, search and reset

Under **Quick start**, **Presets** set several controls at once: **Calm** (plain, lots of white space), **Festive** (serifs, script font, title image in club colour), **Modern** (round corners, shadows, menu as buttons) and **Easy to read** (Atkinson Hyperlegible font, large text, high contrast, striped tables). Afterwards every control can still be changed one by one. The search field searches all areas, including the further settings, for example by “crest” or “phone”; each hit names its area and section. **Changed only** lists all changed controls: a click jumps to the control, “×” resets it. “↺” next to a control resets it, “↺ … changed” the section, **Reset area** the whole area and **Reset all** under Quick start all controls. A second click on the chosen field also resets the control.

**Undo** and **Redo** above the controls take back the last steps or bring them back, also after a preset or a reset (up to 50 steps, together with design and colours). Outside text fields this also works with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. The history starts again when you reload the page, publish or discard.

## Preview and pointing

After a change the preview jumps to the matching spot and briefly highlights it: header and menu at the top, the footer at the bottom, the page title on a sub-page (the legal notice), the date on the events page. For **Menu on phones** it switches to the phone view.

With **Show** above the preview it also works the other way round: move the mouse over the website, and the part below gets a frame and a label with its area. A click opens the matching controls; links do not lead away while doing so. A second click on **Show** switches it off.

## Font

**Font** (“System”, “Modern” with Open Sans and Roboto, “Festive” with Open Sans and serif headings, “**Clear**” with Source Sans 3 or “**Elegant**” with Source Sans 3 and headings in Crimson Pro or “**Easy to read**” with Atkinson Hyperlegible, whose letters are especially easy to tell apart), a separate **Heading font** (“same as text”, “Modern”, “Festive”, “Clear”, “Elegant”, “**Blackletter**” or “**Bold**” with the narrow, heavy Oswald; blackletter only suits headings and is never used for body text), headings plain, with a line, **short line** (a short stroke in the decorative colour below the heading) or in colour, their alignment, the **Heading size** (“normal” or “larger”), the **Page title size** (“small”, “medium”, “normal” or “larger”; only the heading at the top of the page, it takes precedence over the heading size, and “small” or “medium” fits if the title would otherwise look larger than the club name in the header), the line spacing and a script font for the greeting. The fonts are stored on the website itself (free licences); nothing is loaded from other servers.

**Own font stack** takes a house font from a style guide that is not free, such as “Lucida Sans, Lucida Grande, Segoe UI, Arial”: up to six font names separated by commas. The website does not deliver a font file for it; the font only appears where it is installed on the device, otherwise the next one in the list and finally a sans-serif fallback (or a serif one if you write “serif” last). On phones and under Linux the fallback usually appears. The free fonts above are not affected. The own font stack takes precedence over the font and also applies to headings unless a separate **Heading font** is chosen there; **Own font stack for headings** gives them their own list. Only letters, digits, spaces and hyphens are allowed.

## Readability

**Font size** up to “very large”, **Increased contrast** (darker text, stronger lines), **Dark mode** › “follow device” (the website turns dark when the visitor's phone or computer is set to dark; header, menu and footer keep their club colours, design packages with their own CSS stay light), **Striped tables** (every second row shaded, in the surface colour or lightly tinted), the **“Back to top” button on long pages** and **Fade in while scrolling** (“subtle”: sections and cards appear gently while scrolling; visitors who chose “reduce motion” on their device never see it).

## Basic style

Page, cards and buttons, images: width, background, spacing, corners (“square” also applies to photos and initials of people, the social media icons, the chronicle with its photos, cards and jump links, and the fields of the contact form), **Colour of light sections** (under title image and home page: home page sections with a light background “tinted” in the club colour, “warm” or “grey”), cards (border, shadow, flat or **white with stripe**: white cards with a fine edge, events with a club-colour stripe on the left, subunit tiles on top), images, **Image format in tiles** (3:2, 4:3 or square for home page tiles, gallery albums and posts), frames around images and buttons (“filled”, “outlined” or **Label**: capitals, slightly spaced). On coloured sections, buttons and decorative lines always use the text colour of the section so they stand out.

## Header and menu

With **Background** › **Header image** you choose an image from the image library that appears at the top of the website instead of the coat of arms and club name, for example a banner with lettering. It should contain the club name and be wide, about 1200 × 300 pixels. Without a header image, or with the background “white” or “coloured”, the coat of arms and name stay. **Height** sets how tall the header image can get: “low”, “medium”, “normal” or “high”. On a phone a wide header image quickly becomes too small next to the menu button; below the header image you can therefore choose an optional **Header image for phones**, which the website shows below 600 pixels wide. You can also choose several header images (and several header images for phones), up to ten: the website then shows one of them on each visit, on every page. Also here: **Crest** with its position, **Club name**, **Line below the header** and **Pin to the top**. The coat of arms and the club name cannot both be hidden.

In the menu section: **Menu style** (bar, buttons, text), **Active item**, **Lettering** (“normal” or in capitals), **Menu font size**, **Menu on phones** (“collapsible” behind the “Menu” button or as a **swipeable bar**), **“Home” in the menu** (off: on larger screens the coat of arms, name or header image lead to the home page, the phone menu keeps “Home”) and **Position of the search** (“in the menu”, “top right” in the header or “in the footer”). To hide the search completely, switch off the “Search” block under Website › Menu and pages.

## Title image and home page

**Height** of the title image, **Darken** (“slight” or “strong”) with **Tint** (“black”, “club colour” or “second colour”): a semi-transparent colour over the image makes text on it easier to read and tints the image in the club colours. **Image crop** sets whether the top, middle or bottom stays visible when the image is cropped. **Name on the title image** with **Position of the name** (“top”, “centred” or “band at the bottom”), **Advance** of the carousel (“only by hand”, “slowly”, “normal” or “random”: the home page then shows a different image from the carousel on each visit, which stays fixed like a single title image; the same applies to the image in the heading band of the other pages) and **Separate sections** (“no”, “with a line” or “alternately tinted”; in the “Stage” design this switches the coloured bands off). If the image carousel is set to full width under Website › Home, the title image reaches from edge to edge. **Width** › “edge to edge” puts the cover photo right below the header without border or gap, at a limited height, with a gradient in the dark club colour at the bottom; the title lines and overline from the “Image carousel” block sit on it on the left. Several title lines change calmly with the images; pausing the carousel also pauses the line. **Carousel buttons** (pause, back, next and counter) are “always visible” or only appear “on mouse-over” on the image or with the keyboard; on phones and tablets they always stay visible. Without your own choice they are always shown on the stage, otherwise on mouse-over. “In content width” shows the same stage only as wide as the content column, so more of the photo stays visible on wide screens; the **Stage width** is “like the content”, “a little wider” or “wide”, and the **Area beside the stage** then shows the page background or the dark club colour. On phones it looks like “edge to edge”.

## Subpages and website areas

Under **Subpages**: **Page title** “plain”, as a **coloured band** or **with title image** (the home page image behind the heading, darkened as set there), the **Band of sub-units** on their pages (in the “club colour” like the other bands, in the “box colour” of the colour role “Date field”, “light” in the tone of the light sections with a fine line, or “plain with line” on the page background; light and plain show the name in the dark club colour so the band does not look like a second page header; it only applies when the heading is shown as a band). Under **Website areas** you find the controls for each area: for events the **Events view** (“list by month” or “monthly calendar”; on a phone only the days with events appear), the **Date** as a tile, strip or **Cards** (the upcoming events on the home page and sub-unit pages as cards side by side with a large date, the next one in the club colour) and **Date next to it**: only the weekday, short (“Sat 14/11/2026”) or long (“Saturday 14 November 2026”).

The **Notice board view** is cards with photo or a compact list, the **Sub-units view** large or small tiles or a list, the **Gallery view** tiles or masonry with pictures at their own height; a sketch shows each option. You write an introduction at the top of an area under Website › Texts. If an area is switched off, it says so there with a link to Menu and pages.

There are also controls for individual pages; without your own choice every page stays as it was:

- **Title holders** “Band and cards”: the current title holders appear large in a band in the dark club colour directly below the page title, with the photo on the left and, on the right, the dignity as a top line with a short line in the second colour, the name in large type and the term of office. Below, the last eight predecessors appear as cards (two side by side on a phone) and a button “All … entries” expands the full list. Without a photo the band stays, with text only.
- **People without a photo** “no placeholder”: the board, honours and contacts of sub-units no longer show an initial letter when there is no photo; the name moves to the left. Photos stay.
- **Person cards** “Office as a label”: the office in small capitals above the name, the name in large type in the heading font, “Write a message” as an underlined text link and a top edge in the club colour; several people in one office are listed one below the other, honorary members in a three-column grid.
- **Honours** “one card per person”: all honoured people appear in one shared grid with three columns (one on a phone) like the board, without subheadings. At the top of each card the honour appears in small type, below it the name in large type. If someone was given their own title with the award (field **Title**, e.g. “Ehrenhauptmann” for the honour “Ehrenmitglied”), the card shows this title. You enter the title in the member record next to the honour.
- The **Salutation** (formal “Sie” or informal “du” for the contact form and the events page) has been under Website › Overview › **Salutation** since 16 November 2026, because it concerns the tone of the texts rather than the design. Like the design, it only applies after **Publish**.
- **Chronicle eras** “as a band”: each era edge to edge in the dark club colour, with the period in large type in the second colour, the title and the text of the era; the timeline continues below. The jump links at the top become underlined links, and the years use the heading font. “as a slim band” shows the same band much lower: the period (in the second colour) and the title on one line, the text of the era small below; on phones the title moves below the period.
- **Result rankings** “Places 1 to 3 as cards”: above each ranking the first three places appear as cards with a large place number (place 1 in the club colour), name, sub-unit and result with its unit, such as “48 Ringe”. In a tie everyone sharing a place gets a card; the table below starts at place 4 and is left out when there are no further places.
- **Long lists** “in two columns”: long, narrow tables such as the list of all kings, the winners of a challenge trophy or the winners of a competition over the years appear side by side in two columns on a computer screen, so the page is only half as long. You read the left column from top to bottom first, then the right one; with an odd number of rows the left column has one more, and both show the header row. This only applies to tables with no more than two columns and at least 20 rows, and only from about 800 pixels wide; on a phone and in narrow windows it stays a single column. Screen readers still read one table in the right order.

Labels, the numbers of places 2 and 3 and the box next to the contact form use the colour you chose for the date field among the design colours.

## Footer

Colour, alignment, **Layout** (“simple” or “with crest and name”: the crest on the left, next to it the club name with founding year and town and the note on the umbrella association, on the right the links stacked and social media, below a fine line “© year · data from HUBIplan”; stacked on phones), with the crest layout the switches **Club name next to the crest** and **Founding year and town** (switch off if the crest or the header already shows the name; without the name the note on the umbrella association sits centred next to the crest, and the crest gives the name to screen readers) and **Line above the footer** (none, ornament line, second colour or club colour).
