The change log shows who changed what and when. The entries are chained, so later changes to the log can be detected.

Anyone allowed to audit the accounts without editing them (for example the auditors) receives a weekly email with the checksum of the latest entry. Please keep these emails: they let you prove later that bookings and the log have not been altered. Fixed bookings and sealed log entries are also protected against changes in the database itself.
