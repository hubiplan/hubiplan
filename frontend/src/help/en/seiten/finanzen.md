The overview shows account balances, open invoices and upcoming tasks for the treasurer. On the left, the parts of finance are grouped along the financial year: **Fees & invoices**, **Payments**, **Bookkeeping**, **Evaluate & close** and **Set up**. Without the fees and invoices module the first group is just called **Invoices** and contains the incoming invoices. The group of the page you are on is open; ▸ expands another group for a look. Which groups and entries appear depends on the enabled modules and your rights; a group with only one entry is shown as a plain entry.
