Create a personal calendar-subscription link for your own calendar app. Keep it private. Creating a new link invalidates the previous link. A status confirms when your link is ready.

With a board role you also see the **Board subscription** below: a separate link with only the “board only” events of your unit. This lets you show or hide the board calendar in your calendar app on its own. The link only works while you hold a board office; when it ends, the link no longer returns events. Do not pass it on, not even into a shared calendar.
