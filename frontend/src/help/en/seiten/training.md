Under **Training** you record who attended practice evenings and training sessions. A list belongs
to the club or to a sub-unit such as the youth section or a division; the attendance list then
only shows that sub-unit's members.

## Creating a session

**New training session** creates a practice evening or training session with date, time, place and leader.
If a matching event is in the calendar, **Take over from the calendar** copies its details with
one click. HUBIplan calculates the hours from start and end, for example for coach expenses.

## Recording attendance

In a session you tick off who is there. It is quicker with **Open check-in**: the page then shows
a QR code that members scan with the HUBIplan app or their phone camera. The code changes every 30
seconds, so a photo of it is no use to anyone who is not there. Check-in works on the day of the
session and on the day before or after. **Show code full screen** fills the screen, for example for a
TV in the clubhouse.

## Evaluation

**Evaluation** counts who attended how often in a period and shows the total hours. **Download as Excel** gives the attendance per member and the list of sessions, for example for grant
applications.

## Shooting log and shooting record

If the Shooting module is also switched on, there is the **Shooting log**. For the firearms
licence requirement (section 14 of the German Weapons Act, WaffG), sport shooters must prove that
they shoot regularly:

- Acquisition (para. 3): member for twelve months and, in the last twelve months, shot in
  every month or 18 times in total.
- Possession (para. 4): in the last 24 months once per quarter or six times in each
  twelve-month period, separately for handguns and long guns. After ten years, membership is
  enough.

Entries come about in three ways: a session marked as a shooting date records everyone
present with discipline and firearm. The board adds entries to a member's shooting log by hand.
Members report sessions outside training in the member area; the board confirms or rejects them
under **Reported shooting dates**.

Only confirmed sessions with firearms that need a licence count; air guns do not count. At most
one session per discipline counts per day. HUBIplan also points out breaks of more than four
months and more sessions in other clubs than in your own, as for example the BSSB guideline
expects.

**Create PDF** produces the confirmation of shooting dates for the last 24 months with the
status under section 14 WaffG. The board member authorised to represent the club signs and stamps
it; it is the attachment to the application to the association. The association's application
form is still needed. The association decides what it accepts.

## Rights and retention

Anyone with edit rights for “Training and attendance” can record attendance; roles that edit
events have it from the start. The module can be switched on and off under Settings › Modules.
HUBIplan deletes attendance and shooting log entries after ten years, and immediately when the
member's data is deleted.
