With the “Edit content” permission you change the content of the custom sections of the home page here, for example heading and text, the image or the buttons. Which sections exist, their order and how they look (background, width, column) is decided by whoever manages the website; that happens under Website › Design.

**Edit** opens a section, **Apply** saves the change to the draft. Visitors keep seeing the previous version until you choose **Publish**. On a wide screen the **Preview** on the right shows the website with the draft.

If the draft also changes the design, for example a different design, only the website managers can publish it. Your content changes stay in the draft and go online with it.
