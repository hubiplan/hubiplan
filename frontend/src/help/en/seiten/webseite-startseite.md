Everyone edits the home page in one place: whoever manages the website puts it together here and changes the content; people who may only “Edit content” change the content of the existing sections. The **Preview** on the right shows the website with the draft.

Under **Home** you build the home page from sections and see every change straight away in the preview. The existing sections (such as welcome text, upcoming events, majesties) exist once each; you switch them on and off. **Add section** adds your own sections, which you can use as often as you like: **Text**, **Text with image**, **Banner**, **Button bar**, **Tiles**, **Featured event** (with “N days to go”, disappears after the event), **Album preview**, **Quote**, **Contact persons** (only roles shown on the website, and people who have given their consent, as on the board page), **Frequently asked questions** (questions with answers to expand), **Partners and sponsors** (logos with a link; **Take over from business partners** fetches name and link of the business partners who have consented), **Directions** (address of a saved place with “Plan route”), **Events of one category** (the next public events of one category), **Document to download** (a published file from Website › Documents as a button), **Video as a link** (preview image linking to the video at the provider; the website embeds nothing), **Excerpt from the chronicle** (the latest timeline entries with a link to the chronicle), **Facts at a glance** (two to six numbers with a short text), **Divider** (line or space) and **Countdown** (counts down days, hours, minutes and seconds to the start of a public event or to a date and time of your choice; under **Afterwards** you choose whether the section then disappears or shows a text of your own, such as “The festival has started!”). There are also **Become a member** (an invitation with a button to the “Become a member” page; only appears when the online application is switched on), **Call for donations** (text, bank details, payment reference, optionally a target with a progress bar and a link to a donation page), **Opening and training hours** (days with times and a note), **Two columns** (two blocks of text side by side, one below the other on phones), **Table** (one to six columns with a header row), **Timeline** (your own milestones with a time, independent of the chronicle), **Festival programme** (programme items with day and time; consecutive entries with the same day appear under one heading), **Notice box** (info, caution or success, permanent), **Expandable sections** (headings with text to expand), **Images** (two to eight images of your own with captions, enlarged on click), **Download list** (up to ten published files from Website › Documents), **Majesties of a unit**, **Results of a competition** (winners and standings of a chosen, published competition), **Gallery overview** (the first albums of the gallery), **Latest posts** (from Instagram and Facebook), **Members' area and app** (button to sign in, optionally with a QR code and a link to the app) and **Figures from HUBIplan** (number of members, years since founding and number of units, always up to date and only as totals). Events and chronicle only appear when their area is switched on. Drag the sections by the handle into the order you want. **Edit** opens a section's content and appearance, **Apply** saves it to the draft, **Delete** removes one of your own sections. For each section you choose the **Background** (**None**, **Light**, a palette colour or a gradient, each with a matching text colour) and the **Section width** (**Text width**, **Content width** or **Full width**). In the “Newspaper” design, **Column** sets whether a section is in the **Main column** or the **Side column**. Buttons and tiles lead to a page of the website, the contact form, an event or, as an **External link**, to a new window.

With the “Edit content” permission you change the content of the custom sections, for example heading and text, the image or the buttons. Which sections exist, their order and how they look (background, width, column) is decided by whoever manages the website.

**Edit** opens a section, **Apply** saves the change to the draft. Visitors keep seeing the previous version until you choose **Publish**.

If the draft also changes the design, for example a different design, only the website managers can publish it. Your content changes stay in the draft and go online with it.
