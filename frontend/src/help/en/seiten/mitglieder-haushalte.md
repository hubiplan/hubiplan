Here you link members who belong together. **Families and households** lists members who live together, **Companies** lists members whose fees a company pays. A member belongs to at most one household and at most one company at the same time.

With **Create household** you enter a name, e.g. “Muster family”. **Add member** lets you search for a member, choose the role (**Adult** or **Child**; HUBIplan suggests it from the age) and the date from which they belong. If the member already belongs to another household, HUBIplan asks whether to move them; the previous membership in it then ends the day before. **Remove** ends the membership in the household today; it stays visible as a past entry (**Show former members**). When a member leaves the club, their membership in the household ends on the exit date. You can delete a household without current members.

**Find suggestions** shows current members with the same address who are not yet together in a household. **Create as household** takes over a suggestion; members who already belong to another household stay there.

One letter instead of four: A circular to members combines the letters to a household at the same address into one letter, and likewise emails to the same address. It goes to the first adult; the address block reads e.g. “Familie Erika und Max Muster” and the salutation “Sehr geehrte Familie Muster” or “Liebe Familie Muster” (letters stay in the club language). Anyone who lives elsewhere or has their own email address gets their own copy. You can switch this off per circular under **Recipient**. Circulars for an event (with a reply link per person) and texts with {Mitgliedsjahre} or {Jubiläumsjahre} still go out individually.

In the member list, the **Household** filter shows members with or without a household. In the member file, the household appears on the Master data tab together with its other members.

**Fee via the household**: With the Fees module, a household or company pays its members' fees with one invoice. Under **Payer and mandate** you enter who pays: a **Member** (usually a parent), a **Person without membership** with their own address, an **Organisation member** or a business partner. When adding a member, or later with **Fee via the household**, you decide per member that their fee goes on this invoice; for companies this is preselected, for families it is not. A fee goes via at most one household or company. Without a payer, fees are invoiced individually as before; the checklist before the fee run points this out. Direct debits use the paying member's mandate; if a person without membership or a business partner pays, you record the mandate here on the household. You set the family or company fee under Finance › Set up › Fee rates. The payer sees the invoice in the member area; the others see their line there.

Companies in the member area: if an organisation member pays for a company, its contact persons with access see the company's people under My area › Employees (only name, since when, and whether the fee is paid via the company). They can report new employees, who then get the online application and belong to the company with “Fee via the household” automatically once admitted, and end the payment for someone from a date. Then only the link to the company ends, and you receive an email at the club's contact address.
