Circulars go by email, letter or to the app to members, boards or holders of certain roles. You choose the type: everyone receives official notices such as invitations, while members can unsubscribe from “information”. Sent circulars remain available to read.

For organisations as members, the circular goes to every contact person marked “receives circulars”. Without such an address it goes to the organisation's email address or as a letter marked “attn.” of the primary contact. The placeholder {Organisation} inserts the organisation's name; the salutation addresses the contact person.

If members belong to a **household** at the same address, they get one joint letter, and emails to the same address become one email. The preview shows the others as “im gemeinsamen Schreiben an den Haushalt” (in the joint letter to the household). Under **Recipients** you switch this off with **Combine letters per household**. The placeholder {Haushalt} inserts the household name. You create households under [Members › Households and companies](/mitglieder/haushalte).

For a single letter, Circulars › Letter creates a Word template with the club letterhead and your sender details. The board designs the letterhead under Settings › Letterhead; you store your signature under My account.

A mailing list turns a mail sent to a club address into a circular. Anyone may write to an open mailing list. So that outsiders cannot reach many members unchecked, their mails go out directly only up to an adjustable number of recipients (50 by default) and up to a number per day. Anything beyond that is kept as a draft. Those allowed to write to the list are notified and release or delete the mail.

Name lists use a fixed alphabetical order: accented letters are placed with their base letter. Identical names keep their relative order when paging through the list.

You create a circular about an event in the event under **For this event**. It goes to those invited to the event, optionally only to those with a certain reply.

If the club belongs to an umbrella association, the **Received** tab shows its circulars to the boards. Without an umbrella association this tab does not exist.

Received circulars and their attachments are also available in the member area under **Circulars**. The Android app calls this shared inbox “Messages”. Changes to information emails are confirmed and keyboard focus is kept while saving. Further changes are blocked during the request; official notices are unaffected.
