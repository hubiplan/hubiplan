Here you record which shooting facilities the club has: how many lanes per discipline and whether there is a high stand for the bird shoot.

The details help when creating a competition: target selection first shows the targets that suit your lanes. For competitions at other facilities the full catalogue remains available.

If you already keep your ranges under Inventory & rooms, choose **Take from facilities**; you then maintain the lanes in one place only.
