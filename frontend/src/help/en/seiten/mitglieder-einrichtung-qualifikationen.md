Here you define which certificates you keep track of, such as first aid, coach licence, youth
leader, firearms competence or range officer. Nothing is predefined; under **Suggestions** one
click adds a common type that you can then adjust.

Each type has a **Usual validity in months**: it suggests the expiry date when recording
(empty: unlimited). **Reminder days before expiry** sets when the member receives an email and
from when the certificate counts as “expires soon”.

**Inspection note only (e.g. criminal record certificate)**: HUBIplan only records that and when
the certificate was inspected. There is no remark field and no document; you never store the
content of the criminal record certificate.

A type that is recorded for members can only be **archived**; the entries are kept. Roles with
“Edit organisation data” may edit the types.
