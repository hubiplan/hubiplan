The **Use of funds** tab under taxes (Tax & charitable status module) is a simplified statement on the use of funds. Non-profit funds must be used for the statutory purposes or placed in permitted reserves by the end of the second financial year after they were received.

The page shows per year the amount carried over, the funds received (income of the non-profit area and surpluses of the other areas), their use (expenses of the non-profit area, losses of purpose-related operations), additions to and releases from reserves, and what is still to be used by year of origin. The oldest funds are used first. If a deadline has passed, a notice appears. From 100,000 € income per year the tax office usually expects such a statement.
