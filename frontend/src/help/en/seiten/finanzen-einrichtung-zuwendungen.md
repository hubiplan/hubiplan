Enter here what must appear on every donation receipt. Without these details HUBIplan does not issue receipts.

- **Type of notice**, **Date of the notice** and **Privileged purposes (as in the notice)**: copy the details word for word from the tax office's notice. HUBIplan shows until when receipts are possible with it; after that you enter the new notice.
- **Signed by (name)**: who signs the receipts.

## Donations up to 300 €

For donations up to €300 the tax office accepts the bank statement together with a document from the club. Once the details are complete, the **Donations up to 300 €** section provides this document as a PDF and as text for the website.
