The **VAT** tab under taxes (Tax & charitable status module) is a template for the advance VAT return. For the selected month, quarter or year it shows boxes 81 and 86 (taxable turnover at 19 % and 7 %, with the tax base in whole euros), 48 (tax-exempt turnover), 66 (input VAT) and 83 (amount payable or refund). Bookings with a tax rate are counted. Input VAT only counts for expenses outside the non-profit area, proportionally for mixed use; a cancellation reduces the cancelled booking. Sub-units that belong to the club for tax purposes are included.

## In detail

Under **Settings** you choose whether **The club is liable for VAT (not a small business)**, the **Advance return period** and whether input VAT is calculated as a flat rate under § 23a UStG: 7 % of taxable turnover instead of input VAT from invoices (box 63), only if this turnover did not exceed €50,000 in the previous year; the choice is binding for five years. HUBIplan does not transmit anything to ELSTER. In the DATEV export, bookings with a tax rate get the matching tax key.
