Set your task sharing notices and email and push reminders here. These defaults apply to your account across all units. Each task with a due date can have its own channels and reminder times. Due tasks remain highlighted even when messages are disabled.

You can hide task titles in push messages. You can also choose to move completed private one-off tasks to the trash after twelve months. This retention option starts disabled; recurring task history is retained.
