Your membership card with QR code. Show it on your phone or print it. If you lose your phone, the club can block the card and create a new one.

If your operator has configured Wallet and your club has switched it on, you can add
the card to Google Wallet or download an Apple Wallet pass (`.pkpass`). The pass shows
name, club, membership type, department and umbrella association in the club's colours
and with its logo, in German or English depending on the phone's language. Google
receives these details, but no photo. Apple passes do not update automatically; download them again
after changes. Scan the QR code to check current validity online. If the club creates
a new QR code, add the new pass to your wallet.

If you are the contact person of an organisation, the card shows the organisation with its logo instead of a photo, and the check page lists it as an organisation member. The card does not show who presents it.
