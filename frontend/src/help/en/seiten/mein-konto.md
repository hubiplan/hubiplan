This is where you set everything that only concerns you: your name, the **Display language** and
whether HUBIplan shows you learning aids. The settings apply in every unit you work in.

Open the other sections through **My account** at the top right, for example **Sign-in & security**
for your password and two-factor authentication. The same menu shows your email address and lets you **Sign out**.
