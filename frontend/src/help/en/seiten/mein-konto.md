This is where you set everything that only concerns you: your name, the **Display language** and
whether HUBIplan shows you learning aids. The settings apply in every unit you work in.

Under **Appearance** you choose **Same as device**, **Light** or **Dark**. In dark mode, HUBIplan shows sign-in, the start page, events, My account and My area in dark colours; administration (such as members or finances) stays light. With **Same as device**, HUBIplan follows the setting of your phone or computer. The choice applies on all devices where you are signed in. In the dark, HUBIplan adjusts the club colours so that links and buttons stay easy to read.

Open the other sections through **My account** at the top right, for example **Sign-in & security**
for your password and two-factor authentication. The same menu shows your email address and lets you **Sign out**.
