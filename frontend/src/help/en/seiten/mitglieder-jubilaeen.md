At the top you choose the **Year**. Anniversaries have no exact date, they count for the whole calendar year; a date is only shown if the club counts to the day or month. Anniversaries of units such as squads, based on their founding year, are in a separate table above.

The list shows who has a membership anniversary or a milestone birthday in a given year, for example to prepare honours at the general meeting. The list can be printed and exported.
