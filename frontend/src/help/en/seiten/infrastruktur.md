Equipment & rooms › **Rooms and facilities** (Facilities module) lists the fixed premises of your active unit, for example a shooting range,
a clubhouse or a club hall. Each facility has its own number (INF-…), a type, a short
description and an address.

With editing permission, choose **Create facility**. Optionally enter the capacity,
ownership and use, and an **Access note** such as where the key box is. Only people with
viewing permission for facilities can see the access note.

For the type **Shooting range**, enter the number of lanes per discipline under
**Shooting lanes**, for example ten 10 m air rifle lanes. In the shooting facility settings
the club can choose **Take from facilities**. Target selection in competitions then
recommends targets that suit these lanes.

Add parts of a facility on its detail page with **Create area**, for example a hall, kitchen
or individual ranges. An area uses the address of its facility; areas cannot contain further
areas.

The detail page also shows **Equipment stored here** and **Associated equipment**. Equipment
is stored here when its location is **In a facility**. Associated equipment is linked under
**Belongs to** on the equipment item, for example a key kept by a member. Both are only
visible with equipment permission.

Photo, tasks, documents such as house rules or a lease, event links, history and the recycle
bin work as for equipment. A retired facility is hidden and can no longer be chosen as a
location.
