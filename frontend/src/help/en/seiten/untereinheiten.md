This lists the sub-units of your unit, which can be nested freely. Each sub-unit can have its own roles, its own cash book, events and a website. With “Assign head” a person from the sub-unit gets an account with all permissions for their area.

Unit settings and roles have separate rights. Settings “read” shows administrative data; role “read” shows roles. Without settings read access, a limited sub-unit selection remains available for this purpose. Changing and assigning roles requires role “edit”; “Assign head” additionally requires settings edit access.

“Who has which role?” lists the current holders per role. With **Assign** you give a role to a member, without an account and without email; the predecessor’s term can end in the same step. Earlier terms are under Members › Offices with the Unit filter.
