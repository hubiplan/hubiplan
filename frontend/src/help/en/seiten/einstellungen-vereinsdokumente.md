Here you store the constitution, fee regulations and founding deed, each with versions and dates. If you wish, the valid version appears on the website under “Documents”; older versions remain available for reference.

If you can see the document library, the club documents are under [Documents](/dokumente) at the top of the library; this settings item only appears without access to the library.
