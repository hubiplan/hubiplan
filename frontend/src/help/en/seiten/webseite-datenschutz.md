Every website needs a privacy notice. Here you set where it comes from.

Under **Privacy notice** you choose **own text** or **link to own page**; then enter the **Address of the privacy notice**. For your own text, **Create text draft** writes a draft that matches the areas switched on for the website. **Recreate draft** replaces the existing text; save your own additions first.

The draft is a template and not legal advice. Read it through and add whatever is different for you.
