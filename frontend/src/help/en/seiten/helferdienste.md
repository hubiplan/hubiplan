Under **Volunteer duties** you plan who takes on which duty at a festival or event, such as the
bar, cash desk, setup or teardown. The overview shows each duty roster with its open places.

New duty roster: Choose **New duty roster**, enter a title and link the roster to an event if
needed.
From an event in the calendar, **Create duty roster for this event** leads straight here with
the event already selected. In the roster, the event name leads back to the calendar.

Duties and shifts: Use **Add duty** to create a duty with its place and, if needed, a minimum
age (such as serving alcohol from 18). Below it you create the shifts: day, times and people per
shift. Several shifts can be created at once, such as “14-18, 18-22, 22-02”; a shift past
midnight ends the next day.

Assigning: **Assign** on a shift adds a member or an external person with name and phone. If the
shift is already full, overlaps with another shift of the same person, or the person is younger
than the minimum age, HUBIplan asks first; **Assign anyway** goes ahead regardless. Phone numbers
are only visible to those who may edit duty rosters.

Printing: **Notice as PDF** creates one page per duty with the shifts and empty lines for
signing up. **List as Excel** contains all shifts with the people assigned.

Status: **Publish** marks the roster as final, **Close** ends it; after that it cannot be changed
until you reopen it. Only a published roster appears in the member area.

Roster settings: Under **Roster settings** you choose whether members sign up themselves or only
the administration assigns people, until how many days before a shift members can cancel
themselves, the maximum number of shifts per person and how many days before a shift HUBIplan
sends a reminder email (empty: no reminder). You also choose whether people who signed up see
who else is on their shift, and you can write a note for the helpers. After the cancellation
deadline, only the administration can cancel a shift.

Units: If a role of a unit (such as the platoon leadership) has the right, it plans its own
duties and only assigns members of that unit.

Quotas: In a club roster, **Quota** on a shift gives part of the places to a unit, for example
“the rifle platoon provides 4 people for the bar”. The unit's leadership sees its places in its
volunteer duties under **Quotas from the club** and assigns its own members there. Places of a
quota that are still open count as taken in the member area. Setting 0 places removes a quota;
people already assigned stay on the shift.

Copy roster: **Copy roster** creates a new roster in preparation, for example for next year's
festival. The copy takes over duties, shifts, quotas and settings, but no assignments. If you
enter the first day of the new roster, HUBIplan moves all shifts by the same distance.

Call-out: If a published roster still has free places, **Call-out as circular** creates a draft
to all members of the unit with the free shifts and the link to sign up. You check and send it
like any circular. You also need the right to send circulars.

Completed duties: Once a shift has started, tick **done** for each person. **Completed duties**
in the overview shows per year who took on how many shifts and hours, for example for a thank
you or required hours.
