Under **Volunteer duties** you plan who takes on which duty at a festival or event, such as the
bar, cash desk, setup or teardown. The overview shows each duty roster with its open places.

New duty roster: Choose **New duty roster**, enter a title and link the roster to an event if
needed.

Duties and shifts: Use **Add duty** to create a duty with its place and, if needed, a minimum
age (such as serving alcohol from 18). Below it you create the shifts: day, times and people per
shift. Several shifts can be created at once, such as “14-18, 18-22, 22-02”; a shift past
midnight ends the next day.

Assigning: **Assign** on a shift adds a member or an external person with name and phone. If the
shift is already full, overlaps with another shift of the same person, or the person is younger
than the minimum age, HUBIplan asks first; **Assign anyway** goes ahead regardless. Phone numbers
are only visible to those who may edit duty rosters.

Printing: **Notice as PDF** creates one page per duty with the shifts and empty lines for
signing up. **List as Excel** contains all shifts with the people assigned.

Status: **Publish** marks the roster as final, **Close** ends it; after that it cannot be changed
until you reopen it. Self sign-up by members in the member area follows in the next stage.

Units: If a role of a unit (such as the platoon leadership) has the right, it plans its own
duties and only assigns members of that unit.
