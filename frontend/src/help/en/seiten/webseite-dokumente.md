Here you upload files for the website, as PDF or image (JPEG, PNG, WebP). Each file belongs to a section:

- Press: press clippings, for example newspaper articles with source and date. They appear in the “Press” section, newest first.
- Documents: further files. They appear in the “Documents” section next to the statutes, fee regulations and founding deed.

You switch both sections on under “Menu and building blocks”. Images are rewritten when uploaded, which removes camera and location data. PDFs stay unchanged and get a preview image of the first page. With “Show on the website” you take a file off the website temporarily without deleting it.

Newspaper articles are protected by copyright. Only show them if the newspaper has agreed.
