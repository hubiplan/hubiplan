The overview shows all setup pages of the enabled modules in one place, grouped by area: club, members, events, finance and, if enabled, competitions and music. The pages themselves stay in their area; each row leads there.

On the right of each row you see the status: “4 created”, “filled in”, “Details missing” or “optional”. Required items that are still missing are marked in red, such as the address under Organisation, membership types, fee rates or reminder levels. At the top you see how many required items are done. While one is missing, the home page shows an entry under **To do** that leads here; missing details in the legal notice appear there under **Notices**.

Use **Search setup** to find an item without knowing which area it belongs to.

You only see items whose page you may open. The overview itself needs “View settings”.
