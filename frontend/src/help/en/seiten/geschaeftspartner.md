This lists all companies, authorities, associations and people outside the member list that you deal with: suppliers, service providers, sponsors, landlords, public offices or the press.

- **Create business partner** asks for the type (company, authority/office, association/federation, private person, other), address, contact and tax details. Only the name is required. Each entry gets a running number such as “GP-0001”.
- Under **Roles** you tick what the partner is for you, several if needed: for example supplier and sponsor. Customer and supplier also apply to invoices and incoming invoices.
- **Add contact** stores people at the partner with function, email and phone. A **Primary contact** is shown first in the list.
- Under **Contact with the club** you record when and how the first contact came about (**First contact on**, **First contact**) and which member looks after the partner (**Looked after by**).
- The **Treasury** section (bank account, **Default category**, payment term, artists' social security levy) only appears with cash or fee rights. Below it you see when the bank account was last changed and by whom.
- You can search the list and filter it by type and role. **Show inactive** brings back partners you no longer need.
- **Export to Excel** puts all details into a spreadsheet, with a second sheet “Contact person”. Bank account, payment term and default category are only included if you have cash or fee rights.
- **Import from Excel** reads such a spreadsheet back in. The easiest way is to export, edit in Excel and import the file. A known number updates the partner; empty cells change nothing. Rows without a number are created, unless the name already exists. HUBIplan finds contact persons by partner number, name and first name. **Check file** shows beforehand what will be added and what will change. “Looked after by” is matched most reliably through the column with the member number.
- **Serial letter and mailing list** chooses a group by role and type, for example all sponsors. **Copy e-mail addresses** fetches the addresses of the contact persons who receive mail (otherwise the partner's e-mail) for your mail program. **Create serial letter** writes one letter per partner with an address as a PDF on your letterhead.
- **Merge duplicate entry** takes over a second entry of the same company: invoices, bookings, agreements, performances and contact persons move along.
- **Turnover in the treasury** shows income and expenses with the partner per year and the latest bookings. Cancelled bookings do not count.
- The card “Suggestions from the cash book” lists names that appear several times as payee or payer in the cash book. **Apply** creates a business partner from one and links the bookings with exactly this name. This needs the right to edit the cash book.
- **Add agreement** records sponsorship and other arrangements: period, amount or in-kind contribution, what you give in return (such as a logo on the website, a perimeter board, a commemorative booklet) and the tax classification. “Mention only” without advertising belongs to the non-profit area or asset management, active advertising to the commercial operations. With an end date, HUBIplan creates a task that reminds you of the renewal in good time (Tasks module); you set how many days before on the agreement.
- **Thank-you letter** creates a letter on your letterhead as a PDF, to the partner's address and the first contact person who receives mail. You can replace the suggestion with amount and in-kind contribution by your own text.
- For a **Contract** (type of agreement) you enter the **Notice period in days** and whether it renews automatically. The task then reminds you in good time before the last day to give notice, and the list shows “give notice by …”.
- Under **Documents** you keep contracts, offers, consents or correspondence on the record. The files count towards the storage of the document library and are checked for viruses as there.
- Under **Website** you record whether the partner may be named on your website as a partner or sponsor. Only these partners are taken over by the website section “Partners and sponsors” with **Take over from business partners**.
- **Show at the top of the main menu** puts business partners as a separate item at the very top of the menu instead of under Club, for example if you often work with suppliers and sponsors. The switch applies to everyone in this unit; anyone who may edit business partners can change it.
- You can only delete a partner as long as it is not used anywhere. Otherwise untick **Active**.
- If a partner is also a member, for example a company as a supporting member, please do not create it twice. Only store private persons as far as you need them for a contract.
