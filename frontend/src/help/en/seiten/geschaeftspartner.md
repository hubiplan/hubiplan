This lists all companies, authorities, associations and people outside the member list that you deal with: suppliers, service providers, sponsors, landlords, public offices or the press.

- **Create business partner** asks for the type (company, authority/office, association/federation, private person, other), address, contact and tax details. Only the name is required. Each entry gets a running number such as “GP-0001”.
- Under **Roles** you tick what the partner is for you, several if needed: for example supplier and sponsor. Customer and supplier also apply to invoices and incoming invoices.
- **Add contact person** stores people at the partner with function, email and phone. A **Primary contact** is shown first in the list.
- The **Treasury** section (bank account, **Default category**, payment term, artists' social security levy) only appears with cash or fee rights. Below it you see when the bank account was last changed and by whom.
- You can search the list and filter it by type and role. **Show inactive** brings back partners you no longer need.
- **Export to Excel** and **Import from Excel** exchange the list with a spreadsheet. **Check file** shows what would be added before importing. Partners whose name already exists are skipped.
- **Merge duplicate entry** takes over a second entry of the same company: invoices, performances and contact persons move along.
- You can only delete a partner as long as it is not used anywhere. Otherwise untick **Active**.
- If a partner is also a member, for example a company as a supporting member, please do not create it twice. Only store private persons as far as you need them for a contract.
