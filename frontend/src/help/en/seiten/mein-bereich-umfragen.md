Here are the surveys of your club and your subunits that ask you, open ones first. The overview
of the member area shows how many are still waiting for your answer.

Answer the questions and choose **Submit**. You can leave optional questions empty. In an open
survey you change your answer until it closes with **Change answer** or take it back with
**Withdraw answer**. An anonymous survey says so beforehand: nobody sees what you answered, not
even the administrators, and the answer cannot be changed afterwards.

Depending on the settings you see the result after answering, always or only after closing. If an
event was created from a date finder and you said “yes”, **Accept the event** accepts it for you.

With the personal link from the email you can also answer without signing in.
