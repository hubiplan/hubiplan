The tabs follow the order of work: **Personal data**, **Membership**, **Fees**, **Roles and account**, **Competitions**, **Music**, **Photo & website**, **Additional fields** and **History**. Tabs for modules that are switched off are hidden. **Photo & website** brings together the photo, photo requests, publication and the membership card. **Music** shows a short musician profile with the instruments; edit it via **Open in music area**. Issued equipment is listed under **Membership**. On a phone, choose the section from the **Area** list. The header shows the name, photo (otherwise the initials), status, membership number, membership type, units and current offices. **Call** and **Email** appear when a phone number or email address is recorded. **More actions** holds request photo, print membership card, record leaving, and access report and deletion.

![Member record with header, “At a glance” and tabs](bild:mitgliedsakte)

At the top, **At a glance** shows up to four tiles: **Member since** (with duration and next jubilee), **Fees** (open invoices and total, marked red when overdue), **Account** (member area, app, two-factor, last sign-in) and **Equipment** (items issued). A tile only appears if you may see the matching tab and there is something to show; clicking it opens the tab.

Next to gender is the **Title**: choose Dr., Dr. Dr., Prof. or Prof. Dr., or enter your own with **other title …** (e.g. Dipl.-Ing.). The title appears in the record header, in the addresses of letters and invoices (“Dr. Max Muster”) and in the formal salutation of circulars (“Sehr geehrter Herr Dr. Muster”); the informal salutation stays without a title. On the website it only appears if “Show titles before names” is switched on there. HUBIplan copies an additional field “Titel” into the fixed field once; the additional field remains.

For an organisation as a member (company, association, public body …), the master data show the name addition, legal form, register, VAT ID, website and size (e.g. employees or inhabitants) for fees by size instead of first name, date of birth and gender. In the member list, filter with **organisations only** or **persons only**.

In the **Contact persons** section you record who speaks for the organisation. Choose **Select member** if the person is a member themselves; name and contact details then come from their file. Otherwise enter name, function, email address and phone. Exactly one is the **Primary contact**. The settings **receives circulars**, **receives invoices**, **represents at meetings** define who is responsible for what. You set up a separate member area login for each contact person on the **Roles and account** tab; contacts who have one are marked **Member access**. If the contact person is a member themselves, they choose after signing in whether they act for themselves or for the organisation. Instead of a photo, an organisation has a **Logo**; it needs no consent and also appears on the membership card. When you remove a contact person, their contact details are deleted immediately.

If the person belongs to a **household** or a **company**, the Master data tab shows a section with its name, their role and the other members. You assign and remove them under [Members › Households and companies](/mitglieder/haushalte).

The **Fees** tab lists the member’s invoices with the outstanding amount and, if SEPA is switched on, their mandates (with the right to view fees and invoices). Open an invoice by its number.

In the **Fees** tab, under **Billing address**, you set where invoices and reminders go if not to the member's address, e.g. a company's accounts department, a town's treasury or a child's parents. It also holds the billing email, purchase order number and Leitweg-ID; the last two are printed on every invoice. Without a separate billing address, an invoice to an organisation is marked “attn.” of the contact person marked “receives invoices”, and the email goes to them.

The record shows everything about a person: master data, memberships with periods and membership types, honours, offices, contact details, SEPA mandate and photo. Changes are saved shortly after you type; you can undo them at the top right if needed. You record leaving as the end of the membership; the record is kept. Honours are in the **Membership** tab. In the **Roles and account** tab you see the person's roles and enable or block the member area. **Assign role** opens the same dialog as under Members › Offices.

In the **Membership** tab, use **Edit** next to a unit membership to correct its start and end dates, including past memberships. A blank end date means the membership continues. Apply the correction with **Save**. Overlaps with other unit memberships are checked. To add a past membership in one step, pick the unit, enter “from” and “until”, then click **Add**. Without “until”, the membership runs until today. Units that are no longer active are listed too, marked “(inactive)”; for them “until” is required. If HUBIplan reports an overlap, it names the affected membership with its exact dates; the check works by day even when the file shows only years.

Under **Privacy** you can download the access report as a ZIP. For former members, this is also where you delete their data on request. As after the deletion period, only the name, year of birth and membership periods then remain, for the club's history. If the person objects to that too, “Remove name from chronicle and honours” then removes their name from dignities, companions, honours and competition results. Photos of these dignities are deleted as well.
