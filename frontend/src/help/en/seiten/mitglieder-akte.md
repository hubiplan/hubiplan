The record shows everything about a person: master data, memberships with periods and membership types, honours, offices, contact details, SEPA mandate and photo. Changes are saved shortly after you type; you can undo them at the top right if needed. You record leaving as the end of the membership; the record is kept.

Under **Data protection** you can download the access report as a ZIP. For former members, this is also where you delete their data on request. As after the deletion period, only the name, year of birth and membership periods then remain, for the club's history. If the person objects to that too, “Remove name from chronicle and honours” then removes their name from dignities, companions, honours and competition results. Photos of these dignities are deleted as well.
