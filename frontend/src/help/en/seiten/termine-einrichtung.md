Here you set how the unit's calendar works. The items are tabs next to each other.

## Categories

Categories organise events, such as “Training”, “Festival” or “Meeting”. Each category has a colour and an abbreviation by which you recognise it in the calendar. A category that still has events cannot be deleted.

## External calendars

Shows events from other calendars in your own, for example those of a neighbouring club. There is a separate page help for this.

## Sharing

For each category you choose whether its events also appear elsewhere: **Pass on upwards (in the calendar of the higher unit)** or **Pass on downwards (to the sub-units and their members)**. This way the summer party also appears in every sub-unit's calendar, but the board meeting does not.

## Public link

With **Public events retrievable via a link** there is an address through which other programs read your public events, for example another website. **Create new link** invalidates the old one.
