Under Entries you register teams, people and details with an organiser, for example for a tournament or a parade. You can send the entry as a letter, email or spreadsheet, report changes later and pass a call for entries on to your own sub-units.

When creating a call for entries, your input is saved only when you choose **Create**. If you cancel or leave after changing something, HUBIplan asks for confirmation. **Continue editing** retains your input; **Discard** leaves the draft without saving.

If results from a linked competition cannot be loaded, **Reload** retries the request. When no competition is linked to a call for entries, the results section remains hidden.
