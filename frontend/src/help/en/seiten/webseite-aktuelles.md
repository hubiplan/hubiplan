Here you write your own posts for “News” on the website, for example a report on the summer party or a thank-you to sponsors. The website shows them by date together with the posts from Instagram and Facebook; older posts are in the archive (“Older posts”).

New post: enter a title and a date and choose **Create**. The date may lie far in the past, for example for old posts of a former website; the order follows the date.

## In detail

Text: you format it as on text pages with the buttons above the text, including links and images in the text. The overview on the website shows a short excerpt; the full post has its own page under aktuelles/… (the **Address** is made from the title and can be changed).

Images: under **Pictures (optional)** you choose up to 20 images from the image library; they appear below the text, the first one also in the overview.

Videos: under **Videos (optional)** you enter the address of a video, for example on YouTube, with a title and a preview image. The website shows the preview image with a link to the video and embeds nothing, so visitors have no contact with other servers.

Sub-units: with **Belongs to**, a post appears on the page of a sub-unit instead of under “News” of the whole unit.

Draft and publishing: changes are saved automatically in the draft; the website preview already shows it. Visitors see the post only after **Publish**, later changes only after publishing again. **Withdraw publication** takes the post off the website without deleting it. A connected AI assistant only creates posts as drafts.

“News” must be switched on under Menu and pages.
