Create personal tasks with a title and an optional description and due date. Personal tasks are private. After creation, edits are saved automatically. Completed tasks appear under Done. Deleted tasks can be restored from the trash for 30 days. Export personal tasks downloads your private tasks.

Shared tasks are also visible to selected people or current role holders. View permission allows reading; edit permission also allows changes and completion. Only the responsible account can delete or transfer responsibility. In your profile, disable email and push notices when tasks are shared. Authorised role managers can take over orphaned shared tasks when the responsible account permanently loses access; personal tasks remain hidden. The outbox stores only a neutral delivery record for task notices.
