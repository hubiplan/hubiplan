Here you can see which practice evenings and training sessions you are recorded for and how often
you attended this year and in the last twelve months.

## Checking in

When the session leader has opened check-in, scan the QR code on their screen with the HUBIplan
app or your phone camera. You are recorded straight away. The code changes every 30 seconds;
always scan the current one.

## Shooting log

If your club keeps the shooting log, you see your shooting sessions here and the status under
section 14 of the German Weapons Act (WaffG) for acquisition (last twelve months) and possession
(last 24 months, for handguns and long guns).

Report dates outside training, for example at another club or at a competition, under
**Report a shooting date**. They only count once the club has confirmed them. A report that has not been
confirmed yet can be taken back with **Withdraw**. The board issues the confirmation for the application to the
association.
