Here you set the club colours and logo. They appear in HUBIplan, on cards, letters and the website. Sub-units inherit them unless they set their own.

Besides the main and second colour there are up to four further colours under **Further colours**, six in total. Each colour gets a name and, if you like, a use, such as “lead colour”, “body text” or “surfaces, table rows”. This lets you take over the colour sheet of a corporate design. Under **Gradients** you create up to two named gradients from two palette colours, for example a blue gradient from blue to dark blue, from left to right or from top to bottom. The website uses them for the header, footer, colour bands, decorative lines and sections; where exactly is set under Website › Design and colours. Next to each gradient you see how readable text on it is. Further colours and gradients only apply together with an own main colour.

Below each colour you tick under **Used for** what it is for: **Body text**, **Secondary text**, **Surfaces and table rows** or **Background**. Each role has at most one colour; ticking it for another colour moves it there. The website takes these colours as its default (under Website › Design and colours each role can still be changed per design), and table rows on the website alternate with the surface colour. With **Also use colour roles in HUBIplan** the roles also colour HUBIplan itself, but only in the light view; dark mode keeps its colours. If text on a surface or the background would be less readable than 4.5 : 1, HUBIplan uses its own colour there and the page names the affected role. Sub-units take over the roles together with the main colour. Without ticked roles everything stays as it is.

**View colour sheet** shows all colours with name, hex and RGB value and use, plus the gradients, on one page to pass on or to save as PDF with **Print**.

Below the logo you can upload a second **Logo / coat of arms for dark mode**, for example a version with light lettering or a light outline. It appears when someone uses the dark appearance (sign-in, home page, events, My account and My area). The preview shows it on a dark background. Without a separate dark mode logo, the normal logo is used in the dark as well. Cards, letters and PDFs always use the normal logo. Sub-units upload their dark coat of arms under Sub-units, with the sub-unit itself.

Links are never underlined. So that they are still easy to recognise, HUBIplan uses a shade of your club colour for links that stands out clearly from the background and from the surrounding text. For very dark colours this shade is a little lighter, for very light colours a little darker. The preview shows the shade used and both values. In headings and bold text, a link also has a “›” after it.

At platform level you set the appearance of the whole instance: its colours and logo then apply to the sign-in page, the public pages and all units without their own colours or logo. This lets every HUBIplan installation have its own look. Club colours always take precedence. For the platform, too, HUBIplan checks that text on the colours is easy to read.

Below that, the platform administration sets the **Instance icon**. It appears in the browser tab and on the phone home screen when someone adds HUBIplan there. A square image without text of at least 512 × 512 pixels works best; HUBIplan creates all required sizes from it. Without a custom icon, the HUBIplan H mark remains. Phones only show a new icon once they fetch it again; on an iPhone the page has to be added to the home screen again.
