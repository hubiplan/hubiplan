A fee run creates the fee invoices for all members for a period according to the fee rates. Before the run, a preview shows totals and a checklist; the invoices are only created when you confirm.

Good to know: an honour with a fixed fee never makes the fee higher; if the normal fee is lower, it applies. If a member who does not pay themselves belongs to several sub-units, the invoice goes to the one with the earliest start; the checklist shows how many. With monthly instalments HUBIplan puts remaining cents on the first instalments, so the total can differ by a few cents from a pro-rata annual invoice. A run can only be deleted while it contains drafts only; finalised invoices are also protected against deletion in the database.
