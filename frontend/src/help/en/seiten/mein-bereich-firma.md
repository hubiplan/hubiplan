Contact persons of a company that is a club member and pays the fee for employees see this item. You only see who belongs to the company, since when, and whether the fee is paid via the company. Address, date of birth, events and offices stay with the club.

**Report employee:** enter first name, last name and email address and choose **Report**. The person receives an email with the link to the club's online application, completes their details and confirms the application themselves. The link is valid for 30 days. Once the club admits them, they belong to the company with “Fee via the household”. If the club has not enabled an online application, report employees directly to the club.

**Deregister:** choose **End fee payment** for a person and enter the last day the company pays for. This is not a resignation: the membership continues, afterwards the member pays themselves. The club and the member receive an email.
