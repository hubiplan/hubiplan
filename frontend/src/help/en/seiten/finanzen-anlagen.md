The **Fixed assets** item under “Book” is the fixed asset register: items from €250 net that are used for several years, such as projectors, refrigerated trailers or furniture. With **Add fixed asset** you enter the date of acquisition, cost, tax area and type of depreciation: straight-line over the useful life (by month), at once as a low-value asset (up to €800 net), as a pool over five years (€250 to €1,000 net) or none. The register shows depreciation and book value for the selected financial year, also as Excel. Record a sale or scrapping as a disposal.

Depreciation only appears here and under **Taxes**, never in the cash book. There it replaces the purchase expense in the result, so that an expensive purchase in commercial operations does not show up as a loss.
