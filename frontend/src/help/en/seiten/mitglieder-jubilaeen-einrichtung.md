Here you set which anniversaries HUBIplan shows (for example 25, 40 or 50 years) and how the length of membership is counted, for example whether periods in other units count.

If a member's entry into the umbrella association was entered manually (for periods before HUBIplan), the duration in the umbrella association counts from that day; up to the first reported period the person counts as a member throughout.
