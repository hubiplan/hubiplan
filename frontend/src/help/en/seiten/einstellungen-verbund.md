In the network you connect your HUBIplan instance with that of an umbrella association or partner club to exchange member reports and invoices. You explicitly release what is transferred.

Peering shows the persistent reception reservation for each connection. Replaced states also count. Additional aggregate limits cover the partner, receiving unit and instance. Exhausted budgets reject data before storage; an administrator must check and approve capacity. Retained financial records are never deleted to recover quota.
