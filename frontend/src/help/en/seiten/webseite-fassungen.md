Under **Versions** you find everything that concerns the whole design.

Under **Earlier versions of the design** you find the last five published designs (design, colour assignment, fine-tuning, area display, header images, home page and menu). **Restore** turns one of them into a draft; it only goes live with **Publish**. If nothing has been published yet, a notice appears here.

Under **Texts and text pages** you find the most recently published texts (greeting, text pages, texts of sub-units) with time and person. Each text has its own versions: **Open** takes you to the text under **Content**, where you restore an earlier version. If you only published text pages, you find the change here and not under the design.

## In detail

The website settings, images, design packages and the unit's colours and logo have no versions. They take effect immediately; what was changed is shown in the **Log**.

Under **Design as a file**, **Save design** saves the design, colour assignment, **Fine-tuning** and **Area display** as a small file. For another unit or on another server, **Load design** loads it into the draft again; it is checked like a change made by hand. If the design does not exist there, the other values still apply. Coat of arms, texts, images and the home page are not included; the colours follow the palette of the unit that loads the file. Unlike a design package the file contains no CSS; anyone who manages the website may load it.
