The evaluation shows the treasury in the selected financial year. **Accounts** lists the opening balance, income, expenses, transfers and closing balance per account, with the total below. **By category** and **By tax area** break income and expenses down and show the balance.

**Treasurer's report (PDF)** creates the report for the general meeting. What the club owns and owes at the end of the year is shown on the **Net assets** tab.
