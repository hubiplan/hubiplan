Under **Surveys** you quickly ask members something: find a date, ask for meal choices for the
festive dinner, who is taking the bus, or get an opinion poll. Surveys are polls, not
resolutions; formal votes belong in meetings.

New survey: **New survey** offers templates: **Find a date**, **Meal choice**,
**Who is coming along?**, **Opinion poll (yes/no/abstain)** and **Free survey**. A survey has up
to 10 questions. Question types are single and multiple choice, yes / no / abstain, date finder
(yes, no and optionally maybe per option), quantities (such as 2× schnitzel, 1× vegetarian) and
free text up to 500 characters. Answers can have a number of places; once it is reached, nobody
can choose that answer any more.

Who is asked: all members of the club or subunit, limited by subunits, membership types or the
board only, or selected people. A survey for an event asks everyone invited or only those who
accepted. For a meal choice at an event, guests count too: anyone coming with one guest chooses
up to two meals. If someone declines the event, their answer is removed from the survey.
**How many are asked?** shows the number before starting.

Names and result: **open** (everyone asked sees names), **only the administrators see names** or
**anonymous**. Anonymous means nobody sees who answered what, not even the administrators.
HUBIplan only stores that someone took part, separately from the answer. Anonymous answers cannot
be changed, and the result only appears from three answers on. After starting, settings can only
become stricter, never more open. People asked see the result after answering, always, only after
closing or not at all (administrators only).

Start and close: **Start** notifies everyone asked, by app or by email with a personal link they
can use to answer without signing in. Questions and audience can no longer be changed afterwards;
**Change closing time** still works. Optionally HUBIplan reminds everyone who has not answered a
few days before closing. The survey closes by itself at the end; **Close now** closes it at once.

Results: The survey shows bars per answer, with names for open surveys, and who has not answered
yet. For a date finder, the best date is highlighted; the button “Create event on …” turns it
into an event with RSVPs. Everyone who said “yes” accepts it there with one tap. **PDF** and
**Excel** contain the result, for example as an order list for the kitchen.
**Enter for a member** records an answer given by phone or on paper (not for anonymous surveys).

Retention: HUBIplan deletes answers with names the set number of months after closing (default
12). The totals remain. **Copy** creates the survey again for next year.

Subunits: If a subunit role has the right to do so, it asks its own members.
