In Tasks › **Personal music reminders**, enable private tasks for your own sheet music returns and equipment returns and maintenance. Choose zero to 30 days ahead; overdue records are included. Your choice saves itself; tasks are reconciled then and hourly. Equipment still requires inventory read access; department equipment is excluded. Enabling requires task read and edit permission. You cannot select another recipient. Each task links directly to its source record. Notifications use your existing task settings. Unchanged tasks are completed after a recorded return or changed/completed maintenance. Your edits, completions and deletions are preserved; only a new due date creates another task. Disabling completes unchanged automatically created tasks. A review task does not itself record return or maintenance.
