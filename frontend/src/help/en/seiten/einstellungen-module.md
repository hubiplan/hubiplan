Here you switch on yourselves what your club uses. A switched-off module disappears from the menu; its data and the rights of roles are kept and come back when you switch it on again. Public parts go offline in the meantime.

You can only choose from the modules the platform administration has released; others do not appear here at all. A switched-on module the platform no longer releases stays visible, marked “not released by the platform”, so you can switch it off. If a module needs another one, that one is switched on as well; if you switch off a requirement, the dependent modules go too. Before every change a confirmation shows what changes.

Below a switched-on module you find its add-on features, such as the cash count in the cash book, SEPA direct debit for fees and invoices, or reserves for tax & charitable status. They are the same switches as in the module’s setup, which the link below them opens.

Anyone with the right to edit settings may change this.
