If you paid for something for the club yourself, claim it here and get exactly that amount refunded. Choose **Claim an expense**, write what you bought and for whom, enter date and amount and add the receipt: with **Take photo** straight from your phone's camera or with **Choose file** as an image, PDF or e-invoice.

For the payout choose **Bank transfer** (account holder and IBAN, prefilled from your direct debit mandate) or **cash**. **Submit** sends the claim to the treasurer and gives it a number; **Save as draft** lets you send it later.

If the treasurer has a query, it appears here and on the overview. Answer it, add receipts or change the details; the treasurer then reviews it again. You are notified about approval, rejection and payout by push in the app or by email. Once submitted, receipts can only be added, not deleted.

Depending on the club's settings you can also claim **Travel costs** by kilometres or request an **Advance** before buying. If the club has set a basis for expense donations, you can choose **Waive as a donation** for an approved claim and get a donation receipt instead.
