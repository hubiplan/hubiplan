Here you see, for all current members, the decisive certificate per qualification, i.e. the one
valid the longest, with its expiry date and state (**valid**, **expires soon** or **expired**).
Whatever expires first is at the top.

With **Status** › **expired or expiring soon** only the certificates you need to take care of
remain; **Qualification** shows a single type only. The name leads to the member file, where you
renew the certificate.

“Soon” means: within the days set as reminder for the type. Expired and soon-expiring
certificates are also listed on the home page under “To do”.
