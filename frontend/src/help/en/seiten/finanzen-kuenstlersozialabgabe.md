The **Artists' social security levy** tab under taxes (Tax & charitable status module) adds up the fees paid to self-employed artists and writers per calendar year, e.g. for a band, choir director, DJ or graphic designer. It counts expenses ticked **Fee paid to artists (artists' social security levy)** in the cash book and all expenses of a category marked as **Artist fees** under Set up › Categories; cancellations do not count. The page shows the total per recipient, the levy at that year's rate (2026: 4.9 %) and the deadline for reporting to the Künstlersozialkasse (31 March of the following year), also as Excel.
