The home page shows the most important things about the selected unit at a glance: open tasks, upcoming events, birthdays and anniversaries. What you see depends on your rights. At the top right you switch units if you work in more than one.

The navigation on the left starts with **Home**, **Tasks** and **Events**. Club activities follow, then organisation and finally administration. Spacing separates the visible groups; **Help** stays separate at the bottom.

At the top, **Help on this page** opens help for the current page; on narrow screens only the “?” sign is shown. **Help** in the sidebar opens the overview of all guides. **My account** contains your email address, personal settings and **Sign out**.

Buttons have their own opaque background, including subtle actions, disabled buttons and file pickers. Selected views also use a distinct fill. Very light club colours are darkened for action buttons to keep them recognisable; the club logo and the remaining brand colour are preserved.

The **To do** tile comes first and collects tasks, approvals, requests, overdue invoices, open booking requests, held contact form messages, your own circular drafts untouched for a week, meeting invitations that are due, received motions and open minutes, open helper places in the next two weeks, queries and close entry deadlines, received invoices to review, invoice drafts, due reminders and direct debits, and expired account invitations; each entry only appears with the matching permission. Finance › Overview shows the same entries where they concern finance. The count is shown beside its heading. Birthdays and anniversaries show at most five entries. **New task** at the bottom of the tile opens the task dialog. **All tasks** beside it opens the overview. **Upcoming events** uses the same footer: **New event** opens the event dialog when you have permission to create events, and **Calendar** opens the overview. Each row separates its type, title and details; tasks that are due also show a due-date notice. Use the checkbox to complete a task if you have permission. Its title opens the details. Tiles in the same row keep equal heights.

As a member you see the **Upcoming events** card at the top, with the next event highlighted and the following events below it, next to **Show ID card**. Under **My area** the three most important tiles are shown; **Everything in My area** leads to the rest.

If two-factor sign-in is required for your permissions, every page shows the same short notice with **Set up now**; **Learn more** shows which permissions are affected. If the connection drops, the “No connection” band appears straight away. Automatically saved changes are sent as soon as the connection is back; after about ten seconds at the latest the form shows that nothing has been saved yet.
