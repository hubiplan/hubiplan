The home page is your workplace in the selected unit. It has the same order for every role: **To do**, **Quick actions**, key figures, **Upcoming events**, **Anniversaries and birthdays** and **Notices**. What you see depends on your rights; anything you may not do is simply left out. At the top right you switch units if you work in more than one.

The navigation on the left starts with **Home**, **Tasks** and **Events**. Club activities follow, then organisation and finally administration. Spacing separates the visible groups; **Help** stays separate at the bottom.

At the top, **Help on this page** opens help for the current page; on narrow screens only the “?” sign is shown. **Help** in the sidebar opens the overview of all guides. **My account** contains your email address, personal settings and **Sign out**.

Buttons have their own opaque background, including subtle actions, disabled buttons and file pickers. Selected views also use a distinct fill. Very light club colours are darkened for action buttons to keep them recognisable; the club logo and the remaining brand colour are preserved.

The **To do** tile comes first and collects tasks, approvals, requests, overdue invoices, open booking requests, held contact form messages, your own circular drafts untouched for a week, meeting invitations from two weeks before the invitation deadline, received motions and open minutes, open helper places in the next two weeks, queries and close entry deadlines, received invoices to review, invoice drafts, due reminders and direct debits, expired account invitations, bookings without a receipt in the current financial year, bank accounts whose statement for the previous month is still missing from the 5th, and missing required items of the [setup](/einstellungen/einrichtung); each entry only appears with the matching permission. Finance › Overview shows the same entries where they concern finance. The count is shown beside its heading. **New task** at the bottom of the tile opens the task dialog. **All tasks** beside it opens the overview. Each row separates its type, title and details; tasks that are due also show a due-date notice. Use the checkbox to complete a task if you have permission. Its title opens the details.

**Quick actions** are at most four buttons for what your role does most often; the first one is highlighted. The treasurer gets **Record a booking**, **Import bank statement**, **Issue receipt** and **Prepare reminders**, the board **New event**, **Admit member** and **Write a circular**, the secretary **Create a meeting** and **Write minutes**. The treasurer's actions only appear if you hold a treasurer role (a role with finance rights that may not assign roles). So a chair with all rights does not see them, even though they may book. If you have several roles, the treasurer's actions come first, and so do their key figures. In the workspace of a sub-unit you find **Event for the unit**, **View members** and **Letterhead** there. Under **Customise** you hide single actions (the next one moves up) or, with **Hide quick actions completely**, the whole block. You can turn it back on under My account › Profile › Home.

Below are up to three key figures, only with the matching permission: for the treasurer the cash box balance, the account balance per last bank statement and outstanding fees; for the board the number of members and of open data changes; for the secretary the next meeting and the date by which invitations must go out. A click opens the matching page. In **Upcoming events**, **New event** opens the event dialog when you have permission to create events, and **Calendar** opens the overview.

The **Anniversaries and birthdays** card shows the anniversaries of the current year, from October also those of the next year, each under its own heading such as “Anniversaries 2026”. Anniversaries count for a calendar year; a date is only shown if the club counts to the day or month. Below are the birthdays in the next 30 days. Names lead to the member record, **All anniversaries** to the full list.

The **Notices** card collects what is not urgent: missing details in the legal notice and letter template notices (with **Later**). The number next to the heading is the number of these notices. Without a notice there is no card.

As a member the next event is at the very top, on a phone too. If a response is possible, you accept or decline right there with **Accept** or **Decline**; the following events come below. Under **My area** at most four tiles are shown, **Show ID card** first; **Everything in My area** leads to the rest.

![Member home page with the next event at the top](bild:startseite-mitglied)

If two-factor sign-in is required for your permissions, every page shows the same short notice with **Set up now**; **Learn more** shows which permissions are affected. If the connection drops, the “No connection” band appears straight away. Automatically saved changes are sent as soon as the connection is back; after about ten seconds at the latest the form shows that nothing has been saved yet.
