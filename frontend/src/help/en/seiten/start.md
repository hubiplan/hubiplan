The home page shows the most important things about the selected unit at a glance: open tasks, upcoming events, birthdays and anniversaries. What you see depends on your rights. At the top right you switch units if you work in more than one.

Buttons have their own opaque background, including subtle actions, disabled buttons and file pickers. Selected views also use a distinct fill. Very light club colours are darkened for action buttons to keep them recognisable; the club logo and the remaining brand colour are preserved.

The **Open tasks** tile shows the count beside its heading. **New task** opens the task dialog. Each row separates its type, title and details; tasks that are due also show a due-date notice. Use the checkbox to complete a task if you have permission. Its title opens the details. Tiles in the same row keep equal heights.
