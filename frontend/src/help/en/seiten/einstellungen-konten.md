An account is a person’s sign-in. This page lists all accounts with a role in your unit and everyone allowed to use the member area, with roles, last sign-in and two-factor.

Under **Show** you pick a filter: **with permissions**, **never signed in**, **invitation expired** or **no two-factor although required**. Tick accounts and click “Resend sign-in link”. Accounts with a role get a new invitation as long as they have not set a password yet; member area accounts get a new sign-in link. A link sent this way is valid for 7 days, one requested on the sign-in page for one hour; every new link invalidates the older ones, and at most three links per hour go to one address.

Below, **Terms of office** lists all roles per account with their period. There you resend the invitation to a single person, reset two-factor sign-in or change the period. Roles are assigned under Members › Offices.

You only see accounts with roles with role “read”. With the “Member accounts” area you see the member area accounts.
