Under **Areas** › **Area display** you choose how the fixed areas look: the board as cards with photo or a compact list, events as a list by month or a monthly calendar (on a phone only the days with events appear), sub-units as large or small tiles or a list, and the gallery as tiles or masonry with pictures at their own height. A sketch shows each option. Like everything here, your choice goes into the draft first. You write an introduction at the top of an area under Website › Texts. The preview on the right shows the effect.
