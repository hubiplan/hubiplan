For each area, a role sets what its holders may do: **no access**, **read** or **edit**. Edit includes read. A “treasurer”, for example, gets cash book and dues “edit” and members “read”, an auditor only “read” for the cash book. Below are the additional rights to tick: export member lists, full data export and “view as role”. They only work together with the read right of their area.

If someone has several roles, the highest level per area applies. People are given roles, optionally for a limited time, and receive an invitation by email. With the “view as role” right you check in the top bar what a role sees. Anyone who may only read roles sees roles, rights and holders but changes nothing.

Organisation settings and role administration are separate areas. Editing settings is not enough to change roles, assign an effective role to a member or set up a manager. These actions require role “edit”. Existing settings roles retain their previous authority as an explicit role right during the upgrade; new roles receive this right only when deliberately granted.
