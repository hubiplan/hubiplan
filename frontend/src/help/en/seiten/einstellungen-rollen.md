For each area, a role sets what its holders may do: **no access**, **read** or **edit**. Edit includes read. A “treasurer”, for example, gets cash book and dues “edit” and members “read”, an auditor only “read” for the cash book. Below are the additional rights to tick: export member lists, full data export and “view as role”. They only work together with the read right of their area.

If someone has several roles, the highest level per area applies. People are given roles, optionally for a limited time, and receive an invitation by email. Anyone who has not set a password yet is marked “Password not set yet”; **Resend invitation** sends a new link. With the “view as role” right you check in the top bar what a role sees. Anyone who may only read roles sees roles, rights and holders but changes nothing.

Organisation settings and role administration are separate areas. Editing settings is not enough to change roles, assign an effective role to a member or set up a manager. These actions require role “edit”. Existing settings roles are not automatically promoted either: anyone who should continue managing sub-unit roles needs the role right explicitly granted.

Role administration only shows modules and permissions enabled for your club by the platform. When a module is withdrawn, its permissions disappear from the selection and immediately stop granting access. Existing settings are retained, including when you edit other permissions. Once the module is enabled again, they reappear and apply as before. This also applies to sub-unit roles.
