Here you see what the club has stored about you. You send changes, such as a new address, as a notice to the club; they apply once someone there has accepted them.

If your club has switched it on, you can also cancel your membership here. The earliest leaving date follows the notice period in the statutes; the club confirms the leaving date by email.
