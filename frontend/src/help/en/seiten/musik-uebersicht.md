**Performance overview** combines staffing, pending responses, known conflicts, sheet music requirements and permitted settlement issues. The period starts today and covers the next 180 days. For past performances, such as open settlements, choose an earlier start and select **Update overview**. Each issue links to staffing, the programme or the performance settlement. “Prepared” refers only to information you can access; it does not confirm attendance. Missing planning details remain open. Finance issues require cashbook read access. Cancelled performances are marked separately. Each page shows twelve performances.
