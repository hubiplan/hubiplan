**Performance overview** combines staffing, pending responses, known conflicts, sheet music requirements and permitted settlement issues. Select a period and choose **Update overview** when needed. Each issue links to staffing, the programme or the performance settlement. “Prepared” refers only to information you can access; it does not confirm attendance. Missing planning details remain open. Finance issues require cashbook read access. Cancelled performances are marked separately. Each page shows twelve performances.
