Cancellations that members send in the member area arrive here. Each cancellation names the requested leaving date; HUBIplan calculates it from the notice period and the date you set under Members › Setup › Member area & photos. Nothing changes in the record until you confirm the leaving date. The member receives a confirmation of receipt when sending and an email with the result after your decision.
