Membership applications from the online form and from booth mode arrive here. An application only appears once the applicant has confirmed it with the link in the email; HUBIplan deletes unconfirmed applications after 48 hours.

The list shows name, membership type, date received and status for each application. Clicking the number opens the review page with all details, possible matches among existing people, the approvals and the history.

What admission requires is set under [Members › Setup › Online application](/mitglieder/einrichtung/antrag): one person decides, several roles must approve, or the general meeting decides. Whoever is to approve also sees the application under “To do” on the home page.

On admission, HUBIplan creates the member and takes over a granted SEPA mandate, the consents and, for minors, the legal guardians as contact persons. Declined and withdrawn applications are deleted after the configured period.
