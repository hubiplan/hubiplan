Create a musician from an existing member or enter a guest name and contact details. Pick the member via the search: type at least two letters of the name and click a match. Under **Instrumental skills** choose the instrument from the unit's list (Settings › Music › [Instruments](/musik/instrumente)), enter the section or part and mark the main instrument. A musician can have no, one or several instruments. If an instrument is missing, choose **New instrument …**.

Use **Instrument** above the list to show only musicians who play a given instrument; the number after it says how many. The musician card names the instruments, main instrument first.

The member record shows the instruments on its **Music** tab. Anyone allowed to edit music adds them there with **Add instrument**, uses **Make main instrument** or removes one; a missing musician profile is created on the way. **Open in music area** leads here. Deactivate guests when no longer needed.
