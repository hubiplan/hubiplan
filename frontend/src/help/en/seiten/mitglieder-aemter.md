This lists the unit's offices, such as chair, treasurer or secretary, with their holders and terms. Offices are not rights: what someone may do in HUBIplan is set by the roles under Settings.

With **Assign** you give an office to a new person, for example after the annual general meeting. You pick the person from the member list and the date. The dialog says beforehand whether the role has permissions and where an invitation goes. If the office is held, it ends the predecessor’s term on the day before; the box is ticked by default. Afterwards it points to the imprint and letterhead if the role appears there.

At the top, pick the club or a sub-unit, such as a platoon, under **Unit**. Offices of a sub-unit are assigned the same way with **Assign**; no account is created and no email is sent. Current holders are ended there with **End**. The sub-unit’s page and its workspace show the same list under “Who has which role?”.
