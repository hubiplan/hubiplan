Here you write the website texts and put together the blocks of the home page. Changes are saved as you type; the latest versions of each text are kept.

Chronicle as a timeline: The “Chronik” text has a short introduction at the top and the timeline below it (box **Timeline**). Each event has a year or a period (e.g. 1939 to 1948), a heading, at most 400 characters of text and, if you like, up to six pictures, such as posters from several years. On the website they appear small side by side; in the large view you can page through them. Under “Picture position” you choose for each event whether the pictures appear on the left, on the right or large below the text; automatic alternates the side from entry to entry. On a phone they always appear below the text. Chapters (marked **Chapter**) such as “Founding years 1920–1938” are subheadings: below them the website shows all events from their year until the next chapter begins. **Add from Excel** brings a list with the columns Jahr (year), Überschrift (heading) and optionally bis (until), Text and Art (type, “Kapitel” for a chapter) into the draft. With **Newest entries at the top** the website shows the timeline backwards, from the present to the founding; chapters still stay above their events and the jump links follow. Without the tick the order stays from old to new. The timeline is published together with the chronicle.
