Here you write the website texts and put together the blocks of the home page. Changes are saved as you type; the latest versions of each text are kept.

## Text pages from sections

Under **Page sections** you build a text page from sections, like the home page. The existing text with the image at the top is the fixed section “Page text”; without further sections the page stays as before. **Add section** adds, for example, a **Banner**, a **Button bar**, **Tiles**, a **Quote**, **Frequently asked questions**, **Directions** or a **Document to download** above or below it; drag them by the handle into order. **Edit** opens content, background and width, **Apply** saves the section to the draft.

For the “Page text”, **Content width** sets the text at reading width along the content edge, **Text width** as a centred column and **Full width** without a limit; the page title always sits at the content edge. The website preview (Website › Design and colours, or a preview link) also shows the draft of texts and their sections. Everything becomes visible with **Publish**; earlier versions bring back their sections too. A **Cover image** is a narrow image at the very top above the heading; each page has at most one, and it always appears at the top, wherever it is in the list.

## Event pages

An event page is a text page about a public event. You create it at the event under “For this event” › **Website** › **Create**. At the top of the page, date, place, directions, programme, prices, places left, cancellation rule, terms of participation and the registration button appear by themselves, all taken from the event. Here you only write the text and sections below, such as the invitation, sponsors or pictures from last year. The menu item disappears on the day after the end; with **Remove from the website after the event has ended** the page disappears too.

## Sub-unit pages from sections

The text of a sub-unit (platoon, department, band, team) also has **Page sections**. Besides the “Page text” (image, text and picture carousel), it lists the fixed blocks “Contact”, “Own homepage”, “Title holders”, “Upcoming events”, “News (posts)” and “Follow us”. Drag them into a different order or hide them with the tick box. Before and after them you can add your own sections, such as **Text with image**, **Images**, a **Quote**, **Frequently asked questions**, **Opening and training hours**, a **Video as a link** or the **Title holders of a unit**. Without changes the page looks as before.

## Picture slot without a picture

If the photo is not there yet, leave the picture empty in **Text with image** or **Images** and write a **Note for the picture slot (optional)**, e.g. “Rehearsal scene, landscape”. The preview then shows a placeholder with this note. The empty picture slot does not appear on the published page.

## Area introductions

**Introduction for an area** lets you write a short text, with an image if you like, that appears at the top of the board, events, sub-units or gallery, as on the memorial page. The **Contact** introduction appears as a box to the right of the contact form (below it on a phone): the title as its heading, then the text; if the last paragraph holds nothing but a link, for example to your “Join in” page, it becomes a button. Without this introduction the contact page stays in one column. Each area has one introduction; it appears once you choose **Publish**.

## Image captions

For each text, **Show image captions on the website** decides whether the website shows the caption below the image at the top and in the image carousel. Without the tick (the default) the images appear without a caption; the description for screen readers stays.

If someone else or the AI assistant has changed the same page in the meantime, HUBIplan does not save your entry and reports “The page has been changed in the meantime. Please reload.” This also applies to publishing.

## Chronicle as a timeline

The “Chronik” text has a short introduction at the top and the timeline below it (box **Timeline**). Each event has a year or a period (e.g. 1939 to 1948), a heading, at most 400 characters of text and, if you like, up to six pictures, such as posters from several years. On the website they appear small side by side; in the large view you can page through them.

Under “Picture position” you choose for each event whether the pictures appear on the left, on the right or large below the text; automatic alternates the side from entry to entry. On a phone they always appear below the text. Chapters (marked **Chapter**) such as “Founding years 1920–1938” are subheadings: below them the website shows all events from their year until the next chapter begins. **Add from Excel** brings a list with the columns Jahr (year), Überschrift (heading) and optionally bis (until), Text and Art (type, “Kapitel” for a chapter) into the draft.

With **Newest entries at the top** the website shows the timeline backwards, from the present to the founding; chapters still stay above their events and the jump links follow. Without the tick the order stays from old to new. The timeline is published together with the chronicle. The timeline belongs to the “Chronicle” module. When it is switched on, you maintain the introduction and timeline only under its own menu item **Chronicle**, even without rights on the website; here the chronicle then only shows the address, “show in menu” and delete.
