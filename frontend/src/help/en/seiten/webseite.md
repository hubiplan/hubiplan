The overview shows the status of your club website and what is still missing before publishing, such as the legal notice or privacy notice. The website only reads what you release here; names and photos only appear with consent.

Under **Notice bar** a short notice appears at the top of every page, for example “Fair 22–25 Aug: programme”. With **Target** it leads to a page of the website, to an event or to an outside address. **Show from** and **Show until** set the period (both days included); afterwards the bar disappears by itself. Without dates it applies right away and without end; without **Notice bar text** there is none. The notice bar goes online immediately, not with the design draft.
