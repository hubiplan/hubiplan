Here you keep reserves, for example for a new flag or maintenance. Non-profit clubs must generally use funds promptly; reserves are only allowed for certain reasons (§ 62 AO).

At the bottom of the page, **Maximum for the free reserve** works out how much may be added to the free reserve in a financial year: one third of the surplus from asset management plus 10 % of the other funds to be used promptly (income of the non-profit area, surpluses from purpose-related and commercial operations). Any amount not used in the two previous years can be carried over. If additions are higher, a notice appears. The calculation uses the tax areas of the categories; entries without an area count as non-profit income.

If there are assets in the asset register, their depreciation for the year is shown as a guide for a replacement reserve. From 100,000 € income per year, the page points out that the tax office usually expects a statement on the use of funds.
