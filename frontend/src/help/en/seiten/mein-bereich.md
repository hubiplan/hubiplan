This is your area as a member. Here you find your card, your invoices, circulars and the data the club has stored about you. Your club decides which of these appear.

When the music module is enabled, your performances, rehearsals, sheet music and absences also appear here. Individual events are separated; meeting details, uniform and response status are labelled. **Accept** and **Decline** mark your current response. Known availability conflicts are also shown in text.

Under **Circulars**, read received messages with paragraphs, emphasis, lists and links. Attachments are listed by filename with the open circular. A link from the Android app returns to this exact circular and membership access after browser sign-in if needed. **To the overview** closes it; browser Back also works. Retry is available if loading fails.

The information-email setting confirms successful changes. You still receive official notices. If a change fails, the last confirmed setting is retained.
