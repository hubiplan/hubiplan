Here you define which instruments exist in your unit. You choose from this list wherever an instrument is asked for: for musicians, in the required line-up, in instrumentation templates, for sheet music parts and for instruments in the equipment. That way the spelling is the same everywhere and matching musicians are reliably found when assigning. Editing requires music editing permission.

## Preset list

HUBIplan comes with a comprehensive choice for wind bands, grouped into woodwind, brass, percussion, drum and fife corps, and other. Use **Hide** for anything you do not need; it then no longer appears in the choice but stays on existing entries. **Show** brings it back.

## Own instruments and order

Use **Add instrument** to add an instrument and choose its group. Every choice also offers **New instrument …**; a name entered there is added to the list under **Other** when you save. The arrows move an instrument within its group; this is the order in the choice and in the musicians filter.

## Renaming and removing

**Edit** changes name and group. A new name applies everywhere at once, including musicians, required line-up, templates and sheet music. Each instrument shows how often it is used. **Remove** is only offered for instruments that are not used anywhere; hide the others instead.

Upper and lower case and “ph” versus “f” do not matter: “trompete” becomes “Trompete”, “Altsaxophon” becomes “Altsaxofon”.
