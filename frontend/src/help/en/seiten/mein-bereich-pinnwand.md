Here you find short news from your club and your units, pinned posts at the top, otherwise the
newest first. Posts added since your last visit are marked **new**; the overview of the member area
and the app show how many new posts there are. Clicking a photo opens it in full size.

You cannot reply here, the notice board is not a chat. If you have questions, contact the person
named below the post.

If you are allowed to write to the notice board in the administration, you also see **New post**
here and can change, pin and delete posts, in the app as well.
