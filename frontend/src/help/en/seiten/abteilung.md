This is the work area of the sub-unit where you have a role. You see its members, events, documents and invoices as far as your role allows. You switch the area at the top right. Its sub-items (members, development, invoices, offices, entries, export) are in the sidebar, as for every other area.

Under **Offices** you see today’s **Office holders** at the top, and below under “Roles and permissions” what each role of the sub-unit may do: per area _no access_, _read_ or _edit_. If your role may only read roles, you see the rights and who holds which role, but cannot change anything. The same applies to members’ logins: with _read_ you see in the record whether someone has a login, with _edit_ you send login links.

With role “edit” you assign any role under **Office holders** with **Assign**. The dialog can end the predecessor’s term; your own role with role management stays in place.
