This is the work area of the sub-unit where you have a role. You see its members, events, documents and invoices as far as your role allows. You switch the area at the top right.

Under “Roles and permissions” you see what each role of the sub-unit may do: per area _no access_, _read_ or _edit_. If your role may only read roles, you see the rights and who holds which role, but cannot change anything. The same applies to members’ logins: with _read_ you see in the record whether someone has a login, with _edit_ you send login links.
