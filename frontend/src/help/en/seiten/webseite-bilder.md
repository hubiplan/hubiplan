You upload and crop images for the website here. Instead of a file you can also use a copied image, such as a screenshot or an image from the browser: just press Ctrl+V, here or in the image picker. JPEG (including iPhone photos), PNG and WebP up to 15 MB are allowed; save HEIC images from an iPhone as JPEG first. Mind the rights: for photos you need the photographer's permission and, for recognisable people, their consent. Each image needs a short alternative text.
