Here you set what your unit and its sub-units are called, for example “corps” and “platoon”, with article and plural and, if you like, in English. HUBIplan uses these words everywhere in the menu, lists and texts; with several levels it counts per level, for example “7 corps · 15 platoons”. Whether sub-units keep their own members and have their own access is also set here. HUBIplan saves changes automatically.
