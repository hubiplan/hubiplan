Here you set what your unit and its sub-units are called, for example “club” and “section”, with article and plural and, if you like, in English. HUBIplan uses these words everywhere in the menu, lists and texts; with several levels it counts per level, for example “7 corps · 15 platoons”. Whether sub-units keep their own members and have their own access is also set here. If your club has no sub-units, turn off “Show … in the main menu” here; the menu item then disappears for everyone, existing sub-units are kept, and you can turn it back on at any time. HUBIplan saves changes automatically.
