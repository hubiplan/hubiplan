The legal notice states who is responsible for your pages. It applies to HUBIplan and the website. If required details are missing, HUBIplan shows this on the home page.

The authorised representatives come from the roles marked “represents the unit”. If “Show titles before names” is switched on under Website › Menu and sections › People, their title from the member record appears there too (e.g. “Dr. Max Muster”).
