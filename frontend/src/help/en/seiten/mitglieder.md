The list shows all members of the selected unit. At the top you search by name, email or
number; next to it you filter by status, membership type or gender. Clicking a name opens the
record with all periods, honours and contact details.

Each search word must match, but one may occur in the name and another in the email
address or number. Email addresses are found by the full address (`erika@example.org`) or
from the @ onwards (`@example.org` for everyone with that domain); parts without @ only
search names and numbers. Names are searched without regard to case, and `ß` and `ss` are
treated alike. Name sorting places umlauts with their base letter (`ä` with `a`).
Equal names have a fixed order so that moving between pages remains stable.

![The member list with search and filters](bild:mitglieder-liste)

On a phone, search stays visible. The Filters button opens status, membership type and other restrictions; the number indicates how many filters are set. **More actions** contains export and membership cards. For organisations, the Excel export contains type, name suffix, legal form and the primary contact; all contact persons are on the second sheet “Ansprechpartner”. The separate billing address with billing email, purchase order number and Leitweg-ID can be included as columns of their own. **Per page** lets you choose 10, 20 or 50 results; phones initially show 10. For multiple pages, navigation is available above and below the results. Your selection and page size are retained when you open a record and return. Small screens show name and membership type; open the record for other details.
