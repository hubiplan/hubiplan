HUBIplan congratulates your members on their birthday and club anniversary, by app or email, and by letter for members without an email address. Under **Settings** you choose which birthdays to congratulate on, whether to include anniversaries, and write the texts. Then **Save**.

## How it works

A few days in advance (seven by default) a draft is created for each day. Under **Upcoming greetings** you see who receives it and how. Print letters there in good time using the print button (PDF). On the day the greeting is sent automatically from the hour you set, or with **only after approval by the board** only when you click **Send now**. **Skip** drops a day; **Schedule again** brings it back. Sent greetings are listed under **History (30 days)** with their delivery log; they do not appear in the list of circulars.

## Texts and placeholders

The placeholders {Alter} and {Jubiläumsjahre} insert the age on the birthday and the years in the club, {Anrede Du} the greeting with the first name. **Use default text** restores the supplied text.

## Objection

Members can object to greetings in the members' area under **Messages**. If someone tells the board, untick **Wants birthday and anniversary greetings** in the member file under **Communication & delivery**. Anyone who has objected is shown in the preview with “does not want greetings” and receives nothing.
