Under Design you choose the look, colours and logo of the website. The colours usually come from the unit's appearance and can be adjusted here.

Under **Share design** you save a design as a ZIP file with **Save as package**. The package contains the styling, the layout of header, footer and home page and the colour defaults, but no coat of arms, texts or images.

Platform administrators can import a package under **Load design from package**. With a new **Short name** and **Name** it becomes an own design, for example today's “Klassisch” as a design for your club only. **Check** lets HUBIplan check the package: no JavaScript, no content from other addresses, only permitted templates. **Import** releases the design for this website; under **Imported designs** the release can be switched on and off for each website.
