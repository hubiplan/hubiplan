Your entries and your results are on one page here.

At the top you see which competitions you are entered for, with the organiser and whether the entry is confirmed. Below, **All starts and awards** lists your results so far with your place, and **Titles and winners** your titles. As long as you have not competed anywhere, the list stays empty.
