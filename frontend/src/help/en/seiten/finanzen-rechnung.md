This holds the details that appear on every invoice: payment term, bank details, creditor ID for direct debits and the number range.

Under **E-invoices for public authorities** you can have invoices to members with a Leitweg-ID (e.g. a town or district as a member) created as XRechnung. Enter the Leitweg-ID in the member’s billing address. When finalising, HUBIplan validates the XRechnung; if something is missing, such as the club’s address, contact, phone or an account with IBAN, the invoice stays a draft and the message names what is missing. Membership fees appear in it as not subject to VAT. The invoice email carries the XRechnung as an attachment.

Cancel bookings with receipts instead of deleting them. Imported bank statements are retained; ignore unwanted transactions. Archive used categories and create a new category for new assignments. Reminders retain their archived version.
