For donations and tax-deductible membership fees you create official donation receipts here, individually or as an annual statement. A checklist first shows missing details such as the tax exemption notice. You enter these details under Finance › Setup › Donations.

An issued donation receipt also protects its source cashbook entry: it cannot be edited, deleted or reverted with Undo. To make a correction, cancel the donation receipt first. You can then correct the entry under the usual cashbook rules; the cancelled receipt copy remains available.

Name searches ignore letter case and normalize Unicode spelling variants; “ß” and “ss” are treated as equal. Other selected filters still apply.

For donations up to 300 €, the tax office accepts the bank statement together with a note from the club (simplified donation proof). Once the tax-privileged status details are complete, Finance › Setup › Donations offers this note under **Donations up to 300 €** as a PDF and as text for the website. It names the notice, the purposes, the donation account and whether it also covers membership fees.

In the **Earmarks** tab you create purposes that people donate to specifically, such as a new flag. Assign a donation to a purpose with the **Earmarked for** field, and the expenses for it in the cash book with **Earmarked donation used for**. The overview shows the donations per purpose, how much has been used and the remainder. Only money donations count; cancellations do not. If the first donation was more than two years ago and something is left, a notice reminds you of the prompt use of funds. A purpose with donations or expenses can only be archived, not deleted.

A donation taken from the cash book follows its booking: if you change the booking's amount or date, HUBIplan changes the not yet confirmed donation as well. If the booking is deleted or cancelled (also by cancelling a receipt), the unconfirmed donation is removed. Once it is confirmed, the booking and receipt can only be changed, deleted or cancelled after the confirmation has been cancelled. Before issuing, HUBIplan checks that booking and donation match; the checklist names mismatches from older data.
