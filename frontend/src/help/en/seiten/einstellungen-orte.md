Here you save places that come up again and again, such as the clubhouse, fairground or shooting range. In events, competitions, volunteer duties and meetings you then choose them in the **Place** field instead of typing them every time.

- **New place**: name, address and **Directions and notes** (e.g. parking or entrance). Under **Exact map point (optional)** you enter latitude and longitude if the address is not enough.
- **Suggestions from earlier entries** shows places that were typed in several times in the last two years. **Apply** saves one of them.
- When you choose a saved place in a form, HUBIplan copies its name and address. Later changes here do not change earlier entries.
- If you type a new place in a form, **Save as place** saves it right away.
- A club also sees the places of its federation, a sub-unit those of its club. Only the owner can change or delete them.
- Places you no longer need are removed from the choice with **Edit** and the **Active (offered in forms)** box, without deleting them.
