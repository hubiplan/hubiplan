Here you see the published duty rosters of your club and your sub-units, for example for the
festival. Each shift shows how many places are still free.

**Sign me up** takes a free shift. HUBIplan checks whether the shift is already full, whether it
overlaps with another of your duties and whether you meet the minimum age. Some rosters allow
only a certain number of shifts per person; in others only the administration assigns people.

**Cancel duty** removes you again while the cancellation deadline is open. After that the shift
shows “Cancellation only via the administration”; please contact the administration then.

**My duties** lists all shifts you are signed up for. **Add to calendar** downloads them as a
calendar file. HUBIplan sends you a reminder email before the duty if your club has set this up.
