Here you decide what the menu on the left looks like for everyone in your unit: which main menu items are in the bar, in which order, and under which main menu item each menu item sits. A menu item can also be a main menu item itself, such as **Website** or **Competitions**; its sub-items then appear directly in the menu next to it.

The arrangement only decides the place. Who sees what is still decided by the switched-on modules and the rights of the roles. A main menu item without any visible menu item does not appear for that person.

## In detail

- **Move**: drag a menu item onto another main menu item with the mouse, or choose the target in the “Move … to” selection on the right. **Own main menu item** turns the menu item into a main menu item itself. You can place further menu items under such a main menu item, for example **Sheet music** under **Music**: they then appear in the menu after its own sub-items. If the menu item itself is moved again, the menu items placed under it move along.
- **Order**: the arrows move a main menu item up or down. **Home** always stays first.
- **Add main menu item**: choose a name in German (and in English if you like) and an icon, then move menu items into it. A new main menu item automatically gets an icon that no other one uses yet; icons already in use are marked in the list. The main menu items HUBIplan provides can be renamed too; an empty name field means: as provided. An empty main menu item can be removed.
- **Save** applies to everyone immediately. **Undo** brings back the previous arrangement, **Restore default** arranges everything as HUBIplan provides it.

At most twelve main menu items fit in the bar. Sub-units use the menu of their club. The structure of the help follows your arrangement. To see the menu as a role sees it, use “View as role” in the account menu.

Anyone with the right to edit settings may change this.
