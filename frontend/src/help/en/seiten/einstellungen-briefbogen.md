The letterhead defines the header, footer, colours and font for all of the unit's letters and PDFs, such as invoices, receipts and circulars.

The defaults match the previous look. For your own corporate design (style guide) you can set:

- **Body text font** and **Font for name, title and subject**: besides Office fonts such as Arial or Georgia, also the website fonts (Source Sans 3, Crimson Pro, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Open Sans, Roboto, Oswald, blackletter). They are embedded in PDFs. In the Word template their name comes with a fallback font (**Fallback font in Word (text)** or **Fallback font in Word (headings)**) that Word uses if the font is missing on the computer.
- **Logo/crest size**: small, medium or large. Portrait crests become noticeably wider with “large”.
- **Logo for the letterhead**: an image only for letters, such as a landscape word-and-image mark, while HUBIplan, membership cards and certificates keep the coat of arms. Without a logo of its own, the one from the appearance settings applies. Sub-units with their own letterhead can set their own, otherwise they take it over. Wide logos usually fit best with the size “large”.
- **Name as text next to the logo**: switch off if the logo already contains the name. Without a logo, the name is always shown in the header.
- **Name in the letterhead**: decides how the name breaks in the header, two lines at most, for example “Musterschützen-Bruderschaft” and below it “Musterstadt von 1920”. With **Second line smaller** the second line looks like a subtitle. If the field is empty, the unit's name appears in the header: a long name is first reduced and, if it still does not fit, spread evenly over two lines, in Word as in the PDF. Following pages and the return address always show the full name.
- **Line colour** from the unit's palette, plus **Line below the header** and **Line above the footer**, each on or off. If the colour barely stands out on white (contrast below 3:1, such as a light yellow), the page points this out.
- **Colour of title and subject**: black or main colour, for example for “Invoice” and the subject of the Word template.
- **Table lines** and **Table stripes** for invoices, cancellation invoices and receipts.

Every sub-unit with its own letterhead sets this separately. A connected AI assistant can also change the letterhead if you explicitly allow it when connecting.
