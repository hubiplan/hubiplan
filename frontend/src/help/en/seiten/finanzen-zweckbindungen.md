In the **Earmarks** tab you create purposes that people donate to specifically, such as renovating the clubhouse. Assign a donation to a purpose with the **Earmarked for** field, and the expenses for it in the cash book with **Earmarked donation used for**. The overview shows the donations per purpose, how much has been used and the remainder. Only money donations count; cancellations do not. If the first donation was more than two years ago and something is left, a notice reminds you of the prompt use of funds. A purpose with donations or expenses can only be archived, not deleted.
