Categories group bookings, such as “fees”, “festival” or “uniforms”, and each category belongs to a tax area (non-profit area, asset management, purpose-related business, commercial business). The cash report and tax overview are built from them.

**Use a template** creates the usual categories of a shooting, sports or music club, each with a tax area and, where it fits, the donations or artist fees mark. Only missing categories are created; existing ones stay unchanged. The areas are a suggestion, and your tax adviser enters the DATEV accounts. For sponsorship: if the sponsor is only named, the money belongs to asset management; active advertising such as boards, ads or jerseys is a commercial operation.
