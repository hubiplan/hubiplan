The notice board is for short news to members, such as “The bus leaves 10 minutes later today”
or photos from the party. It sits between the formal circular and a messenger group: members see
the posts in the member area and in the app but cannot reply. There is no chat and there are no
comments.

## Writing a post

Under **New post** you write a text of up to 2,000 characters and attach up to six photos if you
like (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 10 MB each). **Publish** shows the post to the members right away.

- **Pin to the top** keeps important posts above all others until you remove them with **Unpin**.
- **Show until (optional)** hides the post from members after that day.
- **Send a push notification to the app** sends a short notification to all members with the app
  and notifications switched on. No email is ever sent; use circulars for that.

Changing the text, pinning and deleting is available below each post. Anyone allowed to write can
also do so in the member area and in the app under **Notice board**, so on the go from a phone.

## Who sees what

Posts of the club are seen by all current members. Posts of a unit (such as a platoon) are seen
only by its members, including those of the units below it. Each post shows who wrote it.

Writing requires the **Notice board** right for editing. Roles that edit circulars or events have
it from the start; you can change it under Settings › Roles. The notice board is a module and can
be switched on and off under Settings › Modules.

## Clean-up

You still see expired posts here for 30 days, then HUBIplan deletes them together with their
photos. Posts without “Show until” disappear after a year, pinned ones only once you unpin them.
