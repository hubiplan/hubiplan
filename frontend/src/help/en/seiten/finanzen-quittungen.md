For cash payments you issue a receipt here, with your stored signature if you like. Each receipt gets a sequential number and is booked in the cash book.

The signature on the screen is a simple electronic signature: it shows that someone signed on the spot, but it does not verify who it was. If the app is used offline, date and time come from the phone. For the cash book this is as good a voucher as a paper receipt. If you need more certainty (for example for large amounts), also have the receipt confirmed in writing or by email.

Name searches ignore letter case and normalize Unicode spelling variants; “ß” and “ss” are treated as equal. Other selected filters still apply.

The details of a new receipt are saved with **Continue to signature**. If you change details and cancel or leave the page, HUBIplan asks before discarding them. **Continue editing** retains your input. An automatically selected sole cash account does not trigger a warning on its own.

If the receipt list or the account and category choices fail to load, use **Reload**. A failed request for choices does not discard receipt details you have already entered.
