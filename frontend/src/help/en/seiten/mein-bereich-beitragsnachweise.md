If you pay a reduced fee, for example as a student, the club needs a current proof every year. Here you see, per fee year, by when it is due and whether it has been accepted.

Choose **Choose file or photo**, optionally pick the type of proof and click **Submit proof**. PDF files and photos up to 10 MB are allowed. Until the board has reviewed it, the reduction continues to apply. You get an email about the decision or a question.

Instead of here you can also use the link in the reminder, or reply to the reminder email with the certificate attached. If no proof arrives by the deadline, the regular fee applies for that year.
