The cut-off date statistics count the members on one day by age group and gender, by place of residence and by {pl}. They are meant for reports: the membership census of the local sports federation, the report to your association, a grant application to the town or district. **PDF**, **Excel** and **CSV** download the same figures as a file.

## How it counts

It counts the members after the leavings of the cut-off date, as under Trends: anyone leaving on that day no longer counts. For **Age** you choose between completed years on the cut-off date and the year of birth (year of the cut-off date minus year of birth), as many sports federations require. Members without a date of birth have their own row; organisations as members are counted separately because they have neither age nor gender.

The place comes from the address in the member record; the same place with different postcodes is one row. Anyone in several {pl} counts in each. Under **Membership types** you can leave out, for example, supporting members.

In an umbrella association only memberships with a reported membership type count; instead of {pl} it lists the clubs.
