Overview by month, latest changes at the top. All changes in each version (release) are listed below.

After an update to a new release, HUBIplan shows everyone who manages something a one-time notice “HUBIplan has been updated to version …” with the link “What's new”. “Hide” removes it; it appears only once per browser.

November 2026:

#### Highlights

- New modules: **Surveys and opinion polls**, **Deadline calendar**, **Qualifications with expiry date**, **Meetings**, **Volunteer duties** and **Business partners**.
- **New look** with dark mode and **quick search** (Ctrl+K); the main menu has been regrouped.
- **Website as a construction kit**: home page and text pages built from sections, fine-tuning, draft and versions.
- **Finance**: budget, taxes and VAT, cash audit, fixed assets, cancellation invoice.
- **Members**: online application, cancellation in the member area, households and companies.
- **Letterhead** to your own style guide, **two-factor sign-in** for every role, **AI assistants** with sign-in.
- **Help**: help for every page, guides, terms and this news.

#### Website

- Website: **Clearer fine-tuning.** Design › Fine-tuning now has a bar on the left with the areas of the website from top to bottom (basic style, font, readability, header and menu, title image and home page, subpages, website areas, footer), each with the number of changed controls. The most important controls come first, the rest is under “More settings”.
- Website: **Eye-catching notice bar.** The notice bar under Website › Overview now appears in the accent colour below the header, with an icon, a button to its target and an optional countdown (“5 days to go”). Style (accent, attention, festive, plain), place (above or below the header, floating at the bottom) and icon can be chosen.
- Website: **Readable addresses** for dignities, competitions and series, such as `/majestaeten/hubertuskoenige/` instead of a long identifier. You can choose the address yourself; old addresses and links redirect.
- Website: **House font from the style guide.** Under Design › Fine-tuning › Font, **Own font stack** uses fonts installed on the device, such as “Lucida Sans, Segoe UI, Arial”. No font file is delivered; anyone without the font sees the next one. Also for AI assistants.
- Website: **Set off the band of sub-units.** Under Design › Fine-tuning › **Band of sub-units** the band on the pages of sub-units can use the box colour, a light tone or plain with a line, so it does not look like a second page header.
- **Wide logos** (landscape wordmarks) are no longer tiny in the header of HUBIplan and the website, on cards, certificates and in PDFs: the height is fixed and the width follows the image.
- Website: **Long lists in two columns.** Under Design › Fine-tuning › **Long lists** › “in two columns”, long, narrow tables such as all kings or the winners over the years appear side by side in two columns on a computer screen; on a phone it stays a single column.
- Website: **Cover photo as stage, watermark, event cards and full footer.** Under Design › Fine-tuning, **Cover photo as stage** › “edge to edge” puts the cover photo right below the header without border, with a gradient and your own title lines that change with the carousel images. Sections with a background can get a large, faint **watermark** (crest or your own image).
- Website: **AI assistants create introductions.** A connected AI assistant now also writes the introduction of an area (board, events, sub-units, gallery) and the box next to the contact form when it does not exist yet. It goes into the draft under Website › Texts; publishing happens in HUBIplan as always.
- Website: **Button in a tile.** A tile with a target can get a button at the bottom, such as “Get in touch”, instead of only the title becoming a link. This works on the home page and on text pages, also for AI assistants.
- Website: **Versions also show texts.** Under Website › Versions, besides the versions of the design, you now also see the most recently published texts and text pages with time and person; **Open** takes you to the text, where you restore an earlier version. A note names what takes effect immediately without versions (settings, images, design packages, colours and logo); that is shown in the log.
- Website: **Import design packages yourself.** Whoever manages the website imports a design package for their own unit under Design › Share design › Load design from package, without needing the operator. It applies only to your own website and is checked as before. Platform administrators see all units' designs in one list and can block them.
- Website: **Several header images.** Under Design › Fine-tuning › Header you can choose up to ten header images (and header images for phones as well). The website then shows one of them on each visit, on every page, like many WordPress themes with a changing header image.
- Website: **buttons and tiles can also lead to a sub-unit**, such as a platoon's page. Links to your own website open in the same window instead of a new one.
- Website: **Random title image.** Under Design › Fine-tuning › Image carousel advances there is a new level **“random”**: on each visit the home page shows a different image from the image carousel, which stays fixed instead of cycling. This helps, for example, when moving from WordPress sites with a changing header image.
- Website: New fonts under Design › Fine-tuning › Font: **Clear** (Source Sans 3), **Elegant** (Source Sans 3, headings in Crimson Pro) and **Easy to read** (Atkinson Hyperlegible), plus **Blackletter** and **Bold** (Oswald) for headings. The “Easy to read” preset now uses Atkinson Hyperlegible. Like all other fonts they are stored on the website itself.
- Website: In **Text with image** the image can now also stand **above or below the text**, small or across the full width. The **Images** section also shows a **single image** with caption large across the whole width.
- Website: **How long something is current.** For “Current results”, **Results of a competition**, **Gallery overview** and **Latest posts** you choose under **Edit** how many days after the competition, album or post it is still shown. Running competitions stay; if nothing is current any more, the section disappears by itself.
- Website: **Design reorganised and fine-tuning extended.** The sidebar now has **Design and colours**, **Fine-tuning**, **Areas** and **Versions**, each with a preview; everyone puts the home page together in one place under Website › Home, language, search engines and images for AI assistants are on the overview, and “Menu and sections” is now called “Menu and building blocks”.
- Website: New section type **Countdown** for the home page and text pages. It counts down days, hours, minutes and seconds to the start of a public event or to a point in time of your choice. Afterwards the section disappears or shows a text of your own. An AI assistant can add it to the draft as well.
- Website: 17 more section types for the home page and text pages: **Become a member**, **Call for donations** with a progress bar, **Opening and training hours**, **Two columns**, **Table**, **Timeline**, **Festival programme**, **Notice box**, **Expandable sections**, **Images**, **Download list**, **Majesties of a unit**, **Results of a competition**, **Gallery overview**, **Latest posts**, **Members' area and app** with a QR code and **Figures from HUBIplan** (number of members and years since founding, always up to date).
- Website: **Search** now finds everything the website shows publicly, including the greeting, tiles and other sections of the home page, sections of text pages, the chronicle and the unit pages. Part of a word is enough, accents do not matter.
- Website: a full menu bar can now be relieved. Under Website › Menu and sections, **Menu items in the footer** sets for each main item whether it appears in the menu, only in the footer (next to the legal notice and privacy policy) or in both, for example “Contact”.
- Website: Design › Fine-tuning › Header and menu now offers an optional **Header image for phones**. On narrow screens below 600 pixels the website shows this more compact version instead of the wide header image, so lettering and subtitle stay readable. A connected AI assistant can also set it in the draft.
- Website: Design › Fine-tuning now offers **Dark mode** following the device setting, **Increased contrast**, a **“Back to top” button on long pages**, the font size “very large”, the **Image format in tiles** (3:2, 4:3, square), the **Menu on phones** as a collapsible menu or swipeable bar, the cover image height “medium” and “only by hand”, “slowly” or “normal” for image carousels.
- Website: nine more kinds of your own sections for the home page and text pages: **Frequently asked questions** to expand, **Partners and sponsors** with logo and link, **Directions** to a saved place with “Plan route”, **Events of one category** (e.g. only training), **Document to download**, **Video as a link** without embedding, **Excerpt from the chronicle**, **Facts at a glance** and **Divider**.
- Website: under Overview › **Notice bar** a notice with a period appears at the top of every page and disappears by itself afterwards. Text pages can get a **Cover image** above the heading. Under Design › **Design as a file** you save and load design, colours, fine-tuning and display, for example for another unit.
- Website: people who may only “Edit content” change the content of the existing sections under Website › **Home** and publish it. Adding, moving and styling sections stays with the website managers.
- Website: Under Design › **Area display**, the website shows the board as cards or a compact list, events as a list or monthly calendar, sub-units as large or small tiles or a list, and the gallery as tiles or masonry. Under Website › Texts, **Introduction for an area** adds a text at the top of these areas.
- Website: **Text pages** can be built from sections like the home page. Under Website › Texts, **Add section** inserts, for example, banners, buttons, tiles or a quote above or below the text. The existing text stays the first section; without changes the page looks as before.
- Website: the home page is now a **builder**. Under Design › **Home**, **Add section** inserts your own sections, as often as you like: **Text**, **Text with image**, **Banner**, **Button bar**, **Tiles**, **Featured event** with a countdown, **Album preview**, **Quote** and **Contact persons**. Each section gets a background and width, and in the “Newspaper” design a column. Everything goes into the draft first and appears straight away in the preview.
- Website: changes to design, colours, fine-tuning, header image and home page are now a **draft** first. Under Design, a preview next to them shows the real website as **Phone**, **Tablet** or **Desktop**; nothing goes live until you choose **Publish**. You can restore the last five published versions under **Earlier versions**.

Settings you leave alone show what the design provides.
- Website: the menu can contain **external links**, for example to your federation; they open a new window. One main item can be highlighted as a coloured **button**, such as “Become a member”. You now arrange the home page sections by dragging, like the menu, instead of with arrows.
- Website: For each text you can choose whether the caption appears below the image and in the image carousel. The default is without a caption, for existing pages too.
- Website: New “Klassisch” (classic) design with a light header, a coloured line on top and menu buttons in the club colour. To keep the previous Klassisch, save it as a package first and import it as an own design.

- Website: The chronicle can be kept as a timeline, with events (year or period), chapters as subheadings and Excel import; if you like, the newest entries come first. An event can have up to six pictures, on the left, on the right, alternating or large below the text.
- Website: Copied images can be pasted with Ctrl+V, in the image library and in the image picker.

#### Finance

- Finance › Set up › Fee rates: **Work hours with a substitute fee.** Set required hours per year and a substitute fee per missing hour for each membership type, optionally with age limits. Volunteer shifts ticked as done count. Missing hours are added to the invoice as a separate item in the first fee run of the following financial year; the status per member can be viewed beforehand.
- Finance: **Expense claims.** Members claim what they paid for the club themselves in the member area and the app, with a photo of the receipt, including mileage and advances.
- Finance › Set up › Fee rates: the **Fee when joining during the period** can be chosen: full fee or pro rata per started **month**, **quarter** or **half-year**. Per half-year means, for example: joining in the first half of the financial year pays the full annual fee, joining in the second half pays half. Existing settings are kept (on = per month).
- Finance › Evaluate & close › **Budget**: plan income and expenses per category for a financial year, optionally per sub-unit, and compare them with the cash book as you go: plan, actual, variance and a forecast based on the previous year. The previous year's plan or actual figures serve as a template.
- Finances › Incoming invoices: **invoice mailbox**. Every unit gets its own address for e-invoices, e.g. `rechnung+k7m2q9ab@example.org`. HUBIplan collects emails to this address every minute and imports XML and ZUGFeRD/Factur-X attachments into incoming invoices. Senders that match no business partner are marked **Unknown sender**; emails without an e-invoice appear as a notice. The server administration sets up the mailbox.
- Finances › Incoming invoices: technical validation of an imported e-invoice now runs in the background; the entry shows **Checking** until the result is ready. Importing no longer holds up other work in the treasury. Declined and unreadable entries without a booking are deleted after 90 days.
- Finance: every group in the sidebar has its own page. Clicking the group name shows all entries with a short description, what is to do there, and the main action, e.g. **Record income** for Book. ▸ expands the group without changing the page.
- Menu: Finance is organised in expandable groups: **Fees & invoices**, **Payments**, **Book**, **Evaluate & close** and **Set up**. Only the group of the current page is open, ▸ opens another one. The former tabs under Invoices and Setup (e.g. Reminders, Accounts) are entries of their own, batch payouts are under Payments. The addresses stay the same.
- New: cancellation invoice. Cancelling creates a separate document with a number (e.g. R2026-0012-S), date and reason that can be sent by email. Paid invoices can be cancelled too; the credit is refunded or offset against an open invoice. **Cancel and create again** opens a copy as a draft right away.
- Menu: Finance follows the course of the year. “Book” now also contains fixed assets, allowances and batch payouts, “Evaluate” contains evaluation (with net assets), development and taxes (with artists’ social security levy and use of funds), “Close the year” contains donation receipts and year-end closing (with cash audit). Instruments and uniforms are under Equipment & rooms, the personal music reminders under Tasks. Old addresses redirect.
- New: VAT in the cash book. Tax rate per booking (default from the category), input VAT also proportionally and under Finance › Taxes › VAT a template for the advance return, optionally with flat-rate input VAT under § 23a UStG.
- New: allowances now also count waivers as expense donations, payments from the musician settlement and the cash books of sub-units (Finance › Allowances).
- New: statement on the use of funds. Finance › Taxes › Use of funds shows in a simplified calculation which funds must be used by when, and warns if a deadline has passed.
- New: earmarked donations. In donation receipts › **Earmarks**, donations and cash book expenses can be assigned to a purpose; the overview shows the remainder and reminds you to use the funds promptly.
- New: donations up to 300 €. Finance › Setup › Donations offers the simplified donation proof as a PDF and as text for the website; together with the bank statement it is enough for the tax office.
- New: category templates. Under Finance › Setup › Categories, **Use a template** creates the usual categories of a shooting, sports or music club with their tax areas, without changing existing ones.
- New: maximum for the free reserve. Finance › Reserves works out per financial year how much may be added to the free reserve, including amounts carried over from the two previous years, and warns if it is exceeded. Depreciation is shown as a guide for the replacement reserve.
- New: statement of net assets. Finance › Evaluation › Net assets shows account balances, open receivables, fixed assets and open liabilities at the year end, plus the funds set aside in reserves. The overview is also part of the treasurer's report.
- New: artists' social security levy. Expenses for bands, DJs or graphic designers can be marked as artist fees in the cash book or via the category. Finance › Taxes › Artists' social security levy shows the total per recipient, the levy and the reporting deadline, also as Excel.
- New: cash audit. Under Finance › Year-end closing › Cash audit the treasury creates an audit assignment per financial year. The auditors tick off samples with their documents and the account balances at year end, rate checkpoints, record findings and recommend discharge. The audit report is produced as a PDF.
- New: fixed asset register. Finance › Fixed assets lists tents, trailers and other club property with depreciation and book value. Depreciation only counts in the tax report; the cash book stays unchanged.
- New: allowances for volunteers. In the cash book an expense can be marked as trainer allowance or volunteer allowance. Finance › Allowances shows the totals per person and calendar year, warns above the tax-free amount and offers a declaration to sign as PDF for each person.
- New: split bookings. In the cash book, **Split** divides a booking across several categories or tax areas, for example the caterer's settlement. Each part becomes a booking of its own; receipts and the bank transaction stay with the first part.
- New: tax report. Under Finance › Taxes a traffic light compares income from commercial operations with the tax-free limit (€50,000 since 2026), with results per tax area (cancellations offset), a note on losses, the small-business limit and when the next tax return is due; as PDF and Excel. Sub-units with their own cash book count towards the club unless marked "separate for tax purposes". The finance overview points out when something needs attention there.

#### Club and members

- Members: **Cut-off date statistics as a report file.** Under Members › Trends › Cut-off date HUBIplan counts the members on one day by age group and gender, by place of residence and by department, optionally with the age groups of the sports federation or youth funding and by year of birth. The figures come as PDF, Excel and CSV for sports federations, associations and grant applications.
- **Data protection tools.** Under Administration › GDPR, HUBIplan builds the record of processing activities (Art. 30 GDPR) from the enabled modules, with your own additions and as a PDF. Processors come from the business partners (new role “Processor”, agreement “Data processing agreement”), and data breaches are documented with the 72-hour reporting deadline.
- **Birthday and anniversary greetings.** Under Circulars › Greetings, HUBIplan congratulates on special or all birthdays and on club anniversaries, by app, email or letter. A draft is created a few days in advance so that letters can be printed in time; on the day it is sent automatically or after approval. Members can object in the members' area.
- Bookings: **Charge rent.** An item can have a rental price per booking, day or hour. “Create invoice” on a booking for a member creates a draft invoice directly to the member, even if their fee is otherwise paid through a collective invoice.
- Bookings: **Book for a member and view a month or year.** When booking under Equipment & rooms › Bookings you can look up a member; the booking then appears in the member record under “Bookings” and in the member's members area, without entering contact details twice. Besides the week there is now a month and a year view.
- Members: **Reduction with proof.** For reduced fees, for example for students, the treasurer sets up a reduction under Finances › Fee rates with a deadline (e.g. 28/02), grace period and types of proof. HUBIplan reminds members in January and before the deadline, accepts proofs in the members' area, the app, via a personal link or as a reply to the reminder email, and runs a pre-check (name, studies, validity).
- **Family application**: in the online application you can turn on the family application under Fields. Adults then register further family members at the same address at once; on acceptance a household is created and the applicant pays for everyone with one mandate.
- **Households in Excel import and export**: columns “Haushalt”, “Rolle im Haushalt”, “Firma” and “Beitrag über”; the same name in the file makes one household. HUBIplan books payments on household and company invoices to the payer, so the donation receipt goes to them as well.
- **Companies report employees**: if an organisation member pays for employees, its contact persons see under My area › Employees whom the company pays for. They report new employees with name and email (the person gets the link to the online application and joins the company once admitted) and end the payment from a date; the membership continues.
- **Guardians**: for members under 18 you can now enter parents or a legal guardian by hand in the member file. If you like, they also get circulars to the child by email and their own member area login. Four weeks before the 18th birthday they get an email; on the birthday their access and entry end automatically.
- **Fees via household or company**: a household or company can have a payer (member, person without membership, organisation member or business partner). The fee run then creates one invoice for all members with “Fee via the household”, optionally with a family or company fee (maximum, flat rate or graduated, under Finance › Set up › Fee rates). Direct debit uses the payer's mandate; the payer sees the invoice in the member area.
- **Households and companies**: under Members › Households and companies you link members who live together into a household (with suggestions from the same address) and members whose fees a company pays into a company. Circulars and invitations to a household at the same address go out as one letter, emails to the same address as one email.
- **Cancellation in the member area**: under Members › Setup › Member area & photos you can switch on that members cancel themselves, with a notice period and date according to the statutes (e.g. three months to the end of the financial year). HUBIplan suggests the earliest leaving date and sends a confirmation of receipt. Under Members › Cancellations you confirm the leaving date or decline; the member receives the confirmation of leaving by email.
- **Qualifications with expiry date**: The new module records certificates such as first aid, coach licence, youth leader or range officer in the member file, with validity and history. Each club defines its own types (Members › Setup › Qualifications); for the criminal record certificate only the date of inspection is noted, never its content.
- **Online membership application**: under Members › Setup › Online application every club gets its own joining form with address and QR code, optionally per sub-unit. Applicants confirm by email; under Members › Membership applications you review details and matches, collect approvals as your statutes require, ask questions and admit with one click.
- Member file: new tab “Fees” with invoices, outstanding amounts, SEPA mandates and the billing address.
- Assigning offices: under Members › Offices every office has an “Assign” button, as does the member record (“Assign role”). The dialog says beforehand what will happen and can end the predecessor’s term at the same time. With the “Unit” filter this also works for sub-unit offices; their page and workspace assign with the same dialog.
- Members: Past unit memberships (such as platoons) can be added with start and end in one step. This also works for units that are no longer active. If they overlap, the message names the affected membership with its exact dates.
- Usability: Member admission guides keyboard focus through two clearly named stages. Calendar responses confirm saving for screen readers; submission methods and validation hints appear in English when English is selected.
- Terms: changes members make to their own details are now called “data changes”; “entries” only means competition entries. In a sub-unit’s work area the tab is called “Offices” and shows the office holders first.
- Chronicle: you now maintain the introduction and timeline only under Club › Chronicle. Under Website › Texts the chronicle only shows its address, menu setting and delete, plus a link to the chronicle.
- **Hide sub-units**: a club without sub-units (platoons, sections …) can permanently hide the menu item under Settings › Names and terms. Existing sub-units are kept.
- Work area of a sub-unit: the sub-items are now in the sidebar instead of tabs above the content.

#### Business partners

- **Business partners** have their own menu item under Club: suppliers, service providers, sponsors, authorities and others with type, roles, number, several contact persons, notes and (for the treasury) bank account. Duplicate entries can be merged, and the list can be exported to and imported from Excel.
- **Business partners in the cash book**: business partners come first for payee/payer and bring their default category. Bank statements recognise partners by IBAN, the record shows turnover per year, and frequent names from the cash book can be applied as business partners.
- **Sponsorship for business partners**: agreements with period, contribution, consideration and tax classification, a renewal reminder as a task, a thank-you letter as PDF and consent for the website section “Partners and sponsors”.
- **Business partner spreadsheet with all details**: the export contains all fields and a second sheet “Contact person”. The import updates existing partners by their number and adds contact persons, so you can maintain the list in Excel. Also new: **First contact** (date and occasion) and **Looked after by** (a member).
- **Additional fields for business partners**: your own fields as for members, such as sponsorship level or delivery time, with values in the partner record and as a column in the Excel export and import.
- **Business partners in incoming invoices**: HUBIplan suggests the matching business partner for each incoming invoice (VAT ID, IBAN, sender or name) or creates one from the invoice. If the IBAN differs from the stored one, approval requires confirmation by phone. A linked cash book entry takes over the partner, and contact persons' addresses count as known senders in the invoice mailbox.
- **Contracts, documents and serial letters for business partners**: contracts with notice period and reminder, files on the partner record, serial letter as PDF and e-mail list for a partner group.

#### Events, meetings and planning

- **Events from your email program.** Invite a mailing list such as `vorstand@…` to an event in Outlook, Thunderbird, the Nextcloud calendar or on your phone: after you confirm, HUBIplan creates the event, informs the list and takes over a Talk link or creates a new video room. Changes and cancellations come along. HUBIplan's invitations can be accepted or declined in the email program; that counts as a reply.
- **Binding registration with participation fee.** With the new **Registrations** module an event gets binding registration instead of accepting and declining, with rates (members, guests, age groups), price tiers such as early bird, a place limit and a registration deadline. Members register themselves and their family in the members' area; the board can also add guests.
- **Clearer events**: In an open event the buttons (Edit, circular, reminder, add to my calendar) now sit right below when and where. Responses show acceptances, refusals and open replies as large numbers, and **For this event** brings meeting, duty roster, booking, tasks, equipment, statement and attachments together, one row each with matching buttons. Empty areas take only one line now.
- **Meetings: sub-items on the agenda.** Items can have sub-items (8.1, 8.2 under “Elections”). Dragging an item onto another makes it a sub-item of that item; dragging it between two items changes the order; the **Belongs to** selection does the same. The numbers appear the same way in the invitation, minutes, member area and online voting.
- **Meetings: check-in via app and online voting.** The chair shows a QR code that changes every 30 seconds; members scan it with the app or their phone camera and tap **I'm here**, so the quorum is always up to date. For resolutions and elections, those present vote on their phones, open or secret and with proxies; one click transfers the result to the minutes.
- **Deadline calendar for club obligations** (new module): recurring obligations such as the tax return and exemption notice, cash audit, transparency register or insurance are kept in one place with deadline, repetition and responsible role, with suggestions for registered associations. HUBIplan reminds the holders of the role by email and under “To do” and keeps a history of when an obligation was completed.
- **Group orders** (new module **Orders**): club clothing, a commemorative booklet or uniform parts with sizes, a deadline and optional maximum quantities. Members order in the member area and in the apps and can change their order until the deadline; the office sees totals per item and size as Excel and ticks off the hand-out. Price and invoice are optional, for example for a plain size survey or when the club pays.
- **Notice board** (new module): short news with up to six photos for the club or a unit, such as “The bus leaves 10 minutes later”. Members read them in the member area and in the apps, new posts are marked; a push notification to the apps is optional, never an email. Not a chat: there are no replies. Anyone allowed to write can also post from the app.
- **Surveys and polls** (new module): find a date, meal choice, “who is coming along?”, yes/no/abstain polls or free questions, open, with names for administrators only or anonymous. You can ask the club, a subunit, selected people or those invited to an event; guests count for meal choices. Members answer in the member area, in the app or via the link in the email.
- New: **Directions** on events with notes and the links **Route in OpenStreetMap** and **Route in Google Maps** in the event, emails and reply page. An exact point can be taken from a map link; saved places bring their directions and point.
- New: replying via a link in the email. Invitations, reminders and circulars about an event contain a personal link to a page without login with the event, its attachments and the choice accept, decline or “Maybe”. New on the event: **Allow “Maybe” as an answer** and an own question for the comment. The invitation email carries the event's attachments.
- New: circulars about an event. In the event under **For this event**, **Write a circular about it** creates a draft to those invited, with time, place, reply deadline and the event's PDF attachments. Optionally it goes only to those who have not replied yet, accepted or declined.
- New: **Places** under Settings. Save frequent places such as the clubhouse or fairground once with address and directions and choose them in the **Place** field of events, competitions, volunteer duties and meetings. **Save as place** saves a new place straight from the form; suggestions come from earlier entries. Clubs also see the places of their federation.
- **Rooms in places**: Under Settings › Places a place (a building with address) gets rooms such as hall, shooting range or meeting room. You maintain the address only once; events, competitions, volunteer duties and meetings offer “Place, Room”. Existing places such as “Community centre, Hall” and “Community centre, Shooting range” with the same address have been combined into one place with rooms; earlier events stay as they were.
- Events: in the unit's calendar, the board can choose **Event for** to create events for sub-units that do not manage themselves. Only their members are invited then.
- New: Meetings. General meetings, board meetings and meetings of units can be prepared with an event and an agenda. For each type the club sets the notice period; HUBIplan shows the date by which invitations must go out. The invitation is created as a circular with the agenda to the members entitled to vote.
- New: Volunteer duties. Create duty rosters with duties and shifts for festivals and events (quick entry such as “14-18, 18-22”), assign members and external helpers, with notes on overlaps, full shifts and minimum age. Notice as PDF and list as Excel. The platform switches the module on.
- Setup: Events have their own “Setup” with categories, external calendars, sharing and the website link. The donation receipt details are now under Finance › Setup › Donations. Roles, accounts and offices use the same terms everywhere (account, term of office, leadership, permissions); in the member file, offices and the account are on the “Roles and account” tab.

#### Settings, security and platform

- **Video room for events.** With **Video room for events** switched on under Settings › Nextcloud, **Create video room** in an event creates a Talk conversation with link and password. Everyone who can see the event sees both, also in the apps, the invitation and the Nextcloud calendar; HUBIplan deletes the room after the event.
- **Save files to Nextcloud and take files from it.** Next to the cash report, tax evaluation, cash count, minutes, resolution book and documents, **Save to Nextcloud** puts the file into a folder only one group can read, with a period per type (minutes permanently, accounting records 10 years, lists of people 3 months). When uploading, **From Nextcloud** fetches a file from a shared folder.
- **Keep Nextcloud groups in sync.** Under Settings › Nextcloud, HUBIplan no longer sets groups only at sign-in, but after every change to roles, offices or units, and every night. Anyone who gives up a role also loses the group and its folders, even while their sync app keeps running. **Compare groups** shows beforehand what would change.
- **Events in the Nextcloud calendar.** Under Settings › Nextcloud, HUBIplan writes the events for members and, if you like, the “board only” events into two Nextcloud calendars, shared read-only with the groups “Mitglieder” and “Vorstand”. Editing still happens in HUBIplan; changes arrive every 15 minutes.
- **Letterhead: own logo and name in the letterhead.** Under Settings › Letterhead you can set a **logo only for letters**, such as a landscape word-and-image mark, while HUBIplan keeps the coat of arms. With **Name in the letterhead** you decide how a long name breaks over two lines, the second one smaller if you like.
- **Letterhead following your style guide**: Under Settings › Letterhead you can now choose fonts for text and headings (including the website fonts, embedded in PDFs), the logo/crest size, whether the name appears next to the logo, the line colour from your palette, header and footer lines separately, the colour of title and subject, and the lines and stripes of invoice tables.
- Appearance: every palette colour gets a **use** (e.g. “body text”), plus up to two named **gradients** made from two palette colours. **View colour sheet** shows the colours with hex and RGB values and the gradients, to pass on or print.
- Appearance: platform, units and sub-units can upload a second **logo / coat of arms for dark mode**. It appears in the dark appearance of the web interface; without one, the normal logo is used.
- Security: **two-factor sign-in for every role.** Anyone with a permission in a club or department sets up two-factor sign-in within 3 days. Until then, circulars, exports and posting in the treasury are already paused, afterwards all permissions. The own member area remains usable without a second factor.
- Security: full exports, assigning roles, pairing the HUBIplan app and permanent deletion ask for a current code from the authenticator app if the last one is older than 10 minutes; without two-factor authentication set up, for your password. Platform administration now only works with two-factor authentication, without a transition period.
- Platform: an **empty unit** that never had members or financial data can be deleted permanently right after deactivating it; the 30-day waiting period now only applies to units with data.
- Platform: units are deleted in two stages. **Deactivate and schedule deletion** deactivates the unit and all its subunits; the data is kept. **Delete permanently** is possible at the earliest 30 days later and only after the retention periods for books and vouchers have expired, with a current two-factor code and the name of whoever confirmed the periods. A deleted unit does not come back with an older backup either.
- Settings: the new **Setup (overview)** lists all setup pages of the modules with the status of each item, a search and the progress of the required items.
- Accounts: Settings › Accounts lists all accounts of the unit with roles, member area, last sign-in and two-factor. Filters show, for example, who has never signed in or whose invitation has expired; selected accounts get the sign-in link again. The terms of office per account moved here from Roles and permissions.
- Nextcloud: The club's Nextcloud can use sign-in with the HUBIplan account. When access in HUBIplan ends, HUBIplan can also disable the Nextcloud account itself. Several roles can share one Nextcloud group (e.g. “1st” and “2nd treasurer” in “Treasurer”).
- View as role: Whoever manages roles can look at HUBIplan the way a role sees it (read only).
- English: The interface is also available in English. You can switch under My account › Profile.
