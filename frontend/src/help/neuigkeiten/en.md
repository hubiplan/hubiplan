Overview by month, latest changes at the top. All changes in each version (release) are listed below.

After an update to a new release, HUBIplan shows everyone who manages something a one-time notice “HUBIplan has been updated to version …” with the link “What's new”. “Hide” removes it; it appears only once per browser.

November 2026:

- Home page: the row of key figures (such as members today, open data changes, outstanding fees, cash box) is gone. The numbers are still on their own pages.
- New: **Directions** on events with notes and the links **Route in OpenStreetMap** and **Route in Google Maps** in the event, emails and reply page. An exact point can be taken from a map link; saved places bring their directions and point.
- New: replying via a link in the email. Invitations, reminders and circulars about an event contain a personal link to a page without login with the event, its attachments and the choice accept, decline or “Maybe”. New on the event: **Allow “Maybe” as an answer** and an own question for the comment. The invitation email carries the event's attachments.
- New: circulars about an event. In the event under **For this event**, **Write a circular about it** creates a draft to those invited, with time, place, reply deadline and the event's PDF attachments. Optionally it goes only to those who have not replied yet, accepted or declined.
- New: **Places** under Settings. Save frequent places such as the clubhouse or fairground once with address and directions and choose them in the **Place** field of events, competitions, volunteer duties and meetings. **Save as place** saves a new place straight from the form; suggestions come from earlier entries. Clubs also see the places of their federation.
- Home page: under **Quick actions** › **Customise** you hide single actions or the whole block; turn them back on under My account › Profile › Home. Only people with a treasurer role see the treasurer's actions, not a chair who simply has all rights. Key figures, upcoming events and notices sit in an even grid.
- Home page: every role sees the same order: **To do**, **Quick actions** for your role (such as **Record a booking** for the treasurer), up to three key figures, **Upcoming events** and a **Notices** card for the legal notice, letter template, anniversaries and birthdays. Members accept or decline the next event right at the top. **To do** now also lists bookings without a receipt, a missing bank statement for the previous month and missing required setup items.
- Settings: the new **Setup (overview)** lists all setup pages of the modules with the status of each item, a search and the progress of the required items.
- New: dark mode for members. Sign-in, the start page, events, My account and My area appear dark when your device is set to dark or you choose “Dark” under My account › Profile › **Appearance**. Administration stays light.
- App: the “Card” tab is now called “Me”. Your card is at the top, below it, as in My area, “My data”, “Invoices” with your direct debit mandate, “Meetings” with agenda, motions and minutes, “My results”, “Volunteer duties” and “Documents”. Long-press the app icon to open “Show ID card”.
- Usability: links stand out clearly from the text even with dark club colours; HUBIplan picks a suitable shade of the club colour for them. In headings and bold text, a link has a “›” after it. Body text is slightly darker.
- Usability: the month calendar shows each event’s category as a name, on phones as a short code, with a legend above the month; events have a background in their category colour. Cards stand out more clearly from the page background. All lists page with the same “‹ Previous” and “Next ›” buttons. The role list groups areas by level (“Edit: Cash book, Dues”). On a phone, connect the app under My account › App & other instances with the button **Open in the HUBIplan app**, no QR code needed.
- Chronicle: you now maintain the introduction and timeline only under Club › Chronicle. Under Website › Texts the chronicle only shows its address, menu setting and delete, plus a link to the chronicle.
- Events: in the unit's calendar, the board can choose **Event for** to create events for sub-units that do not manage themselves. Only their members are invited then.
- Page header: pages share one header at the top. From the second level on, the path is shown above the heading (e.g. “Settings › Roles and permissions”), on phones a “‹ back” link to the page above. The unit's name only appears if you manage more than one unit. Settings come first.
- Finance: every group in the sidebar has its own page. Clicking the group name shows all entries with a short description, what is to do there, and the main action, e.g. **Record income** for Book. ▸ expands the group without changing the page.
- Menu: Finance is organised in expandable groups: **Fees & invoices**, **Payments**, **Book**, **Evaluate & close** and **Set up**. Only the group of the current page is open, ▸ opens another one. The former tabs under Invoices and Setup (e.g. Reminders, Accounts) are entries of their own, batch payouts are under Payments. The addresses stay the same.
- Usability: required fields show “(required)” after the label. If something is missing, the message stays below the field instead of in a browser bubble, and the cursor moves to the first such field. The sidebar only lists items you can open with your rights; the treasurer, for example, only sees “Settings” with the right to do so. Third-level pages have their own heading and their own title in the browser tab, e.g. “Additional fields · Setup · Members”. With light club colours the sidebar stays easy to read, and terms with “?” wrap on phones.
- New guides: invite to a meeting, record attendance, approve minutes, plan volunteer shifts, create a task, plan a performance, add sheet music, add to the chronicle, website texts, pictures and structure, book a room or equipment, prepare the financial audit, check an incoming invoice and, for members, card, invoices, volunteer shifts and motions.
- Help reorganised: the help has four tabs, **Guides**, **Pages**, **Terms** (the former glossary) and **News**, and follows the order of the sidebar. First steps come in packages per task (e.g. treasury or managing members), and members without management rights see their own short start page. The help for a page lists the terms used on it.
- New: cancellation invoice. Cancelling creates a separate document with a number (e.g. R2026-0012-S), date and reason that can be sent by email. Paid invoices can be cancelled too; the credit is refunded or offset against an open invoice. **Cancel and create again** opens a copy as a draft right away.
- Member file: new tab “Fees” with invoices, outstanding amounts, SEPA mandates and the billing address.
- Work area of a sub-unit: the sub-items are now in the sidebar instead of tabs above the content.
- My area: entries and results are together under “Competitions”. Musicians have their own item “Music” with performances, rehearsals and availability.
- Menu: Finance follows the course of the year. “Book” now also contains fixed assets, allowances and batch payouts, “Evaluate” contains evaluation (with net assets), development and taxes (with artists’ social security levy and use of funds), “Close the year” contains donation receipts and year-end closing (with cash audit). Instruments and uniforms are under Equipment & rooms, the personal music reminders under Tasks. Old addresses redirect.
- Menu: the new sidebar group “Planning” brings together events, meetings, volunteer duties and tasks. In an event, the block “For this event” shows the meeting, duty rosters, bookings, tasks and performance; meeting and duty roster link back to the event.
- Home page: “To do” now collects from all areas: due meeting invitations, received motions and open minutes, open helper places, queries and close entry deadlines, received invoices, invoice drafts, reminders, direct debits and expired account invitations. Finance › Overview shows the same entries for finance.
- Terms: changes members make to their own details are now called “data changes”; “entries” only means competition entries. In a sub-unit’s work area the tab is called “Offices” and shows the office holders first.
- New: VAT in the cash book. Tax rate per booking (default from the category), input VAT also proportionally and under Finance › Taxes › VAT a template for the advance return, optionally with flat-rate input VAT under § 23a UStG.
- New: allowances now also count waivers as expense donations, payments from the musician settlement and the cash books of sub-units (Finance › Allowances).
- New: statement on the use of funds. Finance › Taxes › Use of funds shows in a simplified calculation which funds must be used by when, and warns if a deadline has passed.
- New: earmarked donations. In donation receipts › **Earmarks**, donations and cash book expenses can be assigned to a purpose; the overview shows the remainder and reminds you to use the funds promptly.
- New: donations up to 300 €. Finance › Setup › Donations offers the simplified donation proof as a PDF and as text for the website; together with the bank statement it is enough for the tax office.
- New: category templates. Under Finance › Setup › Categories, **Use a template** creates the usual categories of a shooting, sports or music club with their tax areas, without changing existing ones.
- New: maximum for the free reserve. Finance › Reserves works out per financial year how much may be added to the free reserve, including amounts carried over from the two previous years, and warns if it is exceeded. Depreciation is shown as a guide for the replacement reserve.
- New: statement of net assets. Finance › Evaluation › Net assets shows account balances, open receivables, fixed assets and open liabilities at the year end, plus the funds set aside in reserves. The overview is also part of the treasurer's report.
- New: artists' social security levy. Expenses for bands, DJs or graphic designers can be marked as artist fees in the cash book or via the category. Finance › Taxes › Artists' social security levy shows the total per recipient, the levy and the reporting deadline, also as Excel.
- New: cash audit. Under Finance › Year-end closing › Cash audit the treasury creates an audit assignment per financial year. The auditors tick off samples with their documents and the account balances at year end, rate checkpoints, record findings and recommend discharge. The audit report is produced as a PDF.
- New: fixed asset register. Finance › Fixed assets lists tents, trailers and other club property with depreciation and book value. Depreciation only counts in the tax report; the cash book stays unchanged.
- New: allowances for volunteers. In the cash book an expense can be marked as trainer allowance or volunteer allowance. Finance › Allowances shows the totals per person and calendar year, warns above the tax-free amount and offers a declaration to sign as PDF for each person.
- New: split bookings. In the cash book, **Split** divides a booking across several categories or tax areas, for example the caterer's settlement. Each part becomes a booking of its own; receipts and the bank transaction stay with the first part.
- New: tax report. Under Finance › Taxes a traffic light compares income from commercial operations with the tax-free limit (€50,000 since 2026), with results per tax area (cancellations offset), a note on losses, the small-business limit and when the next tax return is due; as PDF and Excel. Sub-units with their own cash book count towards the club unless marked "separate for tax purposes". The finance overview points out when something needs attention there.
- New: Meetings. General meetings, board meetings and meetings of units can be prepared with an event and an agenda. For each type the club sets the notice period; HUBIplan shows the date by which invitations must go out. The invitation is created as a circular with the agenda to the members entitled to vote. Afterwards the club records attendance and quorum and writes minutes with resolutions and elections, which the chair and the minute taker approve. The resolution book lists all resolutions and elections with search, filters and implementation status; a resolution can be turned into a task, and an election can be entered as a term of office with “Take into office”. In the member area, members entitled to vote see invitations with the agenda and approved minutes and submit motions before the deadline, which the leadership adds to the agenda or declines with a reason. The platform switches the module on.
- New: Volunteer duties. Create duty rosters with duties and shifts for festivals and events (quick entry such as “14-18, 18-22”), assign members and external helpers, with notes on overlaps, full shifts and minimum age. Notice as PDF and list as Excel. The platform switches the module on. Members sign up for free shifts in the member area, cancel before the deadline, see “My duties” also as a calendar file and get a reminder email. Shift places can be given to units as a quota, which their leadership fills with its own members. Rosters can be copied for next year, free places announced by circular and completed duties evaluated per person.
- Accounts: Settings › Accounts lists all accounts of the unit with roles, member area, last sign-in and two-factor. Filters show, for example, who has never signed in or whose invitation has expired; selected accounts get the sign-in link again. The terms of office per account moved here from Roles and permissions.
- Assigning offices: under Members › Offices every office has an “Assign” button, as does the member record (“Assign role”). The dialog says beforehand what will happen and can end the predecessor’s term at the same time. With the “Unit” filter this also works for sub-unit offices; their page and workspace assign with the same dialog.
- Music: the sub-items are now in the sidebar instead of a separate bar above the content, grouped as performances, musicians, sheet music, equipment and payments, and setup. Each item has its own address; old bookmarks redirect.
- Home: “To do” now also lists open booking requests, held contact form messages and your own circular drafts untouched for a week. My area shows the same items in the sidebar as on its overview, such as bookings and results when they are enabled.
- Menu merged: equipment, facilities and bookings are under one item, “Equipment & rooms” (facilities are called “Rooms and facilities” there). Letter templates are under Circulars › Letter, social media channels under Website › Social media. New names: Competitions › Titles and winners, Members › Changes and transfers.
- Setup: Events have their own “Setup” with categories, external calendars, sharing and the website link. The donation receipt details are now under Finance › Setup › Donations. Roles, accounts and offices use the same terms everywhere (account, term of office, leadership, permissions); in the member file, offices and the account are on the “Roles and account” tab.
- Menu: “My area” is now in the “Everyday” group. Settings and Website have subheadings in the sidebar (Settings: General, Legal, Roles and connections, Data; Website: Content, Layout, Operation). The document bin is a link at the top of the library. Organisation shows links to the setup of all areas. The home page shows “To do” to treasurers too, and the app uses the same words as the browser (To do, Cash book, Invoices).
- Usability: On a phone the header is a single row; undo and the unit selection are at the top of the menu (☰), “view as role” is under “My account”, and the full club name stays readable. Heading and browser tab name the subpage, e.g. “Organisation · Settings”. Links in text are bold instead of underlined. Units are counted per level, e.g. “7 corps · 15 platoons”.
- Usability: Members read circulars under “My area › Messages”, as in the app. The event list has a heading per month and shows the category. Checkboxes are larger, sign-in reports empty fields itself, and in English dates appear as 31/10/2026 everywhere.
- Menu: The sidebar is grouped into Everyday, Club, Communication and Administration. Finances orders its items by the work steps of the overview. Settings of an area are called “Setup” everywhere; shooting facilities are under Competitions › Setup. “Settings › Organisation” is split into Organisation, Names and terms and Club documents.
- Usability: Member admission guides keyboard focus through two clearly named stages. Calendar responses confirm saving for screen readers; submission methods and validation hints appear in English when English is selected.

- Members: Past unit memberships (such as platoons) can be added with start and end in one step. This also works for units that are no longer active. If they overlap, the message names the affected membership with its exact dates.
- Events: If an external calendar cannot be fetched for 14 days, its events are hidden until a fetch succeeds again. Everyone who manages events receives an email about it.
- Events: You can hide an event’s end time on the website when only the start time is known. The setting is available when creating or editing an event; the entered end time is kept internally.
- Events: External calendars show their events in your own calendar, e.g. the federation’s from Vereinsplaner or from a calendar subscription (iCal). HUBIplan fetches them every hour; individual ones can be hidden (Events › Setup › External calendars).
- Website: For each text you can choose whether the caption appears below the image and in the image carousel. The default is without a caption, for existing pages too.
- Website: New “Klassisch” (classic) design with a light header, a coloured line on top and menu buttons in the club colour. To keep the previous Klassisch, save it as a package first and import it as an own design.
- Website: A design can be saved as a package and imported again by platform administrators as an own design, with custom CSS and colours on maintained built-in layouts (Website › Design › Share design).
- Website: The chronicle can be kept as a timeline, with events (year or period), chapters as subheadings and Excel import; if you like, the newest entries come first. An event can have up to six pictures, on the left, on the right, alternating or large below the text.
- Website: Copied images can be pasted with Ctrl+V, in the image library and in the image picker.
- Help: Every page has "Help on this page" at the top (or the ? key), plus step-by-step guides with pictures. Under "For you" you find first steps that match your rights, to tick off.
- Who helps us?: A unit can name contacts for questions about HUBIplan and add its own notes to guides.
- English: The interface is also available in English. You can switch under My account › Profile.
- View as role: Whoever manages roles can look at HUBIplan the way a role sees it (read only).
- Nextcloud: The club's Nextcloud can use sign-in with the HUBIplan account. When access in HUBIplan ends, HUBIplan can also disable the Nextcloud account itself. Several roles can share one Nextcloud group (e.g. “1st” and “2nd treasurer” in “Treasurer”).
